Big numbers cannot be read without separators. You will make all three
lines readable.

The data you have:

```python
population = 84680000
budget = 1234567.891
```

**What to do:**

1. Print the population with thousands separators.
2. Print the budget with separators **and** two decimal places.
3. Print the budget once more with no decimals, still with separators (it
   will be rounded).

**Expected output:**

```
Population: 84,680,000
Budget: 1,234,567.89
Rounded: 1,234,568
```

> The separator is `,` and the digits are `.2f`. Together they are written
> as `,.2f`.
