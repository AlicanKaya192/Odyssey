Print exactly these three lines:

```
He said "Hello"
It's 5 o'clock
'Python' is "fun"
```

It sounds easy, but every line has quote marks in it, and Python decides
where a piece of text ends by looking at the quotes.

- If you start the text with double quotes (`"..."`), it ends at the next
  double quote. If the text contains double quotes, put single quotes
  (`'...'`) around it; if it contains a single quote (an apostrophe), put
  double quotes around it.
- The third line has **both kinds** of quotes. You cannot write it as one
  piece of text. Hint: you can give `print` several pieces separated by
  commas, and **one space** is placed between them automatically.

> Watch out: the lines must match letter for letter, including where the
> quotes are, the spaces and capital letters.
