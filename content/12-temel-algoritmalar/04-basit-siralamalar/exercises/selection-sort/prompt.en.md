Write the function `selection_sort(items)`: it returns a sorted copy of the
list by **finding the smallest** of the remaining part every round and
putting it at that part's front.

Do not use `sorted`, `.sort()`, `min()` or `.index()`: find the place of the
smallest yourself with an inner loop.

**Expected output:**

```
[10, 13, 14, 29, 37]
[1, 2]
```
