`is_leap(year)` in `leap.py` returns whether a year is a leap year. The rules:

- a year divisible by 4 is a leap year (2024),
- but a year divisible by 100 is not (1900),
- but a year divisible by 400 is a leap year after all (2000).

Write a test function **for each rule** in `test_leap.py` (at least three). Each broken version forgets one of the rules; think about which year gives away which forgotten rule.
