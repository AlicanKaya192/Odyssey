This time `calculator.py` has `cube(n)`: a number's cube (`n * n * n`). Split the tests into **three separate functions**: `test_positive` (2 and 3), `test_negative` (-2 and -3) and `test_zero`. That way, even if one test fails, the other two are tried separately.

A negative number's cube is negative: `cube(-2)` is -8. Odyssey expects at least three tests and also tries broken versions of `cube`.
