Let $R$ be the $90°$ anticlockwise rotation and $S$ the reflection in the $x$-axis:

$$
R = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & -1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{bmatrix}
$$

$$
S = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & -1 \end{bmatrix}
$$

Both are applied to $\mathbf{v} = (1, 3)$ in two different orders:

1. **Order 1:** rotate first, then reflect.
2. **Order 2:** reflect first, then rotate.

Find both results.
