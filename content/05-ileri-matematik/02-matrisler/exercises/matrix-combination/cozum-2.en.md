Because addition and scaling work entry by entry, each entry can be computed on its own:

$$
c_{ij} = 3a_{ij} - 2b_{ij}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
c_{11} &= 3 \cdot 2 - 2 \cdot 1 = 4 \\
c_{12} &= 3 \cdot (-1) - 2 \cdot 4 = -11 \\
c_{21} &= 3 \cdot 0 - 2 \cdot (-2) = 4 \\
c_{22} &= 3 \cdot 3 - 2 \cdot 1 = 7
\end{aligned}
$$

If a question about a large matrix asks for only one entry, this route is much faster: there is no need to compute the whole matrix.

**Answer: $4$, $-11$, $4$, $7$**
