$A\mathbf{x}$ is the sum of the columns of $A$ weighted by the components of $\mathbf{x}$:

$$
A\mathbf{x} = 4 \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 3 \end{bmatrix} + 1 \begin{bmatrix} -2 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix} - 1 \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ 2 \end{bmatrix}
$$

$$
= \begin{bmatrix} 4 - 2 - 0 \\ 12 + 1 - 2 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 2 \\ 11 \end{bmatrix}
$$

The same numbers, added in a different order. The column view shows that the result is built from the columns of $A$.

**Answer: $(2, 11)$**
