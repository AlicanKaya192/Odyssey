**1. Two equations from the row view:**

$$
\begin{aligned}
2x_1 + x_2 &= 7 \\
x_1 - x_2 &= 2
\end{aligned}
$$

**2. Add them;** $x_2$ and $-x_2$ cancel:

$$
3x_1 = 9 \;\Rightarrow\; x_1 = 3
$$

**3. From the second equation:** $3 - x_2 = 2 \Rightarrow x_2 = 1$.

**Check:** $2 \cdot 3 + 1 = 7$ and $3 - 1 = 2$. ✓

**Answer: $x_1 = 3$, $x_2 = 1$**
