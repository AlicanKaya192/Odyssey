The (area, rooms, age) information of three houses is in a data matrix; the area is in hundreds of square metres:

$$
X = \begin{bmatrix} 1.2 & 3 & 10 \\ 0.8 & 2 & 25 \\ 1.5 & 4 & 5 \end{bmatrix}
$$

The weights and constant of a linear model:

$$
\mathbf{w} = \begin{bmatrix} 100 \\ 20 \\ -2 \end{bmatrix} \qquad b = 10
$$

The predictions are $\hat{\mathbf{y}} = X\mathbf{w} + b$ ($b$ is added to every prediction). What is the predicted price (in thousands) of each of the three houses?
