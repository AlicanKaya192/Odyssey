**What is asked?** $3\,\mathbf{u} - 2\,\mathbf{v}$ is a new vector. We need its $x$ and $y$ components, that is, two numbers.

**Idea:** The expression has two operations: scaling ($3\,\mathbf{u}$ and $2\,\mathbf{v}$) and subtraction. As with numbers, multiplication comes first, then subtraction. Both work component by component:

- Multiplying a vector by a number multiplies **every component** by that number.
- Subtracting two vectors subtracts the **matching components** ($x$ from $x$, $y$ from $y$).

**Step 1 — Multiply $\mathbf{u}$ by 3.** Both components of $\mathbf{u} = (2, -1)$ are multiplied by 3:

$$
3\,\mathbf{u} = \big(3 \cdot 2,\ 3 \cdot (-1)\big) = (6,\ -3)
$$

In a picture, this is an arrow pointing the same way as $\mathbf{u}$, three times as long.

**Step 2 — Multiply $\mathbf{v}$ by 2.** The same for $\mathbf{v} = (-3, 4)$:

$$
2\,\mathbf{v} = \big(2 \cdot (-3),\ 2 \cdot 4\big) = (-6,\ 8)
$$

**Step 3 — Subtract.** Now work out $(6, -3) - (-6, 8)$; $x$ with $x$, $y$ with $y$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
x &= 6 - (-6) = 6 + 6 = 12 \\
y &= -3 - 8 = -11
\end{aligned}
$$

**Watch out:** Subtracting a negative number means adding it. The most common slip is writing $6 - 6 = 0$ for the $x$ component.

**Check:** If the result is $3\,\mathbf{u} - 2\,\mathbf{v}$, adding $2\,\mathbf{v}$ back must give $3\,\mathbf{u}$:

$$
(12, -11) + (-6, 8) = (6, -3)
$$

That is indeed $3\,\mathbf{u}$. ✓

**Answer:** $x = 12$, $y = -11$, so $(12, -11)$.
