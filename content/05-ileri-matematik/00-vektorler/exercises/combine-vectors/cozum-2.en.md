A vector operation means doing the same operation separately in each component. Look at the $x$ components only:

$$
x = 3 \cdot 2 - 2 \cdot (-3) = 6 + 6 = 12
$$

Then at the $y$ components only:

$$
y = 3 \cdot (-1) - 2 \cdot 4 = -3 - 8 = -11
$$

When the dimension is very large (784, say) this view is more practical: every component is the same small calculation.

**Answer: $(12, -11)$**
