**1. Equate the components:**

$$
a + 3b = 7 \qquad 2a + b = 9
$$

**2. Express $b$ from the second equation:** $b = 9 - 2a$.

**3. Substitute into the first:**

$$
a + 3(9 - 2a) = 7 \;\Rightarrow\; a + 27 - 6a = 7 \;\Rightarrow\; -5a = -20 \;\Rightarrow\; a = 4
$$

**4.** $b = 9 - 2 \cdot 4 = 1$.

**Check:** $4\,(1, 2) + 1\,(3, 1) = (4, 8) + (3, 1) = (7, 9)$. ✓

**Answer: $a = 4$, $b = 1$**
