Two houses are described by the vectors (floor area, rooms, age):

$$
\mathbf{a} = (120,\ 3,\ 10) \qquad \mathbf{b} = (100,\ 4,\ 12)
$$

1. Find the Euclidean distance between the two houses.
2. Find the same distance again, writing the area in units of **a hundred square metres** ($1.20$ and $1.00$).

Round both answers to **two decimal places**.
