A measurement has standard deviation $\sigma = 20$. Critical values: $1.96$ for $95$ percent, $2.576$ for $99$ percent.

1. What is the $95$ percent margin of error with $100$ observations?
2. At least how many observations are needed for a margin of at most $2$ at $95$ percent confidence?
3. At least how many if the same margin is wanted at $99$ percent confidence?
