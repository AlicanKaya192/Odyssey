**Idea:** If the $95$ percent confidence interval does not contain $250$, the test rejects at the $5$ percent level.

**Step 1 — Standard error.** $1.25$.

**Step 2 — Interval and $z$.** $247.5 \pm 1.96 \cdot 1.25 = [245.05, \ 249.95]$. $250$ is just outside the upper bound; its distance is $\frac{2.5}{1.25} = 2$ standard errors, so $z = -2$.

**Step 3 — p and decision.** For $2$ standard errors the two tails are $0.0456$. The $99$ percent interval $247.5 \pm 3.22$ contains $250$: not rejected with $\alpha = 0.01$.

**Why the same result?** The interval and the test use the same threshold: $\mu_0$ lying more than $1.96$ standard errors from $\bar{x}$ is the same as $|z| > 1.96$.

**Answer:** $1.25$, $-2$ and $0.0456$.
