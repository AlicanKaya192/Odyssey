**What is asked?** Whether the mean differs significantly from the claimed value.

**Idea:** If $H_0$ holds, $z$ is standard normal; the p-value is the area of both tails.

**Step 1 — Standard error.** $\frac{10}{\sqrt{64}} = 1.25$.

**Step 2 — $z$.** $\frac{247.5 - 250}{1.25} = -2$.

**Step 3 — p-value.** $2 \cdot 0.0228 = 0.0456$. With $\alpha = 0.05$, $0.0456 < 0.05$: $H_0$ is rejected. With $\alpha = 0.01$ it is not.

**Check:** $|z| = 2$ is just above the critical value $1.96$; a p-value just under $0.05$ is consistent ✓.

**Watch out:** Taking one tail ($0.0228$) is wrong unless a one-sided $H_1$ was chosen before looking at the data.

**Answer:** $1.25$, $-2$, $\approx 0.0456$.
