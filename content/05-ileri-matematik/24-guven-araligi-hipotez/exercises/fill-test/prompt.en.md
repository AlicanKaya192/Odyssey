A package says $250$ g; the machine's standard deviation is $\sigma = 10$ g. The mean of $64$ random packages is $247.5$ g. $H_0: \mu = 250$, $H_1: \mu \neq 250$. Table: $P(Z \leq -2) \approx 0.0228$.

1. What is the standard error of the mean in grams?
2. What is the test statistic $z$?
3. What is the two-sided p-value? (Also think about the decision for $\alpha = 0.05$ and $\alpha = 0.01$.)
