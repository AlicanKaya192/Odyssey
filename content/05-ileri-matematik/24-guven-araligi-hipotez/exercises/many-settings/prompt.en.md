A team compares $20$ different settings of a model with the old one, running a separate test for each ($\alpha = 0.05$). In fact none of the settings has a real effect, and the tests are independent.

1. What is the expected number of "significant" (false alarm) results?
2. What is the probability of at least one false alarm? (To three decimal places.)
3. With the Bonferroni correction, what should the threshold of each test be?
