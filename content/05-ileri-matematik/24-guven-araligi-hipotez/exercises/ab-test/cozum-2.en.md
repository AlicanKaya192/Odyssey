**Idea:** $H_0$ says the two proportions are equal; so we can estimate one shared proportion and use it for both.

**Step 1 — Pooled proportion and SE.** $\frac{200 + 250}{10{,}000} = 0.045$. $\text{SE} = \sqrt{0.045 \cdot 0.955 \cdot \left(\frac{1}{5000} + \frac{1}{5000}\right)} = \sqrt{0.00001719} \approx 0.00415$.

**Step 2 — $z$.** $\frac{0.01}{0.00415} \approx 2.41$.

**Step 3 — p.** Again $\approx 0.016$.

**Why the same result?** The two groups are the same size and the proportions are close; the variance from the pooled proportion comes out almost the same as the average of the separate variances. With very different proportions the two methods would drift apart a little.

**Answer:** $0.00414$, $2.41$ and $0.016$.
