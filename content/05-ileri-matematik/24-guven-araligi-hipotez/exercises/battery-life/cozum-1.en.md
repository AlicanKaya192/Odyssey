**What is asked?** A $95$ percent confidence interval for the mean.

**Idea:** $\bar{x} \pm 1.96 \cdot \frac{s}{\sqrt{n}}$; since $n$ is large, $z^{*}$ is enough.

**Step 1 — Standard error.** $\frac{5}{\sqrt{100}} = 0.5$.

**Step 2 — Margin of error.** $1.96 \cdot 0.5 = 0.98$.

**Step 3 — Bounds.** $42 - 0.98 = 41.02$ and $42 + 0.98 = 42.98$.

**Check:** The interval is symmetric around $\bar{x}$ and $1.96$ wide, about four standard errors ✓.

**Watch out:** Writing $42 \pm 1.96 \cdot 5$ gives an interval for the life of a single battery; for the mean, use the standard error.

**Answer:** $0.5$, $41.02$, $42.98$.
