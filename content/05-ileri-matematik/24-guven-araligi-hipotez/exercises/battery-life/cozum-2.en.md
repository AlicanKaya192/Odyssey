**Idea:** A confidence interval is the set of all $\mu_0$ values that a two-sided test at the $5$ percent level does not reject.

**Step 1 — Standard error.** $0.5$.

**Step 2 — The no-reject condition.** $|z| = \frac{|42 - \mu_0|}{0.5} < 1.96$, that is $|42 - \mu_0| < 0.98$.

**Step 3 — Bounds.** $41.02 < \mu_0 < 42.98$.

**Why the same result?** Asking "does the data fit this $\mu_0$" for every $\mu_0$ is the interval formula written the other way round; both rest on the threshold of $1.96$ standard errors.

**Answer:** $0.5$, $41.02$ and $42.98$.
