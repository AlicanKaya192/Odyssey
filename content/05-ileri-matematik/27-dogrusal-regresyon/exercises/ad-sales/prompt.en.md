A shop's advertising spend $x$ (thousands) and sales $y$ (hundreds of units) over five weeks:

| week | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $x$ | $1$ | $2$ | $3$ | $4$ | $5$ |
| $y$ | $3$ | $5$ | $4$ | $7$ | $6$ |

$\hat{y} = b + wx$ is fitted by least squares.

1. What is the slope $w$?
2. What is the intercept $b$?
3. What is the prediction at $x = 6$?
