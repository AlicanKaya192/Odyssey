In a single-feature model without intercept ($\hat{y} = wx$), $\sum x_i^2 = 10$ and $\sum x_i y_i = 20$. The ridge loss is $\sum (y_i - wx_i)^2 + \lambda w^2$.

1. What is the unpenalised least squares solution $w$?
2. What is the ridge solution with $\lambda = 10$?
3. What $\lambda$ makes the ridge solution $1.6$?
