A server fails on average once every $200$ hours; failures are independent and arrive at a constant rate, so the time to the next failure is exponential with $\lambda = \frac{1}{200}$.

1. What is the mean wait until the next failure, in hours?
2. What is the probability of no failure in the next $100$ hours? (To four decimal places.)
3. What is the median wait, the $t$ with $P(X > t) = 0.5$, in hours? (To two decimal places.)
