**What is asked?** A lower tail, an interval and the cut-off of an upper tail in a normal distribution.

**Idea:** $Z = \frac{X - \mu}{\sigma}$ turns every normal question into the standard normal.

**Step 1 — $182$.** $z = \frac{12}{8} = 1.5$: $0.933$.

**Step 2 — The interval.** $z = -1$ to $1$: $0.841 - (1 - 0.841) = 0.841 - 0.159 = 0.682$.

**Step 3 — The top $2.5$ percent.** $P(Z \leq z) = 0.975$, $z = 1.96$. $x = 170 + 1.96 \cdot 8 = 185.68$ cm.

**Check:** The second answer is the $68$ percent of the 68–95–99.7 rule ✓.

**Watch out:** Writing $P(Z \leq z) = 0.025$ for the upper tail gives the lower one ($z = -1.96$, $154.32$ cm).

**Answer:** $0.933$, $0.682$, $185.68$ cm.
