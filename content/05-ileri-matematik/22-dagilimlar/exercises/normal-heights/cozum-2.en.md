**Idea:** Read each question as "how many standard deviations from the mean".

**Step 1 — $182$.** $182 - 170 = 12 = 1.5 \cdot 8$: $1.5\sigma$ above the mean; from the table $0.933$.

**Step 2 — $162$ to $178$.** $170 \pm 8$: exactly $\mu \pm \sigma$. The rule: about $68$ percent; from the table $0.682$.

**Step 3 — The top $2.5$ percent.** Symmetry: $2.5$ percent in each tail, $95$ percent in the middle, that is $\mu \pm 1.96\sigma$. The upper cut-off is $170 + 15.68$.

**Why the same result?** $z$ already means "how many standard deviations"; the table is the same for every normal because standardising removes the scale and the shift.

**Answer:** $0.933$, $0.682$ and $185.68$.
