A classifier gives "positive" probabilities $p_1 = 0.8$, $p_2 = 0.6$, $p_3 = 0.9$ for three examples. The model treats the labels as independent Bernoulli variables. The true labels are $(1, 0, 1)$.

1. According to the model, what is the probability (likelihood) of these three labels together?
2. According to the model, what is the expected number of positives among the three examples?
3. What is the variance of the number of positives?
