A worked example for each method in the lesson. Try the question yourself first, then read the solution.

## 1. Partial derivatives

**Question:** For $f(x, y) = 3x^2 y - y^3 + 2x$, what are $f_x$ and $f_y$?

$f_x = 6xy + 2$, $f_y = 3x^2 - 3y^2$.

## 2. The gradient at a point

**Question:** For $f(x, y) = x^2 + xy$, what is $\nabla f(1, 3)$?

$\nabla f = (2x + y, \ x)$; at $(1, 3)$ it is $(5, 1)$.

## 3. An exponential function

**Question:** For $f(x, y) = e^{2x + y}$, what are $f_x$ and $f_y$?

The chain rule: $f_x = 2e^{2x + y}$, $f_y = e^{2x + y}$.

## 4. A directional derivative

**Question:** At a point where $\nabla f = (3, 4)$, what is the rate of change in the direction $\mathbf{u} = (1, 0)$?

$\nabla f \cdot \mathbf{u} = 3$.

## 5. The fastest increase

**Question:** At the same point, what is the rate of the fastest increase?

$\lVert (3, 4) \rVert = 5$.

## 6. Perpendicular to a level curve

**Question:** Which direction is perpendicular to the level curve of $f = x^2 + y^2$ through $(0, 2)$?

$\nabla f(0, 2) = (0, 4)$: the vertical direction. The curve is the circle of radius $2$; its tangent there is horizontal.

## 7. A stationary point

**Question:** Where is the gradient of $f = x^2 + y^2 - 2x$ zero?

$(2x - 2, \ 2y) = (0, 0)$: at $(1, 0)$.

## 8. A linear model

**Question:** For $\hat{y} = 2$, $y = 5$, $\mathbf{x} = (1, 3)$, what is $\nabla_{\mathbf{w}} L$?

$2(2 - 5)(1, 3) = (-6, -18)$.
