**What is asked?** The tangent to a level curve at a point.

**Idea:** The gradient is perpendicular to the level curve; the tangent is a line perpendicular to the gradient.

**Step 1 — The gradient.** $\nabla f = (2x, 2y)$; at $(3, 4)$ it is $(6, 8)$. This direction has slope $\frac{8}{6} = \frac{4}{3}$.

**Step 2 — The tangent's slope.** The perpendicular slope: $m = -\frac{3}{4}$.

**Step 3 — The line.** $y - 4 = -\frac{3}{4}(x - 3)$, $y = -\frac{3}{4}x + \frac{9}{4} + 4 = -\frac{3}{4}x + \frac{25}{4}$.

**Check:** The curve is the circle of radius $5$; the tangent is at distance $\frac{\lvert 25/4 \rvert}{\sqrt{1 + 9/16}} = \frac{25/4}{5/4} = 5$ from the centre ✓.

**Watch out:** Taking the gradient's slope ($\frac{4}{3}$) as the tangent's slope; the gradient is perpendicular to the curve, not along it.

**Answer:** $m = -\frac{3}{4}$, $b = \frac{25}{4}$.
