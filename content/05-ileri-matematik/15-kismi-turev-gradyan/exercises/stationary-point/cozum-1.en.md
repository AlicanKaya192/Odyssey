**What is asked?** The stationary point of a function of two variables and the value there.

**Idea:** Set each partial derivative to zero; two equations, two unknowns.

**Step 1 — The equations.** $2x - 4 = 0$, $2y + 6 = 0$: $x = 2$, $y = -3$.

**Step 2 — The value.** $f(2, -3) = 4 + 9 - 8 - 18 + 5 = -8$.

**Step 3 — The kind.** $f_{xx} = 2 > 0$, $f_{yy} = 2 > 0$, $f_{xy} = 0$: a bowl in every direction, a minimum.

**Check:** A nearby point: $f(2.1, -3) = -7.99 > -8$ ✓.

**Watch out:** $\nabla f = \mathbf{0}$ alone does not mean a minimum; for $x^2 - y^2$ the gradient is zero too, but there is a saddle. Here both second derivatives are positive, so it is a minimum.

**Answer:** $(2, -3)$, smallest value $-8$.
