**What is asked?** The two partial derivatives of a function of two variables at a point.

**Idea:** When differentiating with respect to one variable, treat the other as a number. After finding the formula, put in the point.

**Step 1 — $f_x$.** $y$ fixed: $f_x = 3x^2 y^2 - 4y$. At $(1, 2)$: $3 \cdot 4 - 8 = 4$.

**Step 2 — $f_y$.** $x$ fixed: $f_y = 2x^3 y - 4x + 3y^2$. At $(1, 2)$: $4 - 4 + 12 = 12$.

**Check:** The mixed derivatives must be equal: $(f_x)_y = 6x^2 y - 4$, $(f_y)_x = 6x^2 y - 4$ ✓.

**Watch out:** The derivative of $y^3$ with respect to $x$ is $0$, not $3y^2$; in every term, remember which variable you are differentiating with respect to.

**Answer:** $4$ and $12$.
