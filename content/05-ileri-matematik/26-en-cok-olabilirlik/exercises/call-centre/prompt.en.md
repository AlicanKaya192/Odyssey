The numbers of calls reaching a call centre in six separate hours: $3, 5, 2, 4, 6, 4$. The hourly number of calls is assumed to be Poisson$(\lambda)$.

1. What is the maximum likelihood estimate of $\lambda$?
2. With this estimate, what is the probability of no calls in an hour? (To four decimal places.)
3. What is the derivative of the log-likelihood at $\lambda = 5$?
