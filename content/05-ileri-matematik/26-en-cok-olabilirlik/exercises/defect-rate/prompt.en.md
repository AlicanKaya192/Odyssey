$200$ random parts from a line are inspected; $8$ are defective. Each part is assumed to be defective independently with probability $p$. The log-likelihood is $\ell(p) = 8\ln p + 192\ln(1 - p)$.

1. What is the maximum likelihood estimate of $p$?
2. What is $\ell(\hat{p})$? (To two decimal places.)
3. What is $\ell'(0.05)$? (To two decimal places.) What does its sign say?
