The lifetimes of five batteries (years): $1.5$, $4$, $2.5$, $3$, $1$. Lifetime is assumed to be exponential: $f(t) = \lambda e^{-\lambda t}$.

1. What is the MLE of $\lambda$? (To three decimal places.)
2. With this estimate, what is the probability that a battery lasts more than $3$ years, $P(T > 3) = e^{-3\lambda}$?
3. What is the median lifetime (the $m$ with $P(T > m) = 0.5$) in years? (To two decimal places.)
