A Naive Bayes spam filter is being trained. In the training data none of $40$ spam emails contain the word "invoice"; $12$ of $60$ normal emails do.

1. What is the MLE of the probability that "invoice" appears in spam?
2. With the Laplace correction ($\frac{k + 1}{n + 2}$), what is this probability? (To four decimal places.)
3. With the Laplace correction, what is the probability that it appears in a normal email? (To three decimal places.)
