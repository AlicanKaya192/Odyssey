In a city every day is either sunny (S) or rainy (R). The day after a sunny day is sunny with probability $0.9$, the day after a rainy day with probability $0.5$. The transition matrix (column = today, row = tomorrow):

$$
M = \begin{bmatrix} 0.9 & 0.5 \\ 0.1 & 0.5 \end{bmatrix}
$$

1. In the long run, what share of days is sunny? You may write a fraction or a decimal.
2. What is the eigenvalue of $M$ other than $1$?
