A disease affects $5$ per thousand ($0.005$) of the population. A screening test is positive for $98$ percent of the ill and wrongly positive for $3$ percent of the healthy.

1. What is the probability $P(+)$ that a random person tests positive?
2. What is the probability that someone who tests positive is ill? (To three decimal places.)
3. If the same person is also positive on an independent second test, what does the probability of being ill become? (To three decimal places.)
