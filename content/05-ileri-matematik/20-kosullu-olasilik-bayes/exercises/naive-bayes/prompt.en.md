In a Naive Bayes filter $P(\text{spam}) = 0.4$. The first word appears in $50$ percent of spam and $10$ percent of non-spam; the second in $20$ percent of spam and $25$ percent of the rest. The words are independent given the class.

1. For an e-mail containing both words, what is the spam score $P(\text{spam}) \, P(w_1 \mid \text{spam}) \, P(w_2 \mid \text{spam})$?
2. What is the probability that this e-mail is spam?
3. What is the probability that an e-mail containing the second word but **not** the first is spam?
