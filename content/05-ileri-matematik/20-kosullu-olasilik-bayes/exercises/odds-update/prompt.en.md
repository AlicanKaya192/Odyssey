$20$ percent of e-mails are spam. A word has likelihood ratio $\frac{P(\text{word} \mid \text{spam})}{P(\text{word} \mid \text{not spam})} = 8$; a second, independent word has $3$.

1. What are the prior odds $\frac{P(\text{spam})}{P(\text{not spam})}$ of spam?
2. What does $P(\text{spam})$ become when the first word is seen?
3. What does $P(\text{spam})$ become when both words are seen?
