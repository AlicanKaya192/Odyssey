$2$ per thousand of $100{,}000$ transactions are fraud. A model catches (raises an alarm for) $95$ percent of the fraud and wrongly raises an alarm for $1$ percent of normal transactions.

1. How many alarms are raised in total?
2. How many of them are real fraud?
3. What is the model's precision $P(\text{fraud} \mid \text{alarm})$? (To three decimal places.)
