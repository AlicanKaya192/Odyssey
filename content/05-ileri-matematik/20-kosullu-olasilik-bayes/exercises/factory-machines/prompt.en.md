In a factory, machine A makes $50$ percent of the products, B $30$ percent and C $20$ percent. Their defect rates are $2$, $3$ and $5$ percent.

1. What is the probability that a random product is defective?
2. A defective product is found. What is the probability that it came from machine C? (To three decimal places.)
3. What is the probability that it came from machine B? (To three decimal places.)
