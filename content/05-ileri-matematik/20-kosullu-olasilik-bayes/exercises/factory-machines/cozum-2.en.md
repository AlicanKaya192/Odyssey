**Idea:** Think of $10{,}000$ products; turn the rates into counts.

**Step 1 — The counts.** A $5000$ products, $100$ defective. B $3000$ products, $90$ defective. C $2000$ products, $100$ defective. Total $290$ defective: $\frac{290}{10{,}000} = 0.029$.

**Step 2 — C.** $100$ of the defective come from C: $\frac{100}{290} \approx 0.345$.

**Step 3 — B.** $\frac{90}{290} \approx 0.310$.

**Why the same result?** Each count is the formula's product multiplied by $10{,}000$; in the division this factor cancels. Thinking in counts applies the rule without memorising it.

**Answer:** $0.029$, $0.345$ and $0.310$.
