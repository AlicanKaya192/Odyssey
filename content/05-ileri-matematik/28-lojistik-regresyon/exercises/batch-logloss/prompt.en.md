A model's predictions on three examples ($p$: probability of "$y = 1$"):

| example | $y$ | $p$ |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | $1$ | $0.9$ |
| 2 | $0$ | $0.2$ |
| 3 | $1$ | $0.4$ |

1. What is the log-loss of the third example?
2. What is the average log-loss over the three examples?
3. If the model had given $p = 0.01$ for the third example, what would its loss be?
