A purchase model on an e-commerce site: $\ln\frac{p}{1 - p} = -2 + 0.7x$, where $x$ is the number of visits this month. Table: $e^{0.7} \approx 2.0138$, $e^{-2} \approx 0.1353$.

1. By what factor does each extra visit multiply the odds of buying? (To three decimal places.)
2. What is $p$ for someone with no visits ($x = 0$)?
3. At least how many visits (a whole number) give $p \geq 0.5$?
