A worked example for each method in the lesson. Try the question yourself first, then read the solution.

## 1. Average rate of change

**Question:** For $f(x) = x^2 + 1$, what is the average rate of change on $[1, 4]$?

$\dfrac{17 - 2}{4 - 1} = 5$.

## 2. Differentiating from the definition

**Question:** For $f(x) = 3x^2$, use the definition to find $f'(x)$.

$\dfrac{3(x + h)^2 - 3x^2}{h} = \dfrac{6xh + 3h^2}{h} = 6x + 3h \to 6x$.

## 3. The derivative at a point

**Question:** If $f(x) = \dfrac{1}{x}$, what is $f'(2)$?

$f'(x) = -\dfrac{1}{x^2}$: $f'(2) = -\dfrac{1}{4}$.

## 4. The tangent line

**Question:** What is the tangent to $y = x^2$ at $x = -1$?

$f(-1) = 1$, $f'(-1) = -2$: $y - 1 = -2(x + 1)$, $y = -2x - 1$.

## 5. Term by term

**Question:** If $f(x) = 4x^3 - 2x + 9$, what is $f'(x)$?

$12x^2 - 2$.

## 6. Increasing or decreasing?

**Question:** Is $f(x) = x^2 - 6x$ increasing or decreasing at $x = 1$?

$f'(x) = 2x - 6$, $f'(1) = -4 < 0$: decreasing.

## 7. A numerical derivative

**Question:** For $f(x) = x^2$ at $x = 1$ with $h = 0.01$, what is the forward difference?

$\dfrac{1.0201 - 1}{0.01} = 2.01$; the true derivative is $2$.

## 8. A learning step

**Question:** $L(w) = (w - 1)^2$, $w = 4$, $\eta = 0.25$. What is $w$ after one step?

$L'(4) = 6$; $4 - 0.25 \cdot 6 = 2.5$.
