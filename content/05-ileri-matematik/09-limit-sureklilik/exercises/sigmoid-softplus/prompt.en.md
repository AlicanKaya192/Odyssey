Two activations in neural networks: the sigmoid $\sigma(x) = \dfrac{1}{1 + e^{-x}}$ and softplus $s(x) = \ln(1 + e^x)$ (a smooth version of ReLU).

1. What is $\lim_{x \to \infty} \sigma(x)$?
2. What is $\lim_{x \to -\infty} \sigma(x)$?
3. What is $\lim_{x \to \infty} \big(s(x) - x\big)$? (It measures how close softplus gets to ReLU for large inputs.)
