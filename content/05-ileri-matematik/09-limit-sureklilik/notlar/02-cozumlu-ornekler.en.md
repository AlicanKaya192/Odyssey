A worked example for each method in the lesson. Try the question yourself first, then read the solution.

## 1. Substituting

**Question:** What is $\lim_{x \to -1} (2x^3 - x + 4)$?

A polynomial is continuous everywhere: $2(-1) + 1 + 4 = 3$.

## 2. Factoring

**Question:** What is $\lim_{x \to 2} \dfrac{x^2 + x - 6}{x - 2}$?

$\frac{0}{0}$. $x^2 + x - 6 = (x - 2)(x + 3)$; after cancelling, $x + 3 \to 5$.

## 3. The conjugate

**Question:** What is $\lim_{x \to 9} \dfrac{\sqrt{x} - 3}{x - 9}$?

Multiplying by the conjugate gives $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{x} + 3} \to \dfrac{1}{6}$.

## 4. One-sided limits

**Question:** For $f(x) = \dfrac{\lvert x \rvert}{x}$, what are the left and right limits as $x \to 0$?

On the left $x < 0$: $\frac{-x}{x} = -1$. On the right $\frac{x}{x} = 1$. They differ, so there is no limit.

## 5. A limit at infinity

**Question:** What is $\lim_{x \to \infty} \dfrac{6x^3 - x}{2x^3 + 7x^2}$?

Equal degrees: $\frac{6}{2} = 3$.

## 6. An infinite limit

**Question:** What happens to $\dfrac{1}{x - 2}$ as $x \to 2^+$? As $x \to 2^-$?

From the right the bottom is small and positive: $+\infty$. From the left it is small and negative: $-\infty$.

## 7. Continuity

**Question:** $f(x) = 2x + k$ ($x < 1$), $f(x) = x^2 + 4$ ($x \ge 1$). What must $k$ be for $f$ to be continuous?

The left limit is $2 + k$, the right limit and the value are $5$: $k = 3$.

## 8. Intermediate values

**Question:** Does $x^3 + x - 3 = 0$ have a root between $1$ and $2$?

$f(1) = -1 < 0$, $f(2) = 7 > 0$ and $f$ is continuous: yes, it does.
