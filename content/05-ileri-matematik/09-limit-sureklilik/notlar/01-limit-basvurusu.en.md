## Definitions

| Concept | Meaning |
|---|---|
| $\lim_{x \to a} f(x) = L$ | as $x$ approaches $a$, $f(x)$ approaches $L$ |
| $\lim_{x \to a^-}$, $\lim_{x \to a^+}$ | limit from the left, from the right |
| the limit exists | left limit = right limit |
| continuous | $f(a)$ defined, the limit exists, $\lim_{x \to a} f(x) = f(a)$ |

## Order of work

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| substituting gives a number | that number is the answer |
| $\dfrac{0}{0}$ with a polynomial | factor and cancel |
| $\dfrac{0}{0}$ with a root | multiply by the conjugate |
| $\dfrac{c}{0}$, $c \neq 0$ | an infinite limit or none: check the signs |
| $x \to \infty$, a fraction | divide by the highest power |

## Fractions at infinity

| Degrees | Limit |
|---|---|
| top = bottom | ratio of the leading coefficients |
| top < bottom | $0$ |
| top > bottom | unbounded ($\pm\infty$) |

## Known limits

| Limit | Value |
|---|---|
| $\lim_{x \to \infty} \dfrac{1}{x^k}$, $k > 0$ | $0$ |
| $\lim_{n \to \infty} \left(1 + \dfrac{1}{n}\right)^n$ | $e \approx 2.718$ |
| $\lim_{x \to \infty} e^{-x}$ | $0$ |
| $\lim_{x \to \infty} \sigma(x)$, $\lim_{x \to -\infty} \sigma(x)$ | $1$, $0$ |

## Kinds of discontinuity

| Kind | Sign |
|---|---|
| hole | the limit exists, $f(a)$ missing or different |
| jump | left and right limits differ |
| infinite | a vertical asymptote |

## Practical tips

- $\frac{0}{0}$ is not an answer; look for the common factor.
- For a piecewise function, work out both sides separately at the boundary.
- If a parameter is wanted for continuity: left limit = right limit = value.
