A worked example for each method in the lesson. Try the question yourself first, then read the solution.

## 1. The power rule

**Question:** If $f(x) = 6\sqrt{x} - \dfrac{4}{x}$, what is $f'(x)$?

$6x^{1/2} - 4x^{-1}$: $f'(x) = 3x^{-1/2} + 4x^{-2} = \dfrac{3}{\sqrt{x}} + \dfrac{4}{x^2}$.

## 2. The product rule

**Question:** What is $(x^3 \ln x)'$?

$3x^2 \ln x + x^3 \cdot \dfrac{1}{x} = 3x^2 \ln x + x^2$.

## 3. The quotient rule

**Question:** What is $\left(\dfrac{x^2}{x - 1}\right)'$?

$\dfrac{2x(x - 1) - x^2}{(x - 1)^2} = \dfrac{x^2 - 2x}{(x - 1)^2}$.

## 4. The chain rule

**Question:** What is $\big((x^2 + 1)^3\big)'$?

$3(x^2 + 1)^2 \cdot 2x = 6x(x^2 + 1)^2$.

## 5. An exponential chain

**Question:** What is $(e^{5x - 2})'$?

$5 e^{5x - 2}$.

## 6. A logarithm

**Question:** What is $\big(\ln(3x^2 + 1)\big)'$?

The derivative of the inside over the inside: $\dfrac{6x}{3x^2 + 1}$.

## 7. Two rules at once

**Question:** What is $(x e^{-x})'$?

Product, with a chain in the second factor: $e^{-x} + x \cdot (-e^{-x}) = (1 - x)e^{-x}$.

## 8. The sigmoid

**Question:** If $\sigma(z) = 0.8$, what is $\sigma'(z)$?

$0.8 \cdot 0.2 = 0.16$.
