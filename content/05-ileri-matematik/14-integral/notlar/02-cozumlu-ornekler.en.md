A worked example for each method in the lesson. Try the question yourself first, then read the solution.

## 1. An antiderivative

**Question:** What is $\int (6x^2 - 4x + 3) \, dx$?

$2x^3 - 2x^2 + 3x + C$. Check: its derivative is $6x^2 - 4x + 3$ ✓.

## 2. A definite integral

**Question:** What is $\int_0^2 (x^3 + 1) \, dx$?

$\left[\frac{x^4}{4} + x\right]_0^2 = 4 + 2 = 6$.

## 3. An integral that leads to ln

**Question:** What is $\int_1^e \dfrac{1}{x} \, dx$?

$\ln e - \ln 1 = 1$.

## 4. An exponential

**Question:** What is $\int_0^1 e^{2x} \, dx$?

$\left[\frac{e^{2x}}{2}\right]_0^1 = \frac{e^2 - 1}{2} \approx 3.19$.

## 5. Signed area

**Question:** What is $\int_{-1}^{1} x^3 \, dx$?

$\left[\frac{x^4}{4}\right]_{-1}^{1} = \frac{1}{4} - \frac{1}{4} = 0$: the left and right pieces cancel.

## 6. Substitution

**Question:** What is $\int 3x^2 (x^3 + 2)^4 \, dx$?

$u = x^3 + 2$, $du = 3x^2 \, dx$: $\int u^4 \, du = \frac{(x^3 + 2)^5}{5} + C$.

## 7. The trapezoid rule

**Question:** Find $\int_0^2 x^2 \, dx$ with the trapezoid rule and $n = 2$ pieces.

$h = 1$: $\frac{1}{2}(0 + 2 \cdot 1 + 4) = 3$ (the true value is $\frac{8}{3}$).

## 8. Probability

**Question:** For a variable spread uniformly over $[0, 4]$, the density is $\frac{1}{4}$. What is $P(1 \le X \le 2)$?

$\int_1^2 \frac{1}{4} \, dx = \frac{1}{4}$.
