**Idea:** Put both into the pattern $(1 + x)^n \approx 1 + nx$. A root is a power too: $n = \frac{1}{2}$.

**Step 1 — Rearrange the root.** $\sqrt{4.1} = \sqrt{4 \cdot 1.025} = 2\sqrt{1.025} = 2(1 + 0.025)^{1/2}$.

**Step 2 — The shortcut.** $(1 + 0.025)^{1/2} \approx 1 + \frac{0.025}{2} = 1.0125$; twice that is $2.025$.

**Step 3 — The power.** $1.02^{10} = (1 + 0.02)^{10} \approx 1 + 10 \cdot 0.02 = 1.2$.

**Why the same result?** The tangent of $2(1 + u)^{1/2}$ at $u = 0$ and the tangent of $\sqrt{x}$ at $x = 4$ are the same line; the change $x = 4(1 + u)$ only rescales the variable. The benefit of the shortcut: it can be done in your head.

**Answer:** $2.025$ and $1.2$.
