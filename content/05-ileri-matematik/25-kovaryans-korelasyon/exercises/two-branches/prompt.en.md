The daily sales of two branches of a chain are $X$ and $Y$ (thousands). $\sigma_X = 5$, $\sigma_Y = 6$ and their correlation is $r = 0.4$ (they are in the same area and are affected together by weather and holidays).

1. What is $\operatorname{Cov}(X, Y)$?
2. What is the variance of total sales, $\operatorname{Var}(X + Y)$?
3. What is the variance of the difference between the branches, $\operatorname{Var}(X - Y)$?
