**What is asked?** The variance of the sum and the difference of two dependent variables.

**Idea:** Go from correlation to covariance; add the covariance term to the variance formula.

**Step 1 — Covariance.** $0.4 \cdot 5 \cdot 6 = 12$.

**Step 2 — Sum.** $25 + 36 + 2 \cdot 12 = 85$.

**Step 3 — Difference.** $25 + 36 - 2 \cdot 12 = 37$.

**Check:** If they were independent both would be $61$; positive covariance makes the sum larger and the difference smaller ✓.

**Watch out:** Subtracting $\operatorname{Var}Y$ in the variance of the difference ($25 - 36$) gives a negative variance; variances always add, only the sign of the covariance term changes.

**Answer:** $12$, $85$, $37$.
