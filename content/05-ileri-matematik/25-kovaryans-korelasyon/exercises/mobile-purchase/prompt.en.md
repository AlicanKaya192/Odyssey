For a site's visitors, $X = 1$ means "came from mobile" and $Y = 1$ "made a purchase" ($0$ otherwise). The joint probabilities:

| | $Y = 0$ | $Y = 1$ |
|---|---|---|
| $X = 0$ | $0.4$ | $0.1$ |
| $X = 1$ | $0.2$ | $0.3$ |

1. What is $E[XY]$?
2. What is $\operatorname{Cov}(X, Y)$?
3. What is the correlation? (To three decimal places.)
