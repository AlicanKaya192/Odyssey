Five observations with two features, with the column means subtracted (centred):

$$
X = \begin{pmatrix} -2 & -1 \\ -1 & -2 \\ 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 1 \\ 2 & 2 \end{pmatrix}
$$

The covariance matrix is computed as $\Sigma = \frac{1}{n - 1}X^\mathsf{T}X$.

1. What is $\Sigma_{11} = \operatorname{Var}(X_1)$?
2. What is $\Sigma_{12} = \operatorname{Cov}(X_1, X_2)$?
3. What is the correlation of the two features?
