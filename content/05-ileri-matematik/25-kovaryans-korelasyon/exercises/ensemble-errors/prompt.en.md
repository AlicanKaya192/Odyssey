The prediction errors of two regression models both have variance $\sigma^2 = 9$. Their predictions are averaged; the error of the average is $\frac{e_1 + e_2}{2}$.

1. If the errors have correlation $\rho = 0.6$, what is the error variance of the average?
2. What if the models are trained on different features and the correlation drops to $\rho = 0.2$?
3. At most what correlation gives an error variance of $6$? (To three decimal places.)
