The weekly study time (hours) and exam grade of five students:

| student | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| time $x$ | $2$ | $3$ | $5$ | $6$ | $9$ |
| grade $y$ | $50$ | $60$ | $65$ | $75$ | $90$ |

1. What is the sample covariance $s_{xy}$?
2. What is the correlation $r$? (To three decimal places.)
3. What would the covariance be if time were measured in minutes instead of hours?
