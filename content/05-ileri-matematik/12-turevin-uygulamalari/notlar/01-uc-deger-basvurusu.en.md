## Concepts

| Concept | Meaning |
|---|---|
| critical point | $f'(a) = 0$ or $f'(a)$ undefined |
| stationary point | $f'(a) = 0$ |
| local maximum / minimum | the largest / smallest of the nearby values |
| absolute maximum / minimum | the largest / smallest over the whole domain |
| inflection point | where $f''$ changes sign |

## Tests

| Test | Maximum | Minimum | No decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| first derivative | $f'$: $+ \to -$ | $f'$: $- \to +$ | no sign change |
| second derivative ($f'(a) = 0$) | $f''(a) < 0$ | $f''(a) > 0$ | $f''(a) = 0$ |

## Bending

| $f''$ | Shape | Tangent |
|---|---|---|
| $> 0$ | bends upward (a bowl) | below the curve |
| $< 0$ | bends downward (a cap) | above the curve |

## Absolute extremes on a closed interval

1. Find the critical points inside $(a, b)$.
2. Evaluate $f$ at the critical points, at $a$ and at $b$.
3. Pick the largest and the smallest.

## Optimisation steps

1. Write the objective function.
2. Use the constraint to get down to one variable.
3. Solve $f' = 0$.
4. Confirm with $f''$, the sign or the ends; do not forget the allowed range.

## Ready results

| Problem | Result |
|---|---|
| minimise $\sum (y_i - c)^2$ | $c$ = the mean |
| minimise $\sum \lvert y_i - c \rvert$ | $c$ = the median |
| minimise $\sum (y_i - w x_i)^2$ | $w = \dfrac{\sum x_i y_i}{\sum x_i^2}$ |
| rectangle with fixed perimeter, largest area | a square |
