A worked example for each method in the lesson. Try the question yourself first, then read the solution.

## 1. Critical points

**Question:** Where are the critical points of $f(x) = 2x^3 - 3x^2 - 12x$?

$f'(x) = 6x^2 - 6x - 12 = 6(x - 2)(x + 1)$: $x = -1$ and $x = 2$.

## 2. The first derivative test

**Question:** For the same function, what kind of point is $x = -1$?

At $x = -2$, $f' = 24 > 0$; at $x = 0$, $f' = -12 < 0$: $+ \to -$, a local maximum.

## 3. The second derivative test

**Question:** What kind of point is $x = 2$?

$f''(x) = 12x - 6$, $f''(2) = 18 > 0$: a local minimum.

## 4. An inflection point

**Question:** Where is the inflection point of $f(x) = 2x^3 - 3x^2 - 12x$?

$f''(x) = 12x - 6 = 0 \Rightarrow x = \frac{1}{2}$; the sign changes there.

## 5. A closed interval

**Question:** What are the largest and smallest values of $f(x) = x^2 - 4x$ on $[0, 5]$?

Critical point $x = 2$: $f(2) = -4$. Ends: $f(0) = 0$, $f(5) = 5$. Largest $5$, smallest $-4$.

## 6. A fixed perimeter

**Question:** What is the largest area of a rectangle with perimeter $40$?

Sides $x$ and $20 - x$: $A = 20x - x^2$, $A' = 20 - 2x = 0$, $x = 10$. A square, area $100$.

## 7. The mean

**Question:** For $2, 5, 11$, which $c$ minimises $\sum (y_i - c)^2$?

The mean: $\frac{18}{3} = 6$.

## 8. Least squares

**Question:** For $(1, 3)$ and $(2, 5)$, what is the best $w$ in the model $\hat{y} = wx$?

$w = \frac{1 \cdot 3 + 2 \cdot 5}{1 + 4} = \frac{13}{5} = 2.6$.
