**What is asked?** The largest and smallest value of a function on a closed interval.

**Idea:** The candidates are the critical points inside the interval and the two ends. Evaluate at all of them and compare.

**Step 1 — The critical points.** $3x(x - 2) = 0$: $x = 0$ and $x = 2$, both inside $[-1, 4]$.

**Step 2 — The table.**

| $x$ | $-1$ | $0$ | $2$ | $4$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $f(x)$ | $-4$ | $0$ | $-4$ | $16$ |

**Step 3 — Choose.** The largest is $16$ ($x = 4$), the smallest $-4$ ($x = -1$ and $x = 2$).

**Check:** $f(x) = x^2(x - 3)$: for $x < 3$, $f \le 0$, so the largest value must be on the $x > 3$ side, at the right end ✓.

**Watch out:** Looking only at the critical points leads to thinking the maximum is $0$; the absolute maximum is at an end.

**Answer:** Largest $16$, smallest $-4$.
