**What is asked?** The dimensions that give the largest area when the length of fence is fixed.

**Idea:** Write the area as a function of one variable (the constraint: $60$ m of fence) and set its derivative to zero.

**Step 1 — The constraint.** Perpendicular sides $x$, parallel side $y$: $2x + y = 60$, $y = 60 - 2x$.

**Step 2 — The objective.** $A(x) = x(60 - 2x) = 60x - 2x^2$, $0 < x < 30$.

**Step 3 — The derivative.** $A'(x) = 60 - 4x = 0$: $x = 15$. $A''(x) = -4 < 0$: a maximum.

**Step 4 — The values.** $y = 30$, $A = 15 \cdot 30 = 450$.

**Check:** $x = 14$: $14 \cdot 32 = 448$. $x = 16$: $16 \cdot 28 = 448$. Both are below $450$ ✓.

**Watch out:** If all four sides needed fence the answer would be a square ($15 \times 15$); since the river side is free, the parallel side is twice the perpendicular one.

**Answer:** $15$ m, $30$ m, $450$ m².
