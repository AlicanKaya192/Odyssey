**Idea:** $A(x)$ is quadratic; its vertex can also be found by completing the square, without derivatives.

**Step 1 — Complete the square.**

$$
\begin{aligned}
A(x) &= -2(x^2 - 30x) \\
&= -2\big((x - 15)^2 - 225\big) \\
&= 450 - 2(x - 15)^2
\end{aligned}
$$

**Step 2 — Read it off.** $2(x - 15)^2 \ge 0$, and it is zero only at $x = 15$. The largest area is $450$, at $x = 15$.

**Step 3 — The parallel side.** $60 - 30 = 30$.

**Why the same result?** The vertex of the parabola is where the derivative is zero. Completing the square only works for quadratics; the derivative method works for every differentiable function (including cubics like the box problem).

**Answer:** $15$, $30$ and $450$.
