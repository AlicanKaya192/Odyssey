**What is asked?** The local extreme values of a cubic function.

**Idea:** Find the critical points and look at the sign of $f'$ on either side: $+ \to -$ a maximum, $- \to +$ a minimum.

**Step 1 — The critical points.** $f'(x) = 3(x^2 - 4x + 3) = 3(x - 1)(x - 3) = 0$: $x = 1$, $x = 3$.

**Step 2 — The signs.** $x = 0$: $f' = 9 > 0$. $x = 2$: $f' = -3 < 0$. $x = 4$: $f' = 9 > 0$.

**Step 3 — Classify.** At $x = 1$, $+ \to -$: a local maximum, $f(1) = 1 - 6 + 9 + 1 = 5$. At $x = 3$, $- \to +$: a local minimum, $f(3) = 27 - 54 + 27 + 1 = 1$.

**Check:** $f(0.9) = 4.969$ and $f(1.1) = 4.971$: both are below $5$ ✓.

**Watch out:** The local maximum value is $f(1) = 5$, not $x = 1$; "which $x$" and "the value" are separate questions.

**Answer:** $x = 1$, value $5$; the local minimum is at $x = 3$.
