**Idea:** Without building a sign table, look at the sign of $f''$ at the critical points: a bowl or a cap?

**Step 1 — The critical points.** As in the first solution, $x = 1$ and $x = 3$.

**Step 2 — The second derivative.** $f''(x) = 6x - 12$. $f''(1) = -6 < 0$: a cap, a local maximum. $f''(3) = 6 > 0$: a bowl, a local minimum.

**Step 3 — The value.** $f(1) = 5$.

**Why the same result?** $f'' < 0$ says $f'$ is decreasing; if $f'(1) = 0$ and it is decreasing, it must be positive on the left and negative on the right: exactly $+ \to -$. The second derivative test is a shortcut for the first.

**Answer:** $1$, $5$ and $3$.
