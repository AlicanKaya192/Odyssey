**Idea:** $(x + y)(x - y) = x^2 - y^2$. Reduce the graph to a single formula.

**Step 1 — Simplify.** $g = x^2 - y^2$.

**Step 2 — Differentiate.** $\frac{\partial g}{\partial x} = 2x = 6$, $\frac{\partial g}{\partial y} = -2y = -2$.

**Why the same result?** The derivative $2x$ of $x^2$ is the sum of the branching's two contributions; the numbers $2 + 4 = 6$ from the $s$ and $d$ roads are, written out, the contributions $x - y$ and $x + y$. Backpropagation reaches the same number without ever simplifying.

**Answer:** $6$ and $-2$.
