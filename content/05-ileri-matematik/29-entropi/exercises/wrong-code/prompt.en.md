A sensor sends four states; the true probabilities are $P = \left(\frac{1}{2}, \frac{1}{4}, \frac{1}{8}, \frac{1}{8}\right)$. An engineer thought the states were equally likely ($Q$ uniform) and gave each a $2$-bit code.

1. What is $H(P)$ in bits?
2. What is the cross-entropy $H(P, Q)$ in bits?
3. What is $D_{\mathrm{KL}}(P \parallel Q)$ in bits?
