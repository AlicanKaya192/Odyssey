**Idea:** Turn accuracy into numbers correct: A $880$, B $900$. Each count has standard deviation $\sqrt{np(1 - p)}$.

**Step 1 — A.** $\sqrt{1000 \cdot 0.88 \cdot 0.12} = \sqrt{105.6} \approx 10.28$ correct; as a proportion $0.0103$.

**Step 2 — The difference.** For B, $\sqrt{90} \approx 9.49$. The difference has standard deviation $\sqrt{105.6 + 90} \approx 13.99$ correct; as a proportion $0.0140$.

**Step 3 — $z$.** A difference of $20$ correct is $\frac{20}{13.99} \approx 1.43$ standard deviations.

**Why the same result?** Counts and proportions always differ by a factor of $1000$; $z$ is a ratio, so the factor cancels.

**Answer:** $0.0103$, $0.0140$ and $1.43$.
