**What is asked?** How the standard error depends on the sample size.

**Idea:** $\text{SE} = \frac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}}$.

**Step 1 — $n = 36$.** $\frac{24}{6} = 4$.

**Step 2 — SE $= 2$.** $\sqrt{n} = 12$, $n = 144$.

**Step 3 — $n = 576$.** $\frac{24}{24} = 1$.

**Check:** $36 \to 144 \to 576$: four times the data at each step, standard error $4 \to 2 \to 1$ ✓.

**Watch out:** Doubling the sample does not halve the error; it takes four times.

**Answer:** $4$, $144$, $1$.
