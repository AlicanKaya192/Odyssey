**Idea:** Look at the total of the $36$ orders instead of the mean: its mean is $36 \cdot 30 = 1080$ and its standard deviation $9\sqrt{36} = 54$.

**Step 1 — Standard error.** The total's standard deviation is $54$; the mean is the total over $36$: $\frac{54}{36} = 1.5$.

**Step 2 — Exceeding $33$.** A mean of $33$ is a total of $1188$; $z = \frac{1188 - 1080}{54} = 2$: $0.023$.

**Step 3 — The interval.** A total between $1026$ and $1134$, $z = \pm 1$: $0.682$.

**Why the same result?** The mean is $\frac{1}{36}$ of the total; the deviation and the standard deviation scale by the same factor, so $z$ does not change.

**Answer:** $1.5$, $0.023$ and $0.682$.
