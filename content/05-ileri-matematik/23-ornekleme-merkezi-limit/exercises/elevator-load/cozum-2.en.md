**Idea:** Turn the limit into a per-person figure: $1300 / 16 = 81.25$ kg. The question is the probability that the mean weight exceeds $81.25$.

**Step 1 — Mean.** $75$ per person, $1200$ in total.

**Step 2 — Standard error.** $\frac{12}{\sqrt{16}} = 3$; turned back into a total, $3 \cdot 16 = 48$.

**Step 3 — Probability.** $z = \frac{81.25 - 75}{3} = 2.08$: again $\approx 0.019$.

**Why the same result?** The total $= 16 \cdot$ mean; dividing the threshold by the same factor leaves $z$ unchanged.

**Answer:** $1200$, $48$ and $0.019$.
