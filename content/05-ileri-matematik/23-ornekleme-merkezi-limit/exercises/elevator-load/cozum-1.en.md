**What is asked?** The mean and standard deviation of a sum of independent variables, and the probability of exceeding a threshold.

**Idea:** Expected values add, and (under independence) so do variances; the CLT makes the total approximately normal too.

**Step 1 — Mean.** $16 \cdot 75 = 1200$ kg.

**Step 2 — Standard deviation.** Variance $16 \cdot 144 = 2304$; standard deviation $48$ kg ($= 12\sqrt{16}$).

**Step 3 — Probability.** $z = \frac{100}{48} \approx 2.08$; $1 - 0.981 = 0.019$.

**Check:** The mean is $100$ kg from the limit, about two standard deviations; the probability is around $2$ percent ✓.

**Watch out:** Taking the total's standard deviation as $16 \cdot 12 = 192$; variances add, not standard deviations.

**Answer:** $1200$, $48$, $\approx 0.019$.
