A matrix has singular values:

$$
\sigma_1 = 12, \quad \sigma_2 = 5, \quad \sigma_3 = 3, \quad \sigma_4 = 1
$$

The best rank-2 approximation $A_2$ is made.

1. What share of the energy (the sum of the squared singular values) does $A_2$ keep? Write a ratio with two decimals (like $0.50$).
2. What is the error $\|A - A_2\|$ of the approximation? Round to two decimal places.
