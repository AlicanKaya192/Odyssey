A search engine describes texts by how many times three words appear: (*vector*, *matrix*, *probability*).

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathbf{q} &= (1,\ 1,\ 0) \\
D_1 &= (2,\ 2,\ 1) \\
D_2 &= (0,\ 6,\ 8)
\end{aligned}
$$

$\mathbf{q}$ is the search query, $D_1$ a short document and $D_2$ a long one.

1. Find the cosine similarity of $\mathbf{q}$ and $D_1$.
2. Find the cosine similarity of $\mathbf{q}$ and $D_2$.

Round both answers to **two decimal places**. Then look at the plain dot products too: which document comes out on top?
