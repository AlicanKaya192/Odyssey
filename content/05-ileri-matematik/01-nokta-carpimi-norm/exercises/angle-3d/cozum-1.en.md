**1. Dot product:**

$$
\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} = 1 \cdot 1 + 1 \cdot 0 + 0 \cdot 1 = 1
$$

**2. Lengths:**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\|\mathbf{a}\| &= \sqrt{1 + 1 + 0} = \sqrt{2} \\
\|\mathbf{b}\| &= \sqrt{1 + 0 + 1} = \sqrt{2}
\end{aligned}
$$

**3. Cosine:**

$$
\cos\theta = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}\cdot\sqrt{2}} = \frac{1}{2} \;\Rightarrow\; \theta = 60°
$$

**Answer: $60°$**
