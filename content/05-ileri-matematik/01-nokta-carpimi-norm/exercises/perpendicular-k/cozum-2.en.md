In the plane the direction perpendicular to $(p, q)$ is $(-q, p)$. For $\mathbf{b} = (4, -2)$ that is

$$
(2,\ 4)
$$

Since $\mathbf{a}$ is also perpendicular to $\mathbf{b}$, it lies **along the same line** as $(2, 4)$, so it is a multiple of it. The second component of $\mathbf{a}$ is $3$ and that of $(2, 4)$ is $4$; the multiple is $\dfrac{3}{4}$:

$$
\mathbf{a} = \frac{3}{4}\,(2,\ 4) = (1.5,\ 3)
$$

In the plane there is only one direction perpendicular to a vector, so this route reaches the answer without setting up an equation.

**Answer: $k = 1.5$**
