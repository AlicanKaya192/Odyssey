**1. The unit vector in the direction of $\mathbf{b}$:**

$$
\hat{\mathbf{b}} = \frac{(3,\ 4)}{5} = (0.6,\ 0.8)
$$

**2. The dot product with a unit vector gives the scalar projection directly:**

$$
\mathbf{a} \cdot \hat{\mathbf{b}} = 6 \cdot 0.6 + 2 \cdot 0.8 = 3.6 + 1.6 = 5.2
$$

**3. Scale the unit vector by this length:**

$$
5.2 \cdot (0.6,\ 0.8) = (3.12,\ 4.16)
$$

The two formulas are really the same thing: “take the direction, stretch it as long as the shadow”. With the unit vector at hand no division is needed at all.

**Answer: $5.2$ and $(3.12,\ 4.16)$**
