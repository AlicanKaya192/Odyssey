**1. The cosine of the angle:**

$$
\cos\theta = \frac{\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b}}{\|\mathbf{a}\|\,\|\mathbf{b}\|} = \frac{6}{15} = 0.4
$$

**2. The difference is the side joining the tips of the two vectors.** By the law of cosines:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\|\mathbf{a} - \mathbf{b}\|^2 &= 3^2 + 5^2 - 2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 \cdot 0.4 \\
&= 34 - 12 = 22
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\|\mathbf{a} - \mathbf{b}\| = \sqrt{22} \approx 4.69
$$

**3. The sum is the other diagonal of the parallelogram.** The angle there is $180° - \theta$ and $\cos(180° - \theta) = -\cos\theta$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\|\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b}\|^2 &= 34 + 2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 \cdot 0.4 = 46 \\
\|\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b}\| &= \sqrt{46} \approx 6.78
\end{aligned}
$$

The same numbers, because in the lesson's proof the geometric form of the dot product came straight from the law of cosines.

**Answer: $6.78$ and $4.69$**
