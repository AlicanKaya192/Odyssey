**1. Expand the squared length of the sum:**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\|\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b}\|^2 &= (\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b}) \cdot (\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b}) \\
&= \|\mathbf{a}\|^2 + 2\,\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} + \|\mathbf{b}\|^2 \\
&= 9 + 12 + 25 = 46
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\|\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b}\| = \sqrt{46} \approx 6.78
$$

**2. For the difference only the sign of the middle term changes:**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\|\mathbf{a} - \mathbf{b}\|^2 &= 9 - 12 + 25 = 22 \\
\|\mathbf{a} - \mathbf{b}\| &= \sqrt{22} \approx 4.69
\end{aligned}
$$

**Check (parallelogram law):** $\|\mathbf{a} + \mathbf{b}\|^2 + \|\mathbf{a} - \mathbf{b}\|^2 = 2\,(\|\mathbf{a}\|^2 + \|\mathbf{b}\|^2)$, that is $46 + 22 = 68 = 2 \cdot 34$. ✓

**Answer: $6.78$ and $4.69$**
