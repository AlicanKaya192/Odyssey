A disease affects $2$ percent of the population. The test is positive in $90$ percent of ill people and (wrongly) positive in $5$ percent of healthy people. The tests are independent.

1. What is the probability that a random person tests positive?
2. What is the probability that someone who tests positive is ill? (To three decimal places.)
3. If a second, independent test is also positive, what is the probability of being ill? (To three decimal places.)
