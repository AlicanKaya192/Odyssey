The covariance matrix of two-feature data:

$$
\Sigma = \begin{pmatrix} 4 & 2 \\ 2 & 1 \end{pmatrix}
$$

1. What is the variance $u^\mathsf{T}\Sigma u$ along $u = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(1, 1)$?
2. What is the largest eigenvalue $\lambda_1$?
3. What is the other eigenvalue $\lambda_2$? What does it say about the data?
