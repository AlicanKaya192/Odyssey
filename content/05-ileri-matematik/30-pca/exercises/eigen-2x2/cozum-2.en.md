**Idea:** In a symmetric matrix with equal diagonal entries the eigenvectors are the directions $(1, 1)$ and $(1, -1)$; the eigenvalues follow from direct multiplication.

**Step 1 — $(1, 1)$.** $\Sigma(1, 1) = (5 + 4, \ 4 + 5) = 9 \cdot (1, 1)$: $\lambda_1 = 9$.

**Step 2 — $(1, -1)$.** $\Sigma(1, -1) = (5 - 4, \ 4 - 5) = 1 \cdot (1, -1)$: $\lambda_2 = 1$.

**Step 3 — Share.** $\frac{9}{9 + 1} = 0.9$.

**Why the same result?** The roots of the characteristic equation are exactly the $\lambda$s satisfying $\Sigma u = \lambda u$; the guessed vectors satisfy it, so they give the same values. The direction $(1, 1)$: the two features rise and fall together.

**Answer:** $9$, $1$ and $0.9$.
