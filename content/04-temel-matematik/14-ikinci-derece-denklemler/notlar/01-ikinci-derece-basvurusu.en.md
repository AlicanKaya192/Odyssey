## Which route?

| Equation | Shortest route |
|---|---|
| $x^2 = k$ or $(x - p)^2 = k$ | square root, with $\pm$ |
| factors easily | factor, set each factor to zero |
| $c = 0$ ($ax^2 + bx = 0$) | take $x$ out: $x(ax + b) = 0$ |
| everything else | the quadratic formula |

## The quadratic formula

$$
x_{1,2} = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{\Delta}}{2a}, \qquad \Delta = b^2 - 4ac
$$

| $\Delta$ | Roots |
|---|---|
| $> 0$ | two different real roots |
| $= 0$ | one root, $-\dfrac{b}{2a}$ |
| $< 0$ | no real roots |

## Completing the square

$$
x^2 + bx = \left(x + \frac{b}{2}\right)^2 - \left(\frac{b}{2}\right)^2
$$

## Roots and coefficients

$$
x_1 + x_2 = -\frac{b}{a}, \qquad x_1 x_2 = \frac{c}{a}
$$

The equation with roots $r$ and $s$: $x^2 - (r + s)x + rs = 0$.

## The parabola

- $a > 0$: opens upwards, smallest value at the vertex.
- $a < 0$: opens downwards, largest value at the vertex.
- Vertex: $x = -\dfrac{b}{2a}$; halfway between the roots.

## Practical tips

- If the coefficients share a factor, divide first: $2x^2 - 10x + 12 = 0 \Rightarrow x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0$.
- If $a$ is negative, multiply both sides by $-1$.
- Check with the sum and product of the roots.
