A worked example for each method in the lesson. Try the question yourself first, then read the solution.

## 1. A square root

**Question:** Solve $(2x - 1)^2 = 25$.

$2x - 1 = \pm 5$: $x = 3$ or $x = -2$.

## 2. A common factor

**Question:** Solve $3x^2 = 12x$.

$3x^2 - 12x = 0$, $3x(x - 4) = 0$: $x = 0$ or $x = 4$.

## 3. Factoring

**Question:** Solve $x^2 + x - 12 = 0$.

$(x + 4)(x - 3) = 0$: $x = -4$ or $x = 3$.

## 4. Completing the square

**Question:** Solve $x^2 - 8x + 3 = 0$.

$$
x^2 - 8x + 16 = 13 \quad\Rightarrow\quad (x - 4)^2 = 13 \quad\Rightarrow\quad x = 4 \pm \sqrt{13}
$$

## 5. The quadratic formula

**Question:** Solve $3x^2 - 7x + 2 = 0$.

$\Delta = 49 - 24 = 25$; $x = \dfrac{7 \pm 5}{6}$: $x = 2$ or $x = \dfrac{1}{3}$.

## 6. The discriminant

**Question:** How many real roots does $2x^2 + 3x + 4 = 0$ have?

$\Delta = 9 - 32 = -23 < 0$: no real roots.

## 7. Roots and coefficients

**Question:** What are the sum and product of the roots of $x^2 - 7x + 10 = 0$?

Sum $7$, product $10$ (the roots are $2$ and $5$).

## 8. The vertex

**Question:** What is the smallest value of $y = x^2 - 6x + 5$?

Vertex at $x = \dfrac{6}{2} = 3$; $y = 9 - 18 + 5 = -4$.
