**Idea:** The quadratic formula works for every quadratic; even if we cannot see the factors, we reach the result.

**Step 1 — Coefficients.** $a = 1$, $b = -9$, $c = 14$.

**Step 2 — The discriminant.** $\Delta = 81 - 56 = 25$, $\sqrt{25} = 5$.

**Step 3 — The roots.**

$$
x = \frac{9 \pm 5}{2}: \quad x = 7 \text{ or } x = 2
$$

**Why the same result?** The formula is the general result of completing the square; it gives the same roots for equations that factor. When $\Delta$ is a perfect square ($25$) the roots are rational and the equation factors over whole numbers.

**Answer:** $7$ and $2$.
