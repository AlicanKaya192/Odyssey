**Idea:** Apply the formula, then simplify the root.

**Step 1 — The discriminant.** $a = 1$, $b = -4$, $c = -1$: $\Delta = 16 + 4 = 20$.

**Step 2 — The roots.**

$$
x = \frac{4 \pm \sqrt{20}}{2}
$$

**Step 3 — Simplify.** $\sqrt{20} = \sqrt{4 \cdot 5} = 2\sqrt{5}$:

$$
x = \frac{4 \pm 2\sqrt{5}}{2} = 2 \pm \sqrt{5}
$$

**Why the same result?** The quadratic formula is completing the square done once with general coefficients, so it reaches the same $2 \pm \sqrt{5}$. The simplifying from the Roots section came in handy here.

**Answer:** $2$, $5$ and $4.24$.
