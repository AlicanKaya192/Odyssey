1. The true values are $y = (2, 4, 6, 8)$ and the predictions $\hat{y} = (3, 4, 4, 9)$. What is $\text{MSE} = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} (y_i - \hat{y}_i)^2$?
2. An agent gets reward $2$ at every step and the future is discounted with $\gamma = 0.8$. What is $\sum_{t=0}^{\infty} \gamma^t \cdot 2$?
3. In a moving average the weights are $(1 - \beta), (1 - \beta)\beta, (1 - \beta)\beta^2, \dots$ with $\beta = 0.5$. What is the sum of the first three weights?
