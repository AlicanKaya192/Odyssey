A ball is dropped from a height of $81$ m. Each time it hits the ground it bounces back up to $\frac{2}{3}$ of the height it fell from.

1. How high does the ball rise on its fourth bounce?
2. When the ball touches the ground for the **fifth** time, what total vertical distance has it travelled?
3. If the ball is assumed to bounce forever, what is its total vertical distance?
