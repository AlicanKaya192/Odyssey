A worked example for each method in the lesson. Try the question yourself first, then read the solution.

## 1. A common factor

**Question:** Factor $15a^3 b - 10a^2 b^2$.

GCD$(15, 10) = 5$; the smallest exponent of $a$ is $2$, of $b$ is $1$. The
common factor is $5a^2 b$:

$$
15a^3 b - 10a^2 b^2 = 5a^2 b(3a - 2b)
$$

## 2. Grouping

**Question:** Factor $x^3 + 2x^2 + 3x + 6$.

$$
x^2(x + 2) + 3(x + 2) = (x^2 + 3)(x + 2)
$$

## 3. A difference of squares

**Question:** Factor $16x^2 - 1$.

$(4x)^2 - 1^2 = (4x - 1)(4x + 1)$.

## 4. A perfect square

**Question:** Factor $x^2 - 14x + 49$.

$49 = 7^2$ and $14x = 2 \cdot x \cdot 7$: $(x - 7)^2$.

## 5. A trinomial with positive c

**Question:** Factor $x^2 - 9x + 20$.

Product $20$, sum $-9$: both negative, $-4$ and $-5$. $(x - 4)(x - 5)$.

## 6. A trinomial with negative c

**Question:** Factor $x^2 + 3x - 28$.

Product $-28$, sum $3$: $7$ and $-4$. $(x + 7)(x - 4)$.

## 7. The ac method

**Question:** Factor $3x^2 - 10x + 8$.

$ac = 24$, sum $-10$: $-6$ and $-4$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
3x^2 - 6x - 4x + 8 &= 3x(x - 2) - 4(x - 2) \\
&= (3x - 4)(x - 2)
\end{aligned}
$$

## 8. Simplifying a fraction

**Question:** Simplify $\dfrac{x^2 - 4x}{x^2 - 16}$.

$$
\frac{x(x - 4)}{(x - 4)(x + 4)} = \frac{x}{x + 4} \qquad (x \neq 4,\ x \neq -4)
$$
