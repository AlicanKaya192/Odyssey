The short version of everything in the lesson. Come back here when you get stuck on a question.

## The order

1. Common factor (GCD and smallest exponent); take it all out.
2. Pattern: difference of squares, perfect square.
3. Trinomial: product–sum or the $ac$ method.
4. Four terms: grouping.
5. Check by expanding again.

## Patterns

| Expression | Factors |
|---|---|
| $ka + kb$ | $k(a + b)$ |
| $a^2 - b^2$ | $(a - b)(a + b)$ |
| $a^2 + 2ab + b^2$ | $(a + b)^2$ |
| $a^2 - 2ab + b^2$ | $(a - b)^2$ |
| $x^2 + (p + q)x + pq$ | $(x + p)(x + q)$ |
| $ax + ay + bx + by$ | $(a + b)(x + y)$ |
| $a^2 + b^2$ | does not factor with real numbers |

## Trinomials

**$x^2 + bx + c$:** $p$, $q$ with product $c$ and sum $b$.

| $c$ | $b$ | signs |
|---|---|---|
| $+$ | $+$ | both $+$ |
| $+$ | $-$ | both $-$ |
| $-$ | $\pm$ | opposite; the larger has the sign of $b$ |

**$ax^2 + bx + c$:** split the middle term with two numbers of product
$ac$ and sum $b$, then group.

## Fractions

Factor the top and bottom, cancel **common factors**. Leave out the values
that make the denominator zero.

## Practical tips

- Write the factor pairs of $c$ from small to large; look for the one with sum $b$.
- If it starts with a minus, take out $-1$ first: $-x^2 + 5x - 6 = -(x^2 - 5x + 6)$.
- $x^4 - 16 = (x^2 - 4)(x^2 + 4) = (x - 2)(x + 2)(x^2 + 4)$: apply the pattern again.
- The result must always expand back to the start.
