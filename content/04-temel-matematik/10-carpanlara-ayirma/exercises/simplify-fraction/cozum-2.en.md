**Idea:** The second question needs no simplifying: put $x = 7$ in directly. For the first, find the common factor from a **shared zero** of the top and bottom.

**Step 1 — The value directly.**

$$
\frac{7^2 - 4}{7^2 + 7 - 6} = \frac{45}{50} = \frac{9}{10}
$$

**Step 2 — A shared zero.** The top is zero at $x = 2$ and $x = -2$. The bottom at $x = 2$: $4 + 2 - 6 = 0$ ✓. So $(x - 2)$ is a factor of both.

**Step 3 — The remaining factors.** $x^2 - 4 = (x - 2)(x + 2)$; $x^2 + x - 6 = (x - 2)(x + 3)$ (the factor that, times $(x - 2)$, gives $x^2$ and $-6$ is $x + 3$).

$$
\frac{x + 2}{x + 3}: \quad p = 2, \; q = 3
$$

**Why the same result?** Cancelling happens exactly when the top and bottom share a zero: that zero produces a factor in both. The numerical value ($x = 7$) must agree on both routes, because simplifying does not change the fraction's value.

**Answer:** $2$, $3$ and $\dfrac{9}{10}$.
