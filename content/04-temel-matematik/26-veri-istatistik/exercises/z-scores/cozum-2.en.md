**Idea:** Think of the standard deviation as the unit of a ruler: in maths one unit is $8$ points, in physics $12$ points.

**Step 1 — Maths.** Ali is $12$ points above the mean; $12$ points in units of $8$ is $1.5$ units.

**Step 2 — Physics.** $21$ points above; in units of $12$, $1.75$ units.

**Step 3 — Convert back.** In physics $1.5$ units $= 18$ points; above the mean: $73$.

**Why the same result?** The division in the z formula converts the point difference into standard deviation units; the third step is the same conversion in reverse.

**Answer:** $1.5$, $1.75$ and $73$.
