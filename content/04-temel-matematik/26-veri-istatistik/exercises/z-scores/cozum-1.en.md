**What is asked?** Relative performance in two exams on different scales, and the score needed for a given z-score.

**Idea:** A z-score says how many standard deviations one is from the mean; it translates different exams into the same language.

**Step 1 — Maths.** $\frac{74 - 62}{8} = \frac{12}{8} = 1.5$.

**Step 2 — Physics.** $\frac{76 - 55}{12} = \frac{21}{12} = 1.75$. Relatively better in physics.

**Step 3 — The score needed.** $x = 55 + 1.5 \cdot 12 = 55 + 18 = 73$.

**Check:** $\frac{73 - 55}{12} = 1.5$ ✓.

**Watch out:** Looking at raw scores, physics ($76$) and maths ($74$) seem close; the difference comes from the means: the physics mean is much lower and Ali is $21$ points above it.

**Answer:** $1.5$, $1.75$, $73$.
