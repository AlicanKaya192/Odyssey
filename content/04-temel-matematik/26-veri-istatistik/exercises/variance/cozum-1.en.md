**What is asked?** The population variance, standard deviation and sample variance of a dataset.

**Idea:** Find the mean, add up the squared deviations, divide by the right number; the standard deviation is the square root.

**Step 1 — The mean.** $\frac{3 + 7 + 7 + 8 + 10}{5} = \frac{35}{5} = 7$.

**Step 2 — The squares.**

| $x_i$ | $3$ | $7$ | $7$ | $8$ | $10$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $x_i - 7$ | $-4$ | $0$ | $0$ | $1$ | $3$ |
| square | $16$ | $0$ | $0$ | $1$ | $9$ |

Total $26$.

**Step 3 — The results.** $\sigma^2 = \frac{26}{5} = 5.2$. $\sigma = \sqrt{5.2} \approx 2.280$. $s^2 = \frac{26}{4} = 6.5$.

**Check:** The deviations add up to $-4 + 0 + 0 + 1 + 3 = 0$ ✓.

**Watch out:** Do not take the standard deviation to be the mean of the deviations ($\frac{8}{5}$, ignoring sign); square first, then average, then take the root.

**Answer:** $5.2$, $2.280$, $6.5$.
