A worked example for each method in the lesson. Try the question yourself first, then read the solution.

## 1. Sharing in a ratio

**Question:** $1\,350$ is shared among three siblings in the ratio $2 : 3
: 4$. How much does each get?

The whole is $2 + 3 + 4 = 9$ parts; one part is $1\,350 \div 9 = 150$. The
shares are $300$, $450$, $600$. Check: $300 + 450 + 600 = 1\,350$ ✓.

## 2. Unit price

**Question:** A $750$ g pack costs $36$ and a $1.2$ kg pack costs $54$.
Which is cheaper?

Price per kilogram: $36 \div 0.75 = 48$ and $54 \div 1.2 = 45$. The large
pack is cheaper.

## 3. Direct proportion

**Question:** A car goes $52$ km on $4$ litres of fuel. How many litres
does it need for $130$ km?

$$
\frac{4}{52} = \frac{x}{130} \quad\Rightarrow\quad 52x = 520 \quad\Rightarrow\quad x = 10 \text{ litres}
$$

## 4. Inverse proportion

**Question:** At $60$ km per hour a journey takes $3$ hours. How long
does it take at $90$ km per hour?

The distance is fixed: $60 \cdot 3 = 180$ km. $90 \cdot t = 180$, $t = 2$
hours.

## 5. Finding a percentage

**Question:** $54$ of $240$ students wear glasses. What percentage wears
glasses?

$54 \div 240 = 0.225 = 22.5\%$.

## 6. Percentage change

**Question:** A product went from $250$ down to $215$. What was the
percentage discount?

$\dfrac{215 - 250}{250} = \dfrac{-35}{250} = -0.14$: a $14\%$ discount.

## 7. Successive change

**Question:** A share rises by $10\%$ one day and falls by $10\%$ the
next. What is the net change after two days?

$1.1 \cdot 0.9 = 0.99$: a $1\%$ fall.

## 8. Reverse percentage

**Question:** With $20\%$ sales tax included, a price is $180$. What is
the price before tax?

If the price before tax is $x$, then $1.2x = 180$, $x = 150$. (Taking
$20\%$ of $180$ off to get $144$ is wrong.)
