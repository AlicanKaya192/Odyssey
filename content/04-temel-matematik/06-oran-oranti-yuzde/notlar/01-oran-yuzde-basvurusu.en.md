## Ratio

- $a : b = \dfrac{a}{b}$; it simplifies like a fraction.
- If the ratio is $a : b$, the whole is $a + b$ parts; $a$'s share of the whole is $\dfrac{a}{a + b}$.
- Sharing an amount $M$ in the ratio $a : b$: one part is $\dfrac{M}{a + b}$.

## Proportion

$$
\frac{a}{b} = \frac{c}{d} \iff ad = bc
$$

| | Direct proportion | Inverse proportion |
|---|---|---|
| What is fixed | $\dfrac{y}{x} = k$ | $x \cdot y = k$ |
| Graph | a line through the origin | a curve (hyperbola) |
| Example | items – price, map – ground | workers – days, speed – time |

## Percentages

$$
p\% = \frac{p}{100}, \qquad \text{part} = \frac{p}{100} \cdot \text{whole}
$$

| Unknown | Formula |
|---|---|
| part | $\dfrac{p}{100} \cdot \text{whole}$ |
| percentage | $\dfrac{\text{part}}{\text{whole}} \cdot 100$ |
| whole | $\text{part} \div \dfrac{p}{100}$ |

## Percentage change

| Case | Factor |
|---|---|
| a $p\%$ rise | $1 + \dfrac{p}{100}$ |
| a $p\%$ fall | $1 - \dfrac{p}{100}$ |
| Successive changes | multiply the factors |

$$
\text{percentage change} = \frac{\text{new} - \text{old}}{\text{old}} \cdot 100
$$

Reverse percentage: new $=$ old $\cdot$ factor, so old $=$ new $\div$ factor.

## Practical tips

- In your head: find $10\%$ first, then multiply or divide.
- $p\%$ of $x$ $=$ $x\%$ of $p$: $25\%$ of $80$ $= 80\%$ of $25$ $= 20$.
- Percentage points are an absolute difference; percentage change is a relative one.
- Before setting up a proportion, decide whether the relationship is direct or inverse.
