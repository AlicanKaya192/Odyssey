**Idea:** First answer "how many km is $1$ cm on the map?" once. Then both questions become a single multiplication or division.

**Step 1 — The unit rate.** $1$ cm $\to 50\,000$ cm $= 500$ m $= 0.5$ km.

**Step 2 — From map to reality.** Each cm is $0.5$ km:

$$
7.4 \cdot 0.5 = 3.7 \text{ km}
$$

**Step 3 — From reality to the map.** Each $0.5$ km is $1$ cm on the map:

$$
12 \div 0.5 = 24 \text{ cm}
$$

**Why the same result?** The unit rate combines the scale and the unit conversion into one number ($0.5$ km per cm). The first method applied the same factors in two steps (multiply by $50\,000$, divide by $100\,000$); $50\,000 \div 100\,000 = 0.5$.

**Answer:** $3.7$ and $24$.
