**What is asked?** Converting both ways between map length and real length on a scale map.

**Idea:** A scale of $1 : 50\,000$ is a direct proportion: the real length is $50\,000$ times the map length. To avoid mixing units, work in cm first, then convert to km.

**Step 1 — From map to reality.**

$$
7.4 \cdot 50\,000 = 370\,000 \text{ cm}
$$

**Step 2 — Convert to km.** $1$ m $= 100$ cm and $1$ km $= 1\,000$ m, so $1$ km $= 100\,000$ cm:

$$
370\,000 \div 100\,000 = 3.7 \text{ km}
$$

**Step 3 — From reality to the map.** $12$ km $= 1\,200\,000$ cm. The map length is $50\,000$ times smaller:

$$
1\,200\,000 \div 50\,000 = 24 \text{ cm}
$$

**Check:** $24$ cm $\cdot\, 50\,000 = 1\,200\,000$ cm $= 12$ km ✓.

**Watch out:** It is easy to lose a zero when converting units. $1$ km $= 100\,000$ cm, not $10\,000$.

**Answer:** $3.7$ km and $24$ cm.
