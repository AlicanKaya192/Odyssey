A worked example for each method in the lesson. Try the question yourself first, then read the solution.

## 1. Place value

**Question:** In the number $58\,406\,219$, what are the place values of $4$ and $8$?

Count from the right: $9$ ones, $1$ tens, $2$ hundreds, $6$ thousands,
$0$ ten thousands, $4$ hundred thousands, $8$ millions, $5$ ten millions.

- The place value of $4$: $4 \cdot 100\,000 = 400\,000$.
- The place value of $8$: $8 \cdot 1\,000\,000 = 8\,000\,000$.

## 2. Multiplication and division left to right

**Question:** What is $36 \div 4 \cdot 3 - 2 \cdot (7 - 4)$?

$$
\begin{aligned}
&= 36 \div 4 \cdot 3 - 2 \cdot 3 &&\text{(bracket)} \\
&= 9 \cdot 3 - 6 &&\text{(left to right: division first)} \\
&= 27 - 6 = 21
\end{aligned}
$$

## 3. An expression with an exponent

**Question:** What is $5 + 2 \cdot 3^2 - 4$?

First the exponent ($3^2 = 9$), then the multiplication ($2 \cdot 9 =
18$), finally addition and subtraction left to right:

$$
5 + 18 - 4 = 19
$$

Doing $2 \cdot 3 = 6$ and then squaring ($6^2 = 36$) would be wrong: the
exponent comes before multiplication and belongs only to the $3$.

## 4. Nested brackets

**Question:** What is $3 \cdot [18 - (4 + 2) \cdot 2] \div 2$?

$$
\begin{aligned}
&= 3 \cdot [18 - 6 \cdot 2] \div 2 \\
&= 3 \cdot [18 - 12] \div 2 \\
&= 3 \cdot 6 \div 2 \\
&= 18 \div 2 = 9
\end{aligned}
$$

## 5. Mental arithmetic with the distributive property

**Question:** What are $102 \cdot 15$ and $48 \cdot 25$?

$$
\begin{aligned}
102 \cdot 15 &= 100 \cdot 15 + 2 \cdot 15 = 1\,500 + 30 = 1\,530 \\
48 \cdot 25 &= 12 \cdot 4 \cdot 25 = 12 \cdot 100 = 1\,200
\end{aligned}
$$

## 6. Division with remainder

**Question:** What are the quotient and remainder of $500 \div 23$?

$23 \cdot 20 = 460$; $40$ is left, one more $23$ fits: $23 \cdot 21 = 483$,
remainder $500 - 483 = 17$. $17 < 23$, so we stop.

Quotient $21$, remainder $17$. Check: $23 \cdot 21 + 17 = 483 + 17 = 500$ ✓.

## 7. Counting days

**Question:** Today is Friday. Which day of the week is it $45$ days from now?

$45 = 7 \cdot 6 + 3$: $6$ whole weeks (Friday again) and $3$ more days.
$3$ days after Friday: Saturday, Sunday, **Monday**.

## 8. Checking with an estimate

**Question:** Someone computed $412 \cdot 19$ and got $78\,280$. Is it right?

Rough estimate: $400 \cdot 20 = 8\,000$. $78\,280$ is about 10 times that:
a digit has slipped. The true result is $412 \cdot 19 = 412 \cdot 20 - 412 =
8\,240 - 412 = 7\,828$.
