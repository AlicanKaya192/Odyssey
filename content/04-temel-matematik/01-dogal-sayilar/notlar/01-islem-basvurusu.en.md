## Place value

Each place is 10 times the one to its right: ones, tens, hundreds,
thousands, ten thousands, hundred thousands, millions…

$$
4\,327 = 4 \cdot 1\,000 + 3 \cdot 100 + 2 \cdot 10 + 7
$$

Digit: $0$–$9$. Number: built from digits. Zero holds the place of an empty position.

## Names of the operations

| Operation | Name of the result |
|---|---|
| $a + b$ | sum |
| $a - b$ | difference |
| $a \cdot b$ | product |
| $a \div b$ | quotient (and remainder) |

## Properties

| Property | Addition | Multiplication | Subtraction / division |
|---|---|---|---|
| Commutative | ✓ | ✓ | ✗ |
| Associative | ✓ | ✓ | ✗ |
| Identity element | $0$ | $1$ | |
| Distributive | $a(b + c) = ab + ac$ and $a(b - c) = ab - ac$ | | |

$a \cdot 0 = 0$. $0 \div a = 0$. $a \div 0$ is **undefined**.

## Division with remainder

$$
a = b \cdot q + r, \qquad 0 \le r < b
$$

Check: $b \cdot q + r$ must give the dividend. The remainder must be smaller than the divisor.

## Order of operations

1. Brackets (inside out; the top and bottom of a fraction bar count as brackets)
2. Exponents and roots
3. Multiplication and division, **left to right**
4. Addition and subtraction, **left to right**

## Mental arithmetic shortcuts

| Calculation | Shortcut |
|---|---|
| $99 \cdot a$ | $100a - a$ |
| $25 \cdot a$ | $a \cdot 100 \div 4$ |
| $5 \cdot a$ | $a \cdot 10 \div 2$ |
| $ab + ac$ | $a(b + c)$ |
| $102 \cdot a$ | $100a + 2a$ |

## Practical tips

- Split a long expression into terms at the $+$ and $-$ outside brackets and work out each term separately.
- Write each intermediate result on a new line; both sides of every $=$ must really be equal.
- Compare the result with a rough estimate ($38 \cdot 51 \approx 40 \cdot 50$).
- For cycles such as days of the week or clock hours, use division with remainder.
