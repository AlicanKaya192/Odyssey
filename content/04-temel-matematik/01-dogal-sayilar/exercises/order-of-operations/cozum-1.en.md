**What is asked?** The value of an expression; the real challenge is doing the operations in the right order.

**Idea:** The order of operations: brackets → exponents → multiplication and division (left to right) → addition and subtraction (left to right). At each step do only the operations on that step and rewrite the expression.

**Step 1 — The bracket.** $2 + 6 = 8$:

$$
48 \div 8 \cdot 3 - 5 \cdot 2 + 7
$$

**Step 2 — Multiplication and division, left to right.** In the left group the division comes first because it is on the left: $48 \div 8 = 6$, then $6 \cdot 3 = 18$. The multiplication on the right: $5 \cdot 2 = 10$.

$$
18 - 10 + 7
$$

**Step 3 — Addition and subtraction, left to right.** $18 - 10 = 8$, then $8 + 7$:

$$
15
$$

**Watch out:** There are two traps. Doing $48 \div (8 \cdot 3) = 2$ (thinking multiplication comes before division), and doing $18 - (10 + 7) = 1$ (thinking addition comes before subtraction). In both, the order is left to right.

**Answer:** $15$.
