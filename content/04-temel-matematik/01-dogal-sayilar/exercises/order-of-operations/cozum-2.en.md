**Idea:** Split the expression into terms at the $+$ and $-$ signs outside the brackets. Multiplication and division always stay inside a term; you work out each term separately and then add. This route applies the order of operations automatically.

**Step 1 — Separate the terms.** Outside the brackets there is one $-$ and one $+$:

$$
\underbrace{48 \div (2 + 6) \cdot 3}_{\text{term 1}} \; - \; \underbrace{5 \cdot 2}_{\text{term 2}} \; + \; \underbrace{7}_{\text{term 3}}
$$

**Step 2 — Term 1.** The bracket, then left to right:

$$
\begin{aligned}
48 \div 8 \cdot 3 &= 6 \cdot 3 \\
&= 18
\end{aligned}
$$

**Step 3 — Terms 2 and 3.** $5 \cdot 2 = 10$ and $7$.

**Step 4 — Add.** Combine the terms with their signs:

$$
18 - 10 + 7 = 15
$$

**Why the same result?** The "multiplication and division first" part of the rule means finishing the operations inside a term before the term ends. Splitting into terms does this automatically and reduces confusion.

**Answer:** $15$.
