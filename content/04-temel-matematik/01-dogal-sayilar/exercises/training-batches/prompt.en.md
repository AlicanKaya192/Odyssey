A model is trained on a data set of $25\,000$ examples. The examples are processed in batches of $64$; each batch is one **step**. Processing the whole data set once is called an **epoch**, and the last batch counts as a step even if it is not full.

1. How many **full** batches are there in one epoch?
2. How many examples are in the last batch?
3. How many steps do $4$ epochs take in total?
