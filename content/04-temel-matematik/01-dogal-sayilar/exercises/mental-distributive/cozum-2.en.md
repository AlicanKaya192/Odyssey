**Idea:** Let us do the same calculations without shortcuts; that checks the results and shows how much work the distributive property saves.

**Step 1 — $47 \cdot 36$.** Split $47$ as $40 + 7$ (distributive again):

$$
40 \cdot 36 + 7 \cdot 36 = 1\,440 + 252 = 1\,692
$$

**Step 2 — $53 \cdot 36$.** $50 + 3$:

$$
50 \cdot 36 + 3 \cdot 36 = 1\,800 + 108 = 1\,908
$$

**Step 3 — Add.** $1\,692 + 1\,908 = 3\,600$ ✓, the same as the first method.

**Step 4 — $99 \cdot 46$.** Split $46$ as $40 + 6$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
99 \cdot 40 + 99 \cdot 6 &= 3\,960 + 594 \\
&= 4\,554
\end{aligned}
$$

✓

**Why the same result?** Both routes use the distributive property; the difference is **which** pieces we split the number into. The first method split so that $100$ appeared and the work became very short; this one split by digits and needed four multiplications. Seeing which split is easy comes with practice.

**Answer:** $3\,600$ and $4\,554$.
