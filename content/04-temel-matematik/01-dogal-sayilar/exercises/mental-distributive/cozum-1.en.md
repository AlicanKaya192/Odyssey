**What is asked?** Doing two calculations without long multiplication, by using the properties of the operations.

**Idea:** The distributive property $a(b + c) = ab + ac$ can be used both ways. Left to right: it splits a product into easy pieces. Right to left: it takes a common factor out.

**Step 1 — Take out the common factor.** $36$ is a factor of both terms:

$$
47 \cdot 36 + 53 \cdot 36 = (47 + 53) \cdot 36
$$

**Step 2 — Work out the bracket.** $47 + 53 = 100$ (which is why the question uses these numbers):

$$
100 \cdot 36 = 3\,600
$$

**Step 3 — Write $99$ as $100 - 1$.**

$$
99 \cdot 46 = (100 - 1) \cdot 46
$$

**Step 4 — Distribute.**

$$
\begin{aligned}
(100 - 1) \cdot 46 &= 100 \cdot 46 - 1 \cdot 46 \\
&= 4\,600 - 46 = 4\,554
\end{aligned}
$$

**Reading the result:** In both calculations a hard multiplication turned into an easy one (by $100$) plus an addition or subtraction. Seeing that a number is close to $100$ or $1\,000$ is most of mental arithmetic.

**Answer:** $3\,600$ and $4\,554$.
