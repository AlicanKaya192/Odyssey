**What is asked?** The solutions of three equations with the unknown in the exponent.

**Idea:** The exponential function is one-to-one: if $b^m = b^n$ then $m = n$. We bring both sides to the same base and set the exponents equal.

**Step 1 — The first equation.** $4^{x - 1} = (2^2)^{x - 1} = 2^{2x - 2}$.

$$
x + 3 = 2x - 2 \quad \Rightarrow \quad x = 5
$$

**Step 2 — The second equation.** Common base $3$: $27^x = 3^{3x}$, $9^{x + 2} = 3^{2x + 4}$.

$$
3x = 2x + 4 \quad \Rightarrow \quad x = 4
$$

**Step 3 — The third equation.** Common base $2$: $\left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^x = 2^{-x}$, $8^{x - 4} = 2^{3x - 12}$.

$$
-x = 3x - 12 \quad \Rightarrow \quad x = 3
$$

**Check:** $2^8 = 256 = 4^4$ ✓; $27^4 = 531{,}441 = 9^6$ ✓; $\left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^3 = \frac{1}{8} = 8^{-1}$ ✓.

**Watch out:** When expanding $(2^2)^{x - 1}$, the $2$ multiplies the **whole** exponent: $2x - 2$, not $2x - 1$.

**Answer:** $5$, $4$, $3$.
