**Idea:** Make a table, halving the amount every $4$ hours; the first two questions are read off the table.

**Step 1 — The table.**

| Hour | $0$ | $4$ | $8$ | $12$ | $16$ | $20$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| mg | $240$ | $120$ | $60$ | $30$ | $15$ | $7.5$ |

$30$ mg at $12$ hours; it reaches $7.5$ mg at $20$ hours.

**Step 2 — The hourly factor.** Four equal hourly steps must multiply to $\frac{1}{2}$. The square root of $\frac{1}{2}$ is $\approx 0.7071$, and its square root is $\approx 0.8409$; a fourth root is two square roots in a row.

**Why the same result?** Each column of the table is the case of $\left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^{t / 4}$ where $t / 4$ is a whole number. The hours in between need the formula; that is why the third question is solved with a root.

**Answer:** $30$, $20$ and $0.8409$.
