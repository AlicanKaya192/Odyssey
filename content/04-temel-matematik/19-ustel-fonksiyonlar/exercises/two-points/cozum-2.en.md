**Idea:** In an exponential function the value is multiplied by the same number every time $x$ goes up by one. From $x = 2$ to $x = 4$, two steps took it from $20$ down to $5$.

**Step 1 — The step factor.** The total factor over two steps is $\frac{5}{20} = \frac{1}{4}$. If two equal factors multiply to $\frac{1}{4}$, each is $\frac{1}{2}$: $b = \frac{1}{2}$.

**Step 2 — Walk back.** From $x = 2$ back to $x = 0$ is two steps: multiply by $2$ at each. $20 \to 40 \to 80$. $a = f(0) = 80$.

**Step 3 — Walk forward.** From $x = 4$ to $x = 6$, two steps: $5 \to 2.5 \to 1.25$.

**Why the same result?** The $b^2 = \frac{1}{4}$ of the first solution means exactly "the total factor of two steps". Walking back is multiplying by $b^{-1} = 2$, the step-by-step form of $a = 20 \cdot b^{-2}$.

**Answer:** $\frac{1}{2}$, $80$ and $1.25$.
