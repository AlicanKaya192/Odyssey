**What is asked?** The base, the initial value and one value of an exponential function with two known points.

**Idea:** Dividing $f(4)$ by $f(2)$ cancels $a$ and leaves only $b$.

**Step 1 — Two equations.** $a b^2 = 20$ and $a b^4 = 5$.

**Step 2 — Divide.**

$$
\frac{a b^4}{a b^2} = b^2 = \frac{5}{20} = \frac{1}{4} \quad \Rightarrow \quad b = \frac{1}{2}
$$

Since $b > 0$, $-\frac{1}{2}$ is not taken.

**Step 3 — $a$.** $a \cdot \frac{1}{4} = 20$, so $a = 80$.

**Step 4 — $f(6)$.**

$$
f(6) = 80 \cdot \left( \tfrac{1}{2} \right)^6 = \frac{80}{64} = \frac{5}{4}
$$

**Check:** $f(2) = 80 \cdot \frac{1}{4} = 20$ ✓, $f(4) = 80 \cdot \frac{1}{16} = 5$ ✓.

**Watch out:** The value is decreasing, so $b$ must come out smaller than $1$. Getting $b = 2$ means the ratio was taken upside down.

**Answer:** $b = \frac{1}{2}$, $a = 80$, $f(6) = \frac{5}{4} = 1.25$.
