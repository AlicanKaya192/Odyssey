A worked example for each method in the lesson, step by step. Try each question yourself first, then read the solution.

## 1. Which kind of table?

**Question:** For $x = 0, 1, 2, 3$, table A is $3, 6, 12, 24$ and table B
is $3, 6, 9, 12$. Which one is exponential?

In A the ratios are constant: $\frac{6}{3} = \frac{12}{6} = 2$. Exponential,
$3 \cdot 2^x$. In B the differences are constant: $6 - 3 = 9 - 6 = 3$.
Linear, $3 + 3x$.

## 2. Initial value and factor

**Question:** For $f(x) = a \cdot b^x$, $f(0) = 5$ and $f(1) = 15$. What is
$f(3)$?

$f(0) = a = 5$. $f(1) = 5b = 15$, so $b = 3$. $f(3) = 5 \cdot 27 = 135$.

## 3. From two points

**Question:** $f(1) = 6$ and $f(3) = 54$. What are $a$ and $b$?

Take the ratio: $\frac{f(3)}{f(1)} = \frac{a b^3}{a b} = b^2 = 9$, so
$b = 3$ ($b > 0$). $a \cdot 3 = 6$, $a = 2$.

## 4. Growth by a percentage

**Question:** What does $5000$ become in two years at $8$ percent annual
compound interest?

$5000 \cdot 1.08^2 = 5000 \cdot 1.1664 = 5832$. With simple interest it
would be $5800$; the difference is interest on the interest.

## 5. Losing value

**Question:** A car worth $400{,}000$ loses $15$ percent of its value each
year. What is it worth after two years?

The factor is $1 - 0.15 = 0.85$. $400{,}000 \cdot 0.85^2 = 400{,}000 \cdot
0.7225 = 289{,}000$.

## 6. Half-life

**Question:** How much is left of $200$ grams of a substance with a
half-life of $3$ days after $12$ days?

$\frac{12}{3} = 4$ half-lives: $200 \cdot \left( \frac{1}{2} \right)^4 =
\frac{200}{16} = 12.5$ grams.

## 7. Doubling

**Question:** $500$ bacteria double every $30$ minutes. How many are there
after $3$ hours?

$3$ hours $= 180$ minutes $= 6$ times $30$ minutes. $500 \cdot 2^6 = 500
\cdot 64 = 32{,}000$.

## 8. An exponential equation

**Question:** Solve $8^x = 4^{x + 1}$.

Common base $2$: $2^{3x} = 2^{2(x + 1)} = 2^{2x + 2}$. Set the exponents
equal: $3x = 2x + 2$, $x = 2$. Check: $8^2 = 64$, $4^3 = 64$ ✓.

## 9. Continuous growth

**Question:** What does $2000$ become in two years at $5$ percent
continuous interest?

$2000 \cdot e^{0.05 \cdot 2} = 2000 \cdot e^{0.1} \approx 2000 \cdot 1.10517
\approx 2210.34$.

## 10. Sigmoid

**Question:** For $\sigma(z) = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-z}}$, what are $\sigma(0)$
and $\sigma(-2)$?

$\sigma(0) = \frac{1}{1 + 1} = 0.5$. $\sigma(-2) = \frac{1}{1 + e^2} \approx
\frac{1}{8.39} \approx 0.12$. A negative score, a low probability.
