**Idea:** $w$ is how much $y$ grows when $x$ grows by one unit. Between the two points, how much did $x$ grow and how much did $y$?

**Step 1 — The increases.** $x$: $5 - 2 = 3$. $y$: $16 - 7 = 9$.

**Step 2 — Increase per unit.**

$$
w = \frac{9}{3} = 3
$$

**Step 3 — $b$.** Going back from $(2, 7)$: to reach $x = 0$, $x$ drops by $2$ and $y$ by $2 \cdot 3 = 6$: $b = 7 - 6 = 1$.

**Step 4 — The prediction.** From $(5, 16)$ to $x = 10$ is $5$ units: $16 + 5 \cdot 3 = 31$.

**Why the same result?** The subtraction in the system ($5w - 2w = 16 - 7$) says exactly "difference in $x$ times $w$ = difference in $y$". This route did the same calculation with the idea of slope; the Coordinate Plane section covers slope in detail.

**Answer:** $3$, $1$ and $31$.
