**Idea:** Every term of the second equation divides by $2$: $x - 2y = -2$. There, $x$ is easy to isolate.

**Step 1 — Simplify and isolate.** $x - 2y = -2 \Rightarrow x = 2y - 2$.

**Step 2 — Put it into the first.**

$$
\begin{aligned}
5(2y - 2) + 3y &= 29 \\
13y - 10 &= 29 \\
y &= 3
\end{aligned}
$$

**Step 3 — $x$.** $x = 2 \cdot 3 - 2 = 4$.

**Why the same result?** Dividing an equation by a number, like multiplying, does not change its solutions. After simplifying, the coefficient of $x$ became $1$ and substitution became a short route.

**Answer:** $(4, 3)$.
