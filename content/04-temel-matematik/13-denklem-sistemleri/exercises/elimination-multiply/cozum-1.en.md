**What is asked?** The solution of a system whose coefficients do not match directly.

**Idea:** The coefficients of $y$ are $3$ and $-4$; their LCM is $12$. Multiplying the first by $4$ and the second by $3$ gives $+12y$ and $-12y$; adding removes $y$.

**Step 1 — Multiply.** Every term, the right side included:

$$
\begin{aligned}
20x + 12y &= 116 \\
6x - 12y &= -12
\end{aligned}
$$

**Step 2 — Add.**

$$
26x = 104 \quad\Rightarrow\quad x = 4
$$

**Step 3 — $y$.** From the first equation: $20 + 3y = 29$, $3y = 9$, $y = 3$.

**Check:** $20 + 9 = 29$ ✓; $8 - 12 = -4$ ✓.

**Watch out:** Forgetting the right side when multiplying ($20x + 12y = 29$) changes the system and gives a wrong result.

**Answer:** $x = 4$, $y = 3$.
