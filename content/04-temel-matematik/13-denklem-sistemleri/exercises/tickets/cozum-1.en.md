**What is asked?** The numbers of two kinds of ticket; two unknowns, two pieces of information.

**Idea:** Each piece of information is an equation: one counts tickets, one counts money. Get one unknown from the ticket equation and put it into the money equation.

**Step 1 — The unknowns.** $t$ = full tickets, $s$ = student tickets.

**Step 2 — The equations.**

$$
\begin{cases}
t + s = 150 \\
40t + 25s = 4\,950
\end{cases}
$$

**Step 3 — Substitute.** $s = 150 - t$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
40t + 25(150 - t) &= 4\,950 \\
40t + 3\,750 - 25t &= 4\,950 \\
15t &= 1\,200 \\
t &= 80
\end{aligned}
$$

**Step 4 — Students.** $s = 150 - 80 = 70$.

**Check:** $80 + 70 = 150$ ✓; $40 \cdot 80 + 25 \cdot 70 = 3\,200 + 1\,750 = 4\,950$ ✓.

**Answer:** $80$ full and $70$ student tickets.
