**Idea:** What would the takings be if all the tickets were student tickets? The shortfall is explained by the extra money each full ticket brings.

**Step 1 — All student.** $150 \cdot 25 = 3\,750$.

**Step 2 — The shortfall.** $4\,950 - 3\,750 = 1\,200$.

**Step 3 — The difference per full ticket.** $40 - 25 = 15$.

**Step 4 — Number of full tickets.** $1\,200 \div 15 = 80$; students $150 - 80 = 70$.

**Why the same result?** Rearranged, the equation $40t + 25(150 - t) = 4\,950$ of the first route becomes $25 \cdot 150 + 15t = 4\,950$: the "all student" takings plus $15$ extra for each full ticket. This route built the same equation in words.

**Answer:** $80$ and $70$.
