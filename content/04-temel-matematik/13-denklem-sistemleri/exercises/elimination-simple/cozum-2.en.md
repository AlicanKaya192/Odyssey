**Idea:** In the second equation $x$ is easy to isolate: $x = y + 2$. Put it into the first.

**Step 1 — Isolate.** $x = y + 2$.

**Step 2 — Substitute.**

$$
\begin{aligned}
&(y + 2) + 2y = 11 \\
\Rightarrow\; &3y + 2 = 11 \\
\Rightarrow\; &y = 3
\end{aligned}
$$

**Step 3 — Go back.** $x = 3 + 2 = 5$.

**Why the same result?** In elimination we removed $x$ by subtracting the equations; here we used what the second equation says, "$x$ is $2$ more than $y$". Both routes reach the same equation in one unknown ($3y = 9$).

**Answer:** $(5, 3)$.
