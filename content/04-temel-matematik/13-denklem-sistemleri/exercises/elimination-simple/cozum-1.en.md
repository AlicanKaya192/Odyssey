**What is asked?** The pair $(x, y)$ satisfying both equations.

**Idea:** Both equations have the same coefficient for $x$. Subtracting one from the other removes $x$ and leaves only $y$.

**Step 1 — Subtract.** The second from the first, **all** its terms:

$$
(x + 2y) - (x - y) = 11 - 2
$$

$$
3y = 9 \quad\Rightarrow\quad y = 3
$$

**Step 2 — Find $x$.** From the second equation: $x - 3 = 2$, $x = 5$.

**Check:** $5 + 6 = 11$ ✓, $5 - 3 = 2$ ✓.

**Watch out:** Writing $2y - y = y$ when subtracting is the most common slip; subtracting the $-y$ of the second equation gives $+y$: $2y + y = 3y$.

**Answer:** $x = 5$, $y = 3$.
