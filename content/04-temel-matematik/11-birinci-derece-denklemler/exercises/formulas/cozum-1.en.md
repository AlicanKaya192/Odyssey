**What is asked?** Getting the unknown on its own in two formulas and putting in the numbers.

**Idea:** A formula is an equation too; the balance rules hold with letters as well. First rearrange for the unknown in general, then put in the numbers. That way the same formula is ready for other numbers too.

**Step 1 — $x$ from the $z$ formula.**

$$
\begin{aligned}
&z = \frac{x - \mu}{\sigma} \\
\Rightarrow\; &z\sigma = x - \mu \\
\Rightarrow\; &x = \mu + z\sigma
\end{aligned}
$$

**Step 2 — The numbers.**

$$
x = 70 + 1.5 \cdot 8 = 70 + 12 = 82
$$

**Step 3 — $C$ from the $F$ formula.**

$$
F - 32 = \frac{9}{5}C \quad\Rightarrow\quad C = \frac{5}{9}(F - 32)
$$

**Step 4 — The numbers.**

$$
C = \frac{5}{9}(98.6 - 32) = \frac{5}{9} \cdot 66.6 = 5 \cdot 7.4 = 37
$$

**Reading the result:** $z = 1.5$ says the observation is $1.5$ spreads (that is, $12$) above the mean. $98.6\,°\mathrm{F}$ is normal human body temperature, $37\,°\mathrm{C}$.

**Watch out:** In the second formula, multiplying by $\frac{5}{9}$ first and subtracting $32$ afterwards breaks the order: $C = \frac{5}{9}F - 32$ is wrong.

**Answer:** $x = 82$; $C = 37$.
