**Idea:** Put the numbers in straight away and solve the ordinary equation that appears. For a one-off calculation this is a fine route too.

**Step 1 — The first equation.**

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\frac{x - 70}{8} = 1.5 \\
\Rightarrow\; &x - 70 = 12 \\
\Rightarrow\; &x = 82
\end{aligned}
$$

**Step 2 — The second equation.**

$$
\begin{aligned}
&98.6 = \frac{9}{5}C + 32 \\
\Rightarrow\; &66.6 = \frac{9}{5}C \\
\Rightarrow\; &C = 66.6 \cdot \frac{5}{9} = 37
\end{aligned}
$$

**Why the same result?** Both routes do the same operations in the same order; the first did them with letters and put the numbers in at the end, this one started with numbers. The general formula pays off when many calculations are coming; starting with numbers is practical for a single one.

**Answer:** $82$ and $37$.
