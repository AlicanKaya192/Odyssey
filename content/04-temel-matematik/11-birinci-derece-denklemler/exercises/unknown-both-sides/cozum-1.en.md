**What is asked?** Solving an equation with the unknown on both sides.

**Idea:** The balance rule: doing the same to both sides keeps the equality. First collect the unknowns on one side and the numbers on the other; then divide by the coefficient.

**Step 1 — Collect the $x$ terms on the left.** Subtract $2x$ from both sides:

$$
4x - 5 = 11
$$

**Step 2 — Collect the numbers on the right.** Add $5$ to both sides:

$$
4x = 16
$$

**Step 3 — Divide by the coefficient.**

$$
x = 4
$$

**Check:** Left $6 \cdot 4 - 5 = 19$, right $2 \cdot 4 + 11 = 19$ ✓.

**Watch out:** Leaving $+2x$ when moving it left and writing $8x = 16$ is a common slip; moving a term across means subtracting it from both sides.

**Answer:** $x = 4$.
