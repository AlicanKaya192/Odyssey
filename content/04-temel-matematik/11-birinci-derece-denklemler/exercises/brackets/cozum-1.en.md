**What is asked?** Solving an equation with brackets.

**Idea:** First simplify each side separately (open brackets, collect like terms); what remains is a two-step equation.

**Step 1 — Open the brackets.**

$$
3x - 6 - 2x - 2 = 4 - x
$$

In $-2(x + 1)$ the minus went to both terms: $-2x - 2$.

**Step 2 — Simplify the left side.** $3x - 2x = x$, $-6 - 2 = -8$:

$$
x - 8 = 4 - x
$$

**Step 3 — Collect.** Add $x$ and $8$ to both sides:

$$
2x = 12
$$

**Step 4 — Divide.** $x = 6$.

**Check:** Left $3 \cdot 4 - 2 \cdot 7 = 12 - 14 = -2$, right $4 - 6 = -2$ ✓.

**Watch out:** Writing $-2(x + 1) = -2x + 2$ is the most common slip; then $x - 4 = 4 - x$ gives $x = 4$ and the check fails.

**Answer:** $x = 6$.
