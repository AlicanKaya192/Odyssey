**Idea:** To bring each side to the form $ax + b$, write down what each piece contributes to $x$ and to the constant. Then $a_1 x + b_1 = a_2 x + b_2$ gives $x = \dfrac{b_2 - b_1}{a_1 - a_2}$.

**Step 1 — The left side.**

| Piece | to $x$ | to the constant |
|---|---|---|
| $3(x - 2)$ | $3$ | $-6$ |
| $-2(x + 1)$ | $-2$ | $-2$ |
| total | $1$ | $-8$ |

**Step 2 — The right side.** $4 - x$: $-1$ to $x$, $4$ to the constant.

**Step 3 — Solve.**

$$
x = \frac{4 - (-8)}{1 - (-1)} = \frac{12}{2} = 6
$$

**Why the same result?** Solving $a_1 x + b_1 = a_2 x + b_2$ with the balance rule always gives $(a_1 - a_2)x = b_2 - b_1$; the formula does those steps once and keeps them. If $a_1 = a_2$, the denominator is zero: either no solution or infinitely many.

**Answer:** $6$.
