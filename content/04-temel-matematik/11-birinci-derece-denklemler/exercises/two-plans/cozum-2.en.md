**Idea:** At the start ($0$ GB) A costs $30$ more than B. With each GB, B adds $4 - 1.5 = 2.5$ more than A. After how many GB is the gap closed?

**Step 1 — The starting gap.** $60 - 30 = 30$ (A is dearer).

**Step 2 — The gap closed per GB.** $4 - 1.5 = 2.5$.

**Step 3 — Closing.** $30 \div 2.5 = 12$ GB.

**Step 4 — The bill.** $30 + 4 \cdot 12 = 78$.

**Why the same result?** Rearranging $60 + 1.5x = 30 + 4x$ gives $60 - 30 = (4 - 1.5)x$: the left side is the starting gap, the coefficient on the right the gap closed per GB. The equation writes this spoken reasoning on one line. On a graph, it is the two lines crossing at $x = 12$.

**Answer:** $12$ and $78$.
