**What is asked?** Solving an equation with fractions.

**Idea:** Multiplying both sides by the LCM of the denominators turns every fraction into a whole number. Keep the numerators in brackets: a fraction bar acts like a bracket.

**Step 1 — The LCM.** $\text{LCM}(2, 3) = 6$.

**Step 2 — Multiply by $6$.** Every term:

$$
6 \cdot \frac{x - 1}{2} + 6 \cdot \frac{x + 2}{3} = 6 \cdot 5
$$

$$
3(x - 1) + 2(x + 2) = 30
$$

**Step 3 — Expand and collect.**

$$
3x - 3 + 2x + 4 = 30 \quad\Rightarrow\quad 5x + 1 = 30
$$

**Step 4 — Solve.**

$$
5x = 29 \quad\Rightarrow\quad x = \frac{29}{5} = 5.8
$$

**Check:** $\dfrac{4.8}{2} + \dfrac{7.8}{3} = 2.4 + 2.6 = 5$ ✓.

**Watch out:** Multiplying the numerators without brackets and writing $3x - 1 + 2x + 2 = 30$, or forgetting to multiply the $5$ on the right, are common slips.

**Answer:** $x = \dfrac{29}{5}$.
