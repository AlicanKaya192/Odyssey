A worked example for each method in the lesson. Try the question yourself first, then read the solution.

## 1. A two-step equation

**Question:** Solve $4x - 9 = 23$.

$4x = 32$, $x = 8$. Check: $32 - 9 = 23$ ✓.

## 2. The unknown on both sides

**Question:** Solve $7x + 2 = 3x - 10$.

$4x = -12$, $x = -3$. Check: $-21 + 2 = -19$ and $-9 - 10 = -19$ ✓.

## 3. Brackets and a minus

**Question:** Solve $3 - 2(x - 4) = x + 2$.

$$
3 - 2x + 8 = x + 2 \quad\Rightarrow\quad 11 - 2x = x + 2 \quad\Rightarrow\quad 9 = 3x \quad\Rightarrow\quad x = 3
$$

## 4. An equation with fractions

**Question:** Solve $\dfrac{x + 1}{4} = \dfrac{x - 2}{3}$.

Multiply both sides by $12$: $3(x + 1) = 4(x - 2)$, $3x + 3 = 4x - 8$, $x = 11$.

## 5. An equation with decimals

**Question:** Solve $0.2x + 1.5 = 0.5x$.

Multiply by $10$: $2x + 15 = 5x$, $15 = 3x$, $x = 5$.

## 6. No solution

**Question:** Does $3(x - 1) = 3x + 2$ have a solution?

$3x - 3 = 3x + 2$, so $-3 = 2$: false. **No solution.**

## 7. Rearranging a formula

**Question:** Get $h$ out of the trapezium area formula $A = \dfrac{(a + b)h}{2}$.

$2A = (a + b)h$, $h = \dfrac{2A}{a + b}$.

## 8. A word problem

**Question:** Three consecutive whole numbers add up to $72$. What is the largest?

$x + (x + 1) + (x + 2) = 72$, $3x + 3 = 72$, $x = 23$. The largest is $25$.
