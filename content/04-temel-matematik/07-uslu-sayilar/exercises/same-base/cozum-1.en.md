**What is asked?** A product and quotient of powers with the same base.

**Idea:** With the same base, exponents add in multiplication and subtract in division. We work with exponents and convert to a number only at the end; no big numbers needed.

**Step 1 — The top: multiplication.**

$$
2^3 \cdot 2^5 = 2^{3 + 5} = 2^8
$$

**Step 2 — Division.**

$$
\frac{2^8}{2^6} = 2^{8 - 6} = 2^2
$$

**Step 3 — Convert to a number.** $2^2 = 4$.

**Check:** With numbers: $2^3 \cdot 2^5 = 8 \cdot 32 = 256$ and $2^6 = 64$; $256 \div 64 = 4$ ✓.

**Watch out:** Writing $2^3 \cdot 2^5 = 2^{15}$ (multiplying the exponents) or $4^8$ (multiplying the bases) are common slips. In multiplication the base stays and the exponents **add**.

**Answer:** $4$.
