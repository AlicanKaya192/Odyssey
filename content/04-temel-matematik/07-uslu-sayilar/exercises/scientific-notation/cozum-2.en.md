**Idea:** Switch from scientific notation to ordinary numbers, calculate, then convert back. Longer, but every step can be checked by eye.

**Step 1 — Expand the factors.** $3 \times 10^4 = 30\,000$ and $2 \times 10^{-7} = 0.000\,000\,2$.

**Step 2 — Multiply.** $30\,000 \cdot 0.000\,000\,2$: $3 \cdot 2 = 6$; the point shifts are $4$ right and $7$ left, a net $3$ left:

$$
0.006
$$

**Step 3 — Expand the quotient.** $8 \times 10^5 = 800\,000$, $4 \times 10^{-2} = 0.04$:

$$
800\,000 \div 0.04 = 80\,000\,000 \div 4 = 20\,000\,000
$$

(We moved both points two places right.)

**Step 4 — Convert back.** $20\,000\,000 = 2 \times 10^7$: $n = 7$.

**Why the same result?** Scientific notation is a shortcut that keeps point shifts in the exponents. Here we counted the shifts by hand; the exponent rules do the same counting with addition and subtraction.

**Answer:** $0.006$ and $7$.
