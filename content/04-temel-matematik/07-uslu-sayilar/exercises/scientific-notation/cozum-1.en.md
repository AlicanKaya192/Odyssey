**What is asked?** Multiplying and dividing numbers written in scientific notation.

**Idea:** In multiplication and division, order and grouping are free; gather the coefficients in one place and the powers of $10$ in another. The exponent rules hold for powers of $10$.

**Step 1 — The product: group.**

$$
(3 \cdot 2) \times (10^4 \cdot 10^{-7}) = 6 \times 10^{4 + (-7)} = 6 \times 10^{-3}
$$

**Step 2 — Convert to a decimal.** $10^{-3}$ means moving the point $3$ places left: $6 \times 10^{-3} = 0.006$.

**Step 3 — The quotient: group.**

$$
\frac{8}{4} \times \frac{10^5}{10^{-2}} = 2 \times 10^{5 - (-2)} = 2 \times 10^{7}
$$

$n = 7$.

**Reading the result:** Dividing by a number with a negative exponent makes the result larger: dividing by $10^{-2}$ is multiplying by $100$. That is why we went from $10^5$ up to $10^7$.

**Watch out:** Writing $5 - (-2)$ as $3$ is a common slip; minus a negative is plus.

**Answer:** $0.006$ and $n = 7$.
