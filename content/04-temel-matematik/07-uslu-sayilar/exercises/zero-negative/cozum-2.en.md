**Idea:** Do the same calculation with decimals and compare with the fraction result. This also shows that negative exponents are small positive numbers.

**Step 1 — Decimal values.**

$$
3^{-2} = \frac{1}{9} = 0.\overline{1}, \qquad 5^0 = 1, \qquad 2^{-1} = 0.5
$$

**Step 2 — Add.**

$$
0.111\dots + 1 - 0.5 = 0.611\dots
$$

**Step 3 — Compare with the fraction.** $11 \div 18$: $110 = 18 \cdot 6 + 2$, $20 = 18 \cdot 1 + 2$, … the remainder is always $2$ and the digit always $1$: $0.6111\dots$ ✓.

**Why the same result?** We added the same numbers in two notations. The fraction route gives the exact result; the decimal route is a quick check that the answer is sensible. Since the answer is asked as a fraction, it is $\frac{11}{18}$.

**Answer:** $\dfrac{11}{18}$.
