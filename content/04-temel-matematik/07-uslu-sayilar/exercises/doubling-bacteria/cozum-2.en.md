**Idea:** Gather the doublings into an hourly factor. There are $3$ doublings in an hour, so the number grows $2^3 = 8$ times per hour.

**Step 1 — The hourly factor.** $60 \div 20 = 3$ doublings; the factor is $2^3 = 8$.

**Step 2 — After $2$ hours.** The two-hour factor is $8^2 = 64$:

$$
100 \cdot 64 = 6\,400
$$

**Step 3 — For $51\,200$.** The total factor needed is $512$. $8^3 = 512$, so $3$ hours $= 180$ minutes.

**Why the same result?** $8^2 = (2^3)^2 = 2^6$ and $8^3 = (2^3)^3 = 2^9$: in a power of a power the exponents multiply. Using an hourly factor means grouping the $20$-minute doublings in threes.

**Answer:** $6\,400$ and $180$.
