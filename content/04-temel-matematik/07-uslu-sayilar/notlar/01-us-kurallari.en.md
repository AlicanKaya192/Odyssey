## Definition

$$
a^n = \underbrace{a \cdot a \cdot \ldots \cdot a}_{n}, \qquad a^0 = 1, \qquad a^{-n} = \frac{1}{a^n} \quad (a \neq 0)
$$

## Rules

| Rule | Formula | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Multiplication | $a^m \cdot a^n = a^{m+n}$ | $2^3 \cdot 2^4 = 2^7$ |
| Division | $\dfrac{a^m}{a^n} = a^{m-n}$ | $\dfrac{5^6}{5^4} = 5^2$ |
| Power of a power | $(a^m)^n = a^{mn}$ | $(3^2)^3 = 3^6$ |
| Power of a product | $(ab)^n = a^n b^n$ | $(2x)^3 = 8x^3$ |
| Power of a quotient | $\left(\dfrac{a}{b}\right)^n = \dfrac{a^n}{b^n}$ | $\left(\dfrac{2}{3}\right)^2 = \dfrac{4}{9}$ |
| Negative exponent | $\left(\dfrac{a}{b}\right)^{-n} = \left(\dfrac{b}{a}\right)^n$ | $\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^{-3} = 8$ |

**There is no rule for addition and subtraction:** $(a + b)^n \neq a^n +
b^n$.

## Powers worth remembering

| $2^n$ | $3^n$ | $5^n$ | $10^n$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| $2^4 = 16$ | $3^3 = 27$ | $5^3 = 125$ | $10^3 = 1\,000$ |
| $2^5 = 32$ | $3^4 = 81$ | $5^4 = 625$ | $10^6 = 1$ million |
| $2^8 = 256$ | $3^5 = 243$ | | $10^9 = 1$ billion |
| $2^{10} = 1\,024$ | | | $10^{-3} = 0.001$ |

## Negative bases

- $(-a)^n$: positive if $n$ is even, negative if odd.
- $-a^n = -(a^n)$: the exponent belongs only to $a$.

## Scientific notation

$$
a \times 10^n, \qquad 1 \le a < 10
$$

When multiplying, multiply the coefficients and add the exponents; when
dividing, divide the coefficients and subtract the exponents. Adjust so
the coefficient lies between $1$ and $10$.

## Practical tips

- Convert different bases to the same base: $4 = 2^2$, $8 = 2^3$, $9 = 3^2$, $27 = 3^3$.
- If $a^x = a^y$ then $x = y$ (same base, $a > 0$, $a \neq 1$).
- A negative exponent flips a fraction; it does not change the sign.
- If you forget a rule, write out a small example: $2^2 \cdot 2^3 = (2 \cdot 2)(2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2)$.
