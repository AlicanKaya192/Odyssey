**Idea:** The first position is the familiar $5$-$12$-$13$ triple. In the second position, $13^2 - 7^2$ is easy with the difference of squares identity.

**Step 1 — The triple.** Seeing $5$ and $13$ means $5$-$12$-$13$: the height is $12$.

**Step 2 — Difference of squares.** $13^2 - 7^2 = (13 - 7)(13 + 7) = 6 \cdot 20 = 120$. $\sqrt{120} = 2\sqrt{30} \approx 10.954$.

**Step 3 — The difference.** $12 - 2\sqrt{30} \approx 1.046$.

**Why the same result?** The identity $a^2 - b^2 = (a - b)(a + b)$ does the subtraction in Pythagoras without squaring large numbers; it reaches the same number as $169 - 49$ in the first way.

**Answer:** $12$, $10.954$ and $1.046$.
