**Idea:** Think of the model's tank as built from tiny cubes. In the real car each tiny cube becomes a cube $20$ times larger in every direction.

**Step 1 — Length.** Every centimetre in one direction becomes $20$ centimetres; the other way round, the $400$ cm car is $20$ cm in the model.

**Step 2 — Surface.** Every tiny square on the surface grows $20$ times in width and in length: it splits into $20 \cdot 20 = 400$ tiny squares.

**Step 3 — Volume.** Every tiny cube grows $20$ times in length, width and height: $20 \cdot 20 \cdot 20 = 8000$ tiny cubes. $5$ mL $\cdot$ $8000 = 40$ litres.

**Why the same result?** The $k^2$ and $k^3$ rule is exactly this count: area is measured in two directions and volume in three, so the scale factor is applied two or three times.

**Answer:** $20$, $400$ and $40$.
