**What is asked?** The length, area and volume ratios of two similar objects.

**Idea:** If lengths are multiplied by $k$, area is multiplied by $k^2$ and volume by $k^3$. Here, from model to real, $k = 20$.

**Step 1 — Length.** $4$ m $= 400$ cm; the model is $\frac{400}{20} = 20$ cm.

**Step 2 — Surface.** $k^2 = 20^2 = 400$ times.

**Step 3 — The tank.** Volume $k^3 = 8000$ times: $5 \cdot 8000 = 40{,}000$ mL $= 40$ litres.

**Check:** $40$ litres is reasonable for a real car's tank; $5 \cdot 20 = 100$ mL would be absurdly small.

**Watch out:** Multiplying the volume by $20$ as well is the most common mistake. The tank's length, width and height each grow $20$ times.

**Answer:** $20$ cm, $400$ times, $40$ litres.
