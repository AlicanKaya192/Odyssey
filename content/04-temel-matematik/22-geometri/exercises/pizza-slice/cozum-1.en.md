**What is asked?** The angle, area and arc length of one of the equal slices.

**Idea:** A slice is the fraction of a full turn given by its central angle; the area and the arc shrink by the same fraction.

**Step 1 — The angle.** $\frac{360°}{8} = 45°$.

**Step 2 — The area.**

$$
\frac{45}{360} \cdot \pi \cdot 15^2 = \frac{225\pi}{8} \approx 88.36
$$

**Step 3 — The crust.**

$$
\frac{45}{360} \cdot 2\pi \cdot 15 = \frac{30\pi}{8} = \frac{15\pi}{4} \approx 11.78
$$

**Check:** The crusts of $8$ slices make $8 \cdot 11.78 \approx 94.25 \approx 30\pi$, the pizza's circumference ✓.

**Watch out:** Do not add the two radii to the crust; the question asks only for the outer edge, the arc. The whole perimeter of a slice would be $11.78 + 30$.

**Answer:** $45°$, $88.36$ $\text{cm}^2$, $11.78$ cm.
