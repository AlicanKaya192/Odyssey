**Idea:** No sector formula is needed: $8$ equal slices means one eighth of everything.

**Step 1 — The angle.** One eighth of a full turn of $360°$: $45°$.

**Step 2 — The area.** The whole pizza is $\pi \cdot 225 \approx 706.86$; one eighth is $\approx 88.36$.

**Step 3 — The crust.** The whole circumference is $2\pi \cdot 15 = 30\pi \approx 94.25$; one eighth is $\approx 11.78$.

**Why the same result?** The $\frac{\theta}{360}$ in the formula answers "what fraction of the whole is the slice"; here $\frac{45}{360} = \frac{1}{8}$. With equal slices we read that fraction straight from the number of slices.

**Answer:** $45$, $88.36$ and $11.78$.
