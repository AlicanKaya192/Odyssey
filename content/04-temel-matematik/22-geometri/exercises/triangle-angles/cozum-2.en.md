**Idea:** The angles are in the ratio $1 : 2 : 3$. Sharing $180°$ in this ratio means cutting $180°$ into $1 + 2 + 3 = 6$ equal parts and handing them out.

**Step 1 — One part.** $180° \div 6 = 30°$; $x$ is one part.

**Step 2 — The largest.** $3$ parts: $90°$.

**Step 3 — The exterior angle.** The exterior angle is the sum of the other two angles: $2 + 3 = 5$ parts, $150°$.

**Why the same result?** The equation $6x = 180°$ is the sentence "cut into $6$ parts" written in algebra. Counting the exterior angle in parts is the equality $180° - x = 5x$ itself, because $6x = 180°$.

**Answer:** $30$, $90$ and $150$.
