**What is asked?** The values of three angles given in proportion, and an exterior angle.

**Idea:** A triangle's interior angles add up to $180°$; an exterior angle is the supplement of the interior angle.

**Step 1 — $x$.** $x + 2x + 3x = 6x = 180°$, so $x = 30°$.

**Step 2 — The angles.** $30°$, $60°$, $90°$. The largest is $90°$: this is a right triangle, in fact a $30$-$60$-$90$ triangle.

**Step 3 — The exterior angle.** The smallest angle is $30°$; the exterior angle is $180° - 30° = 150°$.

**Check:** The exterior angle must be the sum of the other two interior angles: $60° + 90° = 150°$ ✓.

**Watch out:** Do not confuse the exterior angle with the interior one; the exterior angle is formed with the **extension** of a side, so it is the supplement of the interior angle.

**Answer:** $x = 30°$, largest angle $90°$, exterior angle $150°$.
