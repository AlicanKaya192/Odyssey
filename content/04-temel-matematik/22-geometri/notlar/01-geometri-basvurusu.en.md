## Angles

| Situation | Rule |
|---|---|
| complementary | sum $90°$ |
| supplementary | sum $180°$ |
| vertical angles | equal |
| parallels + transversal | alternate interior angles equal |
| full turn | $360°$ |

## Triangles

- The interior angles add up to $180°$; an exterior angle is the sum of the
  other two interior angles.
- Equilateral: every angle $60°$. Isosceles: the angles opposite the equal
  sides are equal.
- Triangle inequality: $|a - b| < c < a + b$.

## Pythagoras

$a^2 + b^2 = c^2$ ($c$ is the hypotenuse, opposite the right angle).

| Triples | $3$-$4$-$5$, $5$-$12$-$13$, $8$-$15$-$17$, $7$-$24$-$25$ |
|---|---|
| test | $c^2 = a^2 + b^2$ right, larger obtuse, smaller acute |
| $45$-$45$-$90$ | $1 : 1 : \sqrt{2}$ |
| $30$-$60$-$90$ | $1 : \sqrt{3} : 2$ |

## Area and perimeter

| Shape | Area | Perimeter |
|---|---|---|
| square | $a^2$ | $4a$ |
| rectangle | $ab$ | $2(a + b)$ |
| triangle | $\frac{1}{2} t h$ | sum of the sides |
| parallelogram | $t h$ | sum of the sides |
| trapezoid | $\frac{a + c}{2} h$ | sum of the sides |
| disc | $\pi r^2$ | $2\pi r$ |
| sector ($\theta$ degrees) | $\frac{\theta}{360} \pi r^2$ | arc $\frac{\theta}{360} 2\pi r$ |

## Volume

| Solid | Volume |
|---|---|
| cube | $a^3$ |
| prism, cylinder | base area $\times$ height |
| cone | $\frac{1}{3}$ base area $\times$ height |
| sphere | $\frac{4}{3} \pi r^3$ (surface $4\pi r^2$) |

Scale $k$: length $k$, area $k^2$, volume $k^3$.

## In machine learning

- Distance: $\sqrt{\sum \Delta_i^2}$, one term per feature.
- IoU $= \dfrac{\text{intersection}}{\text{union}}$, union $= A + B - \text{intersection}$.

## Practical tips

- The height is at right angles to the base; not the slanted side.
- Identify the hypotenuse before using Pythagoras.
- For similarity, match the corresponding sides first, then take ratios.
- Area is in unit squares, volume in unit cubes: square and cube the units
  too.
