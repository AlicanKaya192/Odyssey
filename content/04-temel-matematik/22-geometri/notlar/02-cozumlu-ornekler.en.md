A worked example for each method in the lesson, step by step. Try each question yourself first, then read the solution.

## 1. Complementary and supplementary

**Question:** What are the complement and the supplement of a $28°$ angle?

Complement $90° - 28° = 62°$, supplement $180° - 28° = 152°$.

## 2. An isosceles triangle

**Question:** The top angle of an isosceles triangle is $40°$. What are the
base angles?

The base angles are equal and add up to $180° - 40° = 140°$; each is $70°$.

## 3. The triangle inequality

**Question:** In a triangle with sides $4$, $7$ and $x$, what range is $x$
in?

$x < 4 + 7 = 11$ and $7 < 4 + x$, so $x > 3$. $3 < x < 11$.

## 4. Pythagoras

**Question:** What is the hypotenuse of a right triangle with legs $5$ and
$12$? What is the other leg of one with hypotenuse $17$ and a leg of $8$?

$\sqrt{25 + 144} = \sqrt{169} = 13$. $\sqrt{289 - 64} = \sqrt{225} = 15$.

## 5. Is it a right triangle?

**Question:** Is the triangle with sides $9$, $12$ and $15$ a right
triangle?

$81 + 144 = 225 = 15^2$ ✓. It is right; in fact it is $3$ times the
$3$-$4$-$5$ triple.

## 6. Similarity and scale

**Question:** On a $1 : 50{,}000$ map two villages are $4$ cm apart. What
is the real distance?

$4 \cdot 50{,}000 = 200{,}000$ cm $= 2$ km.

## 7. Area

**Question:** What are the areas of a parallelogram with base $8$ and
height $5$, and of a triangle with base $10$ and height $7$?

Parallelogram $8 \cdot 5 = 40$. Triangle $\frac{1}{2} \cdot 10 \cdot 7 = 35$.

## 8. From circumference to area

**Question:** What is the area of a disc whose circumference is $12\pi$?

$2\pi r = 12\pi$, $r = 6$. Area $\pi \cdot 36 = 36\pi \approx 113.1$.

## 9. A sector

**Question:** In a disc with radius $10$, what are the arc length and the
area of a $90°$ sector?

The sector is $\frac{90}{360} = \frac{1}{4}$ of a full turn. Arc
$\frac{1}{4} \cdot 20\pi = 5\pi$, area $\frac{1}{4} \cdot 100\pi = 25\pi$.

## 10. Volume and scale

**Question:** What is the volume of a cylinder with radius $2$ and height
$5$? By what factor does the volume of a cube grow if its side is tripled?

$\pi \cdot 4 \cdot 5 = 20\pi \approx 62.8$. The volume grows $3^3 = 27$
times.

## 11. IoU

**Question:** The true box is $(0, 0)$–$(4, 4)$ and the prediction
$(2, 2)$–$(6, 6)$. What is the IoU?

Both boxes are $16$ square units. The intersection is $(2, 2)$–$(4, 4)$,
area $4$. The union is $16 + 16 - 4 = 28$.
$\text{IoU} = \frac{4}{28} = \frac{1}{7} \approx 0.14$: a weak prediction.
