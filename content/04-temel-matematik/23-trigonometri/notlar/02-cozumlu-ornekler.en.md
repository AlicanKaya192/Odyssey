A worked example for each method in the lesson, step by step. Try each question yourself first, then read the solution.

## 1. Three ratios

**Question:** In the triangle with legs $5$ and $12$ and hypotenuse $13$,
$A$ is the angle facing $5$. What are $\sin A$, $\cos A$, $\tan A$?

Opposite $5$, adjacent $12$: $\sin A = \frac{5}{13}$, $\cos A = \frac{12}{13}$,
$\tan A = \frac{5}{12}$.

## 2. A missing side

**Question:** A right triangle with hypotenuse $10$ has an angle of $30°$.
How long are the sides opposite and adjacent to this angle?

Opposite $10 \sin 30° = 5$. Adjacent $10 \cos 30° = 5\sqrt{3} \approx 8.66$.

## 3. From the identity

**Question:** $A$ is acute and $\cos A = 0.6$. What are $\sin A$ and
$\tan A$?

$\sin A = \sqrt{1 - 0.36} = 0.8$ (positive for an acute angle).
$\tan A = \frac{0.8}{0.6} = \frac{4}{3}$.

## 4. Radians

**Question:** How many radians is $135°$? How many degrees is
$\frac{5\pi}{6}$?

$135 \cdot \frac{\pi}{180} = \frac{3\pi}{4}$. $\frac{5\pi}{6} \cdot
\frac{180}{\pi} = 150°$.

## 5. Signs on the unit circle

**Question:** What are $\sin 210°$ and $\cos 120°$?

$210°$ is in quadrant III, matching $30°$: $\sin 210° = -\frac{1}{2}$.
$120°$ is in quadrant II, matching $60°$: $\cos 120° = -\frac{1}{2}$.

## 6. A negative angle

**Question:** What are $\cos(-60°)$ and $\sin(-90°)$?

Cosine does not change sign: $\cos(-60°) = \frac{1}{2}$. Sine does:
$\sin(-90°) = -1$.

## 7. Amplitude and period

**Question:** What are the amplitude and period of $y = 5\sin(4x)$?

Amplitude $5$, period $\frac{2\pi}{4} = \frac{\pi}{2}$.

## 8. A height

**Question:** From $30$ m away from the foot of a tower, its top is seen at
an angle of $45°$. How tall is the tower?

$\tan 45° = 1 = \frac{h}{30}$, $h = 30$ m.

## 9. The law of cosines

**Question:** A triangle has two sides $3$ and $5$ with an angle of $120°$
between them. What is the third side?

$c^2 = 9 + 25 - 2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 \cdot \left( -\frac{1}{2} \right) = 34 +
15 = 49$, $c = 7$. The obtuse angle makes the cosine negative, so the third
side is longer than Pythagoras would give.

## 10. Cosine similarity

**Question:** What are the cosine of the angle between $(1, 0)$ and
$(1, 1)$, and the angle itself?

$\frac{1 \cdot 1 + 0 \cdot 1}{1 \cdot \sqrt{2}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \approx
0.707$, angle $45°$.

## 11. Encoding the hour

**Question:** Where on the circle do the hours $h = 6$ and $h = 18$ land?

$\frac{2\pi \cdot 6}{24} = \frac{\pi}{2}$: $(0, 1)$. $\frac{2\pi \cdot
18}{24} = \frac{3\pi}{2}$: $(0, -1)$. Opposite each other; they are $12$
hours apart.
