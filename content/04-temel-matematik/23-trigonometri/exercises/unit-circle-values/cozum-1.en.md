**What is asked?** The trigonometric values of angles larger than $90°$.

**Idea:** Each angle's nearest angle to the $x$-axis (the reference angle) gives the value; the quadrant gives the sign.

**Step 1 — $\sin 240°$.** Quadrant III, sine negative; reference angle $60°$. $\sin 240° = -\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \approx -0.866$.

**Step 2 — $\cos 300°$.** Quadrant IV, cosine positive; reference angle $60°$. $\cos 300° = \frac{1}{2}$.

**Step 3 — $\tan 135°$.** Quadrant II: sine $+\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$, cosine $-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$. $\tan 135° = \frac{\sin}{\cos} = -1$.

**Check:** $\sin^2 240° + \cos^2 240° = \frac{3}{4} + \frac{1}{4} = 1$ ✓ ($\cos 240° = -\frac{1}{2}$).

**Watch out:** Find the sign and the value separately. Stopping at "$240 - 180 = 60$, $\sin 60°$ is positive" misses the sign.

**Answer:** $-0.866$, $\frac{1}{2}$, $-1$.
