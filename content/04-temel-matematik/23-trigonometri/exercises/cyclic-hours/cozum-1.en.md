**What is asked?** The distances between hours placed on a circle, and one component of a code.

**Idea:** Each hour is $15°$. We write the points' coordinates from the unit circle and use the distance formula.

**Step 1 — $22$ and $2$.** $330°$: $\left( \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}, -\frac{1}{2} \right)$. $30°$: $\left( \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}, \frac{1}{2} \right)$. The horizontal difference is $0$, the vertical $1$: distance $1$.

**Step 2 — $6$ and $18$.** $90°$: $(0, 1)$. $270°$: $(0, -1)$. Distance $2$, the diameter of the circle: the two hours furthest apart.

**Step 3 — Hour $8$.** $8 \cdot 15° = 120°$. $\cos 120° = -\frac{1}{2}$.

**Check:** No two codes can be further apart than the diameter ($2$) ✓. $22$ and $2$ are $20$ apart as numbers but $1$ apart as codes: in line with the real four-hour gap.

**Watch out:** When converting the angle to radians, do not forget the $2\pi$: for hour $8$ it is $\frac{2\pi \cdot 8}{24} = \frac{2\pi}{3}$, not $\frac{8}{24}$.

**Answer:** $1$, $2$, $-\frac{1}{2}$.
