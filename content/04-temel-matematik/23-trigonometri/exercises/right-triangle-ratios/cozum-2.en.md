**Idea:** $6$ and $8$ are twice $3$ and $4$. The triangle is the $3$-$4$-$5$ triangle scaled up by two; the ratios are not affected by scaling.

**Step 1 — The hypotenuse.** $2 \cdot 5 = 10$.

**Step 2 — Ratios in the small triangle.** Facing $A$ is $4$, next to it $3$, hypotenuse $5$: $\sin A = \frac{4}{5}$.

**Step 3 — The tangent.** $\frac{4}{3}$.

**Why the same result?** Similar triangles have equal side ratios; $\frac{8}{10}$ and $\frac{4}{5}$ are the same fraction. This is why trigonometric ratios depend only on the angle.

**Answer:** $10$, $\frac{4}{5}$ and $\frac{4}{3}$.
