A worked example for each method in the lesson. Try the question yourself first, then read the solution.

## 1. Evaluating

**Question:** What is $a^2 - 4ab + b$ for $a = -3$, $b = 2$?

$$
(-3)^2 - 4(-3)(2) + 2 = 9 + 24 + 2 = 35
$$

## 2. Adding like terms

**Question:** Simplify $5x^2 - 3x + 2 - 2x^2 + 7x - 9$.

The $x^2$ terms: $5 - 2 = 3$. The $x$ terms: $-3 + 7 = 4$. The constants:
$2 - 9 = -7$. The result is $3x^2 + 4x - 7$.

## 3. Distributing a minus

**Question:** Simplify $4(x + 2) - (3x - 5)$.

$$
4x + 8 - 3x + 5 = x + 13
$$

## 4. A product of monomials

**Question:** What is $(-3a^2 b)(2ab^3)$?

Coefficients $-6$; $a^{2+1} = a^3$; $b^{1+3} = b^4$: $-6a^3 b^4$.

## 5. Multiplying binomials

**Question:** Expand $(3x - 2)(x + 5)$.

$$
3x^2 + 15x - 2x - 10 = 3x^2 + 13x - 10
$$

## 6. A perfect square

**Question:** Expand $(2x + 3)^2$.

$a = 2x$, $b = 3$: $4x^2 + 2 \cdot 2x \cdot 3 + 9 = 4x^2 + 12x + 9$.

## 7. A difference of squares

**Question:** What is $(5 - y)(5 + y)$?

$25 - y^2$.

## 8. Mental arithmetic

**Question:** What are $98^2$ and $61 \cdot 59$?

$98^2 = (100 - 2)^2 = 10\,000 - 400 + 4 = 9\,604$.
$61 \cdot 59 = (60 + 1)(60 - 1) = 3\,600 - 1 = 3\,599$.
