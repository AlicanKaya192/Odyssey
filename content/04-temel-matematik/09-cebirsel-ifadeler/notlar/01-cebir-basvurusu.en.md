## Terms

| Idea | Example ($4x^3 - x + 9$) |
|---|---|
| Terms | $4x^3$, $-x$, $9$ |
| Coefficients | $4$, $-1$ |
| Constant term | $9$ |
| Degree | $3$ |

Like terms: same letters and exponents. $5x^2$ and $-2x^2$ are like terms;
$5x^2$ and $5x$ are not.

## Basic operations

| Operation | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Adding like terms | add the coefficients | $4a - 7a = -3a$ |
| Distributing | $k(a + b) = ka + kb$ | $-2(x - 3) = -2x + 6$ |
| Multiplying monomials | multiply coefficients, add exponents | $(2x^3)(5x) = 10x^4$ |
| Multiplying binomials | every term by every term | $(x + 1)(x - 5) = x^2 - 4x - 5$ |

## Identities

$$
\begin{aligned}
(a + b)^2 &= a^2 + 2ab + b^2 \\
(a - b)^2 &= a^2 - 2ab + b^2 \\
(a + b)(a - b) &= a^2 - b^2 \\
(a + b)^3 &= a^3 + 3a^2 b + 3ab^2 + b^3
\end{aligned}
$$

## Practical tips

- Brackets when substituting: for $x = -2$, $-x^2 = -(-2)^2 = -4$.
- After opening brackets, line up like terms before adding.
- Do not forget the middle term $2ab$ in $(a + b)^2$; remember the area picture.
- If you are unsure about an identity, test it with two numbers; if it fails, it is wrong.
- In your head: write $n^2$ around a round number: $102^2 = (100 + 2)^2 = 10\,404$.
