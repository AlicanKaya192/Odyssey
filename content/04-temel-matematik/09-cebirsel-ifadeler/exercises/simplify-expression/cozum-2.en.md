**Idea:** Without expanding everything, work out what each piece contributes to the $x$ term and to the constant. Since the result is $ax + b$, we can find $a$ and $b$ as two separate sums.

**Step 1 — Contributions.**

| Piece | to $x$ | to the constant |
|---|---|---|
| $3(2x - 5)$ | $3 \cdot 2 = 6$ | $3 \cdot (-5) = -15$ |
| $-2(x - 4)$ | $-2 \cdot 1 = -2$ | $-2 \cdot (-4) = 8$ |
| $+x$ | $1$ | $0$ |

**Step 2 — Add.** $a = 6 - 2 + 1 = 5$; $b = -15 + 8 + 0 = -7$.

**Why the same result?** Opening brackets and collecting like terms is the same as adding the $x$ coefficients and the constants coming from each piece. The table shows these sums in separate columns, where sign slips are easier to spot.

**Answer:** $5$ and $-7$.
