**What is asked?** Bringing an expression with brackets to its simplest linear form.

**Idea:** First open the brackets with the distributive property; then collect like terms (the $x$ terms together, the numbers together).

**Step 1 — The first bracket.**

$$
3(2x - 5) = 6x - 15
$$

**Step 2 — The second bracket.** The factor in front is $-2$; both terms change sign:

$$
-2(x - 4) = -2x + 8
$$

**Step 3 — Write it all out.**

$$
6x - 15 - 2x + 8 + x
$$

**Step 4 — Like terms.** The $x$ terms: $6 - 2 + 1 = 5$. The constants: $-15 + 8 = -7$.

$$
5x - 7
$$

**Check:** Put $x = 1$: $3(2 \cdot 1 - 5) = 3 \cdot (-3) = -9$, $-2(1 - 4) = -2 \cdot (-3) = +6$, $+x = 1$: total $-2$; and $5 \cdot 1 - 7 = -2$ ✓.

**Watch out:** Writing $-2(x - 4) = -2x - 8$ is the most common slip; minus times minus is plus.

**Answer:** $a = 5$, $b = -7$.
