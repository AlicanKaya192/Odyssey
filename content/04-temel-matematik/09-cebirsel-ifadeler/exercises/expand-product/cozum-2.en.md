**Idea:** If we know where each coefficient of $(px + q)(rx + s)$ comes from, we can read them off without expanding: the $x^2$ coefficient is $pr$, the constant $qs$, the middle one $ps + qr$.

**Step 1 — The values.** $p = 3$, $q = -1$, $r = 2$, $s = 5$.

**Step 2 — Read off.**

$$
\begin{aligned}
a &= pr = 3 \cdot 2 = 6 \\
b &= ps + qr = 3 \cdot 5 + (-1) \cdot 2 = 13 \\
c &= qs = (-1) \cdot 5 = -5
\end{aligned}
$$

**Why the same result?** This is doing the four products once with general letters and keeping the result as a rule: $(px + q)(rx + s) = prx^2 + (ps + qr)x + qs$. In the Factoring section we will use this pattern in reverse.

**Answer:** $6$, $13$ and $-5$.
