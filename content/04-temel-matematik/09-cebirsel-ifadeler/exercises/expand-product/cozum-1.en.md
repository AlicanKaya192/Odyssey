**What is asked?** Expanding the product of two binomials.

**Idea:** Every term of the first bracket multiplies every term of the second: $2 \cdot 2 = 4$ products. Then like terms combine.

**Step 1 — Four products.**

$$
\begin{aligned}
3x \cdot 2x &= 6x^2 \\
3x \cdot 5 &= 15x \\
-1 \cdot 2x &= -2x \\
-1 \cdot 5 &= -5
\end{aligned}
$$

**Step 2 — Add.**

$$
6x^2 + 15x - 2x - 5 = 6x^2 + 13x - 5
$$

**Check:** $x = 1$: $(3 - 1)(2 + 5) = 2 \cdot 7 = 14$ and $6 + 13 - 5 = 14$ ✓.

**Watch out:** Multiplying only the "firsts" and "lasts" ($6x^2 - 5$) and forgetting the two middle products is a common slip.

**Answer:** $a = 6$, $b = 13$, $c = -5$.
