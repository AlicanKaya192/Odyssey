**Idea:** First expand with letters: $(y - wx)^2 = x^2 w^2 - 2xy\,w + y^2$. Then read off the coefficients with numbers. This route shows that the same pattern holds for every data point.

**Step 1 — The general expansion.**

$$
\begin{aligned}
(y - wx)^2 &= y^2 - 2wxy + w^2 x^2 \\
&= x^2 \cdot w^2 - 2xy \cdot w + y^2
\end{aligned}
$$

**Step 2 — The coefficients.**

$$
\begin{aligned}
a &= x^2 = 2^2 = 4 \\
b &= -2xy = -2 \cdot 2 \cdot 7 = -28 \\
c &= y^2 = 7^2 = 49
\end{aligned}
$$

**Why the same result?** Both routes use the same identity; one put in the numbers first, the other last. The advantage of the general form: the coefficient of $w^2$ is always $x^2 \ge 0$, so the bowl always opens upwards and has a bottom.

**Answer:** $4$, $-28$ and $49$.
