**Idea:** Multiply directly with the distributive property, without identities. We see that identities are really ready-made results of distributing.

**Step 1 — $103 \cdot 103$.** Split the second $103$ as $100 + 3$:

$$
103 \cdot 100 + 103 \cdot 3 = 10\,300 + 309 = 10\,609
$$

**Step 2 — $47 \cdot 53$.** $53 = 50 + 3$:

$$
47 \cdot 50 + 47 \cdot 3 = 2\,350 + 141 = 2\,491
$$

**Why the same result?** $(a + b)^2 = a^2 + 2ab + b^2$ is what you get by distributing $(a + b)(a + b)$. The identity does this expansion once and keeps it as a pattern; here we redid the same expansion with numbers.

**Answer:** $10\,609$ and $2\,491$.
