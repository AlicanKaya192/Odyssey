**What is asked?** Doing two products in your head with the help of identities.

**Idea:** Write the number as a round number plus or minus a small one; the identity breaks the hard product into easy pieces.

**Step 1 — $103^2$.** $a = 100$, $b = 3$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
(100 + 3)^2 &= 100^2 + 2 \cdot 100 \cdot 3 + 3^2 \\
&= 10\,000 + 600 + 9 \\
&= 10\,609
\end{aligned}
$$

**Step 2 — $47 \cdot 53$.** Both are $3$ away from $50$:

$$
(50 - 3)(50 + 3) = 50^2 - 3^2 = 2\,500 - 9 = 2\,491
$$

**Check:** Rough estimates: $103^2 \approx 100^2 = 10\,000$, a bit more ✓. $47 \cdot 53 \approx 50 \cdot 50 = 2\,500$, a bit less ✓ (a difference of squares always takes a little off).

**Watch out:** Writing $103^2 = 10\,000 + 9$ and forgetting the middle $2ab = 600$ means skipping the two rectangles in the area picture.

**Answer:** $10\,609$ and $2\,491$.
