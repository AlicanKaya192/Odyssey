A worked example for each method in the lesson. Try the question yourself first, then read the solution.

## 1. A perfect-square root

**Question:** What are $\sqrt{144}$ and $\sqrt{0.25}$?

$12^2 = 144$, so $\sqrt{144} = 12$. $0.5^2 = 0.25$, so $\sqrt{0.25} = 0.5$.

## 2. A root in between

**Question:** Between which two whole numbers is $\sqrt{60}$?

$7^2 = 49 < 60 < 64 = 8^2$: between $7$ and $8$, nearer $8$ ($\approx 7.75$).

## 3. Simplifying

**Question:** Simplify $\sqrt{98}$.

$98 = 49 \cdot 2$: $\sqrt{98} = 7\sqrt{2}$.

## 4. Adding like roots

**Question:** What is $\sqrt{12} + \sqrt{27} - \sqrt{3}$?

$\sqrt{12} = 2\sqrt{3}$, $\sqrt{27} = 3\sqrt{3}$:

$$
2\sqrt{3} + 3\sqrt{3} - \sqrt{3} = 4\sqrt{3}
$$

## 5. Multiplying roots

**Question:** What is $\sqrt{6} \cdot \sqrt{15}$?

$\sqrt{90} = \sqrt{9 \cdot 10} = 3\sqrt{10}$.

## 6. Clearing the denominator

**Question:** Simplify $\dfrac{10}{\sqrt{5}}$.

$$
\frac{10\sqrt{5}}{5} = 2\sqrt{5}
$$

## 7. Fractional exponents

**Question:** What are $27^{2/3}$ and $32^{-1/5}$?

$\sqrt[3]{27} = 3$, $3^2 = 9$. $\sqrt[5]{32} = 2$, $2^{-1} = \dfrac{1}{2}$.

## 8. Distance

**Question:** What is the distance between the points $(1, 2)$ and $(7, 10)$?

The differences are $6$ and $8$: $\sqrt{6^2 + 8^2} = \sqrt{36 + 64} = \sqrt{100} = 10$.
