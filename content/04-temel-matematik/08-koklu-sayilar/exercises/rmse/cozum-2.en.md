**Idea:** Bring $\sqrt{4.5}$ to an exact form with the root rules instead of a decimal estimate; then compute using the known $\sqrt{2} \approx 1.414$.

**Step 1 — Write the MSE as a fraction.** $4.5 = \dfrac{9}{2}$.

**Step 2 — The root of a quotient.**

$$
\sqrt{\frac{9}{2}} = \frac{\sqrt{9}}{\sqrt{2}} = \frac{3}{\sqrt{2}}
$$

**Step 3 — Clear the denominator.**

$$
\frac{3}{\sqrt{2}} = \frac{3\sqrt{2}}{2}
$$

**Step 4 — Convert to a number.**

$$
\frac{3 \cdot 1.414}{2} = \frac{4.242}{2} = 2.121 \approx 2.12
$$

**Why the same result?** The first route narrowed the root down by trial; this one found the exact value ($\frac{3\sqrt{2}}{2}$) with the root rules and approximated only at the end. The exact value can be worked out to as many places as needed.

**Answer:** $4.5$ and $2.12$.
