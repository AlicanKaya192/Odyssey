**What is asked?** Which whole numbers a root that does not come out exactly lies between, and its value to two places.

**Idea:** As a root grows, so does its square. Trap $\sqrt{200}$ between two numbers whose squares are below and above $200$, then narrow the range place by place.

**Step 1 — Whole numbers.** $14^2 = 196 < 200 < 225 = 15^2$:

$$
14 < \sqrt{200} < 15
$$

The largest whole number below $\sqrt{200}$ is $14$.

**Step 2 — Tenths.** $200$ is very close to $196$, so the root is near $14$. $14.1^2 = 198.81 < 200 < 201.64 = 14.2^2$: between $14.1$ and $14.2$.

**Step 3 — Hundredths.** $14.14^2 = 199.939\,6 < 200 < 200.222\,5 = 14.15^2$: between $14.14$ and $14.15$. $200$ is much nearer $199.94$:

$$
\sqrt{200} \approx 14.14
$$

**Check:** $14.14^2 \approx 199.94$, $0.06$ away from $200$ ✓.

**Answer:** $14$; $\sqrt{200} \approx 14.14$.
