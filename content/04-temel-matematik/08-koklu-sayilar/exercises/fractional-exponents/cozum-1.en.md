**What is asked?** The values of fractional and negative fractional exponents.

**Idea:** In a fractional exponent the denominator is the degree of the root and the numerator the power: $a^{m/n} = (\sqrt[n]{a})^m$. The minus sign means taking the reciprocal.

**Step 1 — $8^{2/3}$.** Denominator $3$: cube root; numerator $2$: square.

$$
8^{2/3} = \left(\sqrt[3]{8}\right)^2 = 2^2 = 4
$$

**Step 2 — $27^{1/3}$.** A cube root: since $3^3 = 27$, $27^{1/3} = 3$.

**Step 3 — Add.** $4 + 3 = 7$.

**Step 4 — $16^{-1/2}$.** First turn the minus into a reciprocal, then the square root:

$$
16^{-1/2} = \frac{1}{16^{1/2}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{16}} = \frac{1}{4}
$$

**Watch out:** Working out $8^{2/3}$ as $8 \cdot \frac{2}{3} = \frac{16}{3}$ treats the exponent as a factor. Writing $16^{-1/2}$ as $-4$ treats a negative exponent as a negative number.

**Answer:** $7$ and $\dfrac{1}{4}$.
