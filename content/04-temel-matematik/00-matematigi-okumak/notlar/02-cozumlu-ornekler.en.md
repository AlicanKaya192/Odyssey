A worked example for each topic in the lesson. Try the question yourself first, then read the solution.

## 1. Which set?

**Question:** Which sets do $-4$, $0.5$, $\sqrt{16}$ and $\sqrt{3}$ belong to?

- $-4$: an integer ($\in \mathbb{Z}$), so also rational and real; not a natural number ($\notin \mathbb{N}$).
- $0.5 = \tfrac{1}{2}$: rational and real; not an integer.
- $\sqrt{16} = 4$: a natural number! Do not be fooled by the root sign; calculate first.
- $\sqrt{3} \approx 1.732\dots$: real but irrational.

## 2. Terms and coefficients

**Question:** In $2y^3 - y + 9 - 4y^2$, what are the coefficients of $y$ and $y^2$, and the constant term?

Write each term with its sign: $2y^3$, $-y$, $+9$, $-4y^2$.

- Coefficient of $y$: $-y = -1 \cdot y$, so $-1$.
- Coefficient of $y^2$: $-4$.
- Constant term: $9$.

The order of the terms does not matter; sorted by exponent it is $2y^3 - 4y^2 - y + 9$.

## 3. Substituting a value

**Question:** For $a = -2$, $b = 3$, what is $a^2 - 3ab + b$?

Put the negative number in brackets:

$$
\begin{aligned}
(-2)^2 - 3 \cdot (-2) \cdot 3 + 3 &= 4 - (-18) + 3 \\
&= 4 + 18 + 3 = 25
\end{aligned}
$$

## 4. From words to symbols

**Question:** Write these sentences in symbols.

| Sentence | Symbols |
|---|---|
| 5 less than 3 times a number | $3x - 5$ |
| 3 times (a number minus 5) | $3(x - 5)$ |
| Half the sum of two numbers | $\frac{a + b}{2}$ |
| A number's square is 2 more than the number | $x^2 = x + 2$ |
| $x$ is at least $18$ | $x \ge 18$ |

Mind the difference between the first two rows: the first multiplies then
subtracts; the second subtracts first, so it needs brackets.

## 5. Subscripts

**Question:** $x_1 = 4$, $x_2 = -1$, $x_3 = 6$. What is $x_1 - 2x_2 + x_3$?

$x_2$ means "the second $x$"; its value is $-1$:

$$
4 - 2 \cdot (-1) + 6 = 4 + 2 + 6 = 12
$$

## 6. The sum symbol

**Question:** What is $\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{3} (2i + 1)$?

Put $1$, $2$, $3$ in place of $i$ in turn and add:

$$
(2 \cdot 1 + 1) + (2 \cdot 2 + 1) + (2 \cdot 3 + 1) = 3 + 5 + 7 = 15
$$

## 7. Function notation

**Question:** If $f(x) = x^2 - 2x$, what are $f(-1)$ and $f(0)$?

$$
\begin{aligned}
f(-1) &= (-1)^2 - 2 \cdot (-1) = 1 + 2 = 3 \\
f(0) &= 0^2 - 2 \cdot 0 = 0
\end{aligned}
$$

## 8. A chain inequality

**Question:** Which integers satisfy $-3 \le x < 2$?

$\le$ includes the end point, $<$ does not: $-3, -2, -1, 0, 1$. Five
integers; $2$ is not included.
