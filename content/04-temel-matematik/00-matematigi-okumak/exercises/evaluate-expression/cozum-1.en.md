**What is asked?** Which number the expression equals when $-3$ is written in place of the letter.

**Idea:** Write $(-3)$ wherever $x$ appears. Putting a negative number **in brackets** is essential; otherwise the sign gets mixed into the wrong operation. Then do the operations in order: exponent first, then multiplication, and finally addition and subtraction.

**Step 1 — Substitute.**

$$
2 \cdot (-3)^2 - 3 \cdot (-3) + 4
$$

**Step 2 — The exponent.** $(-3)^2 = (-3) \cdot (-3) = 9$ (minus times minus is plus):

$$
2 \cdot 9 - 3 \cdot (-3) + 4
$$

**Step 3 — The multiplications.** $2 \cdot 9 = 18$ and $3 \cdot (-3) = -9$:

$$
18 - (-9) + 4
$$

**Step 4 — Addition and subtraction.** Subtracting a negative number means adding it: $18 - (-9) = 18 + 9$.

$$
18 + 9 + 4 = 31
$$

**Watch out:** Writing $-3^2$ without brackets reads as $-(3^2) = -9$, and gives a wrong number such as $2 \cdot (-9) + 9 + 4 = -5$.

**Answer:** $31$.
