A model's prediction formula:

$$
\hat{y} = w_1 x_1 + w_2 x_2 + b
$$

The weights are $w_1 = 0.5$, $w_2 = -2$ and the constant $b = 10$. For one example, $x_1 = 80$ and $x_2 = 6$.

1. What is the model's prediction $\hat{y}$?
2. If the true value of this example is $y = 35$, what is the squared error $(y - \hat{y})^2$?
