**What is asked?** Recognising the parts of an expression: which number stands in front of which letter, and which term has no letter.

**Idea:** Writing subtraction as "adding a negative number" makes each term's sign belong to the term. The coefficient is the number multiplying the letter in a term (with its sign).

**Step 1 — Write everything as addition.**

$$
5x^3 + (-1)x^2 + 7 + (-2)x
$$

The coefficient of $-x^2$ is $-1$: a lone letter has an invisible $1$ in front of it.

**Step 2 — Read the terms one by one.**

| Term | Coefficient | Letter part |
|---|---|---|
| $5x^3$ | $5$ | $x^3$ |
| $-x^2$ | $-1$ | $x^2$ |
| $7$ | (constant) | none |
| $-2x$ | $-2$ | $x$ |

**Step 3 — Answer the questions.** The coefficient of $x$ is $-2$, of $x^2$ is $-1$, and the constant term is $7$.

**Watch out:** The two most common slips: writing the coefficient of $x$ as $2$ without its sign, and missing the coefficient of $x^2$ and taking it to be $0$.

**Answer:** $-2$, $-1$, $7$.
