**What is asked?** Reading two notations: the sum symbol and the product of subscripted variables.

**Idea:** A subscript is only a name: $x_3$ means "the third measurement", with value $5$. $\Sigma$ says "add all these terms". We write the symbols out and put the numbers in.

**Step 1 — Write the sum out.** $i$ from $1$ to $4$:

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{4} x_i = x_1 + x_2 + x_3 + x_4
$$

**Step 2 — Put in the values.**

$$
\begin{aligned}
4 + (-2) + 5 + 1 &= 4 - 2 + 5 + 1 \\
&= 8
\end{aligned}
$$

**Step 3 — The second expression.** $x_2 x_3$ is a product (written side by side):

$$
\begin{aligned}
x_2 x_3 - x_1 &= (-2) \cdot 5 - 4 \\
&= -10 - 4 = -14
\end{aligned}
$$

**Watch out:** Reading $x_2$ as "$x$ squared" or "2 times $x$" is the most common slip. A subscript does no calculation.

**Answer:** $8$ and $-14$.
