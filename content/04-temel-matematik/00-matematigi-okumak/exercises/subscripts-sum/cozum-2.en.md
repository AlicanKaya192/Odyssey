**Idea:** Since order does not matter in addition, adding positive and negative numbers in separate groups and then combining them reduces sign errors.

**Step 1 — The positive measurements.** $x_1 + x_3 + x_4 = 4 + 5 + 1 = 10$.

**Step 2 — The negative measurements.** $x_2 = -2$.

**Step 3 — Combine.**

$$
10 + (-2) = 8
$$

**Step 4 — The sign rule in the product.** In $x_2 x_3$ a negative and a positive number are multiplied: the result is negative, with size $2 \cdot 5 = 10$. So $x_2 x_3 = -10$ and

$$
-10 - x_1 = -10 - 4 = -14
$$

**Reading the result:** The sum $8$ also tells us the mean of the four measurements is $8 / 4 = 2$. In the statistics sections the mean will be written exactly like this: $\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i$.

**Answer:** $8$ and $-14$.
