**First term.** $\log_3 81 = y$ means $3^y = 81$.

$$
3^1 = 3,\quad 3^2 = 9,\quad 3^3 = 27,\quad 3^4 = 81
\quad\Rightarrow\quad \log_3 81 = 4
$$

**Second term.** $\log_2 \frac{1}{8} = y$ means $2^y = \frac{1}{8}$. Since $2^3 = 8$ and a negative exponent means "one over":

$$
2^{-3} = \frac{1}{2^3} = \frac{1}{8}
\quad\Rightarrow\quad \log_2 \frac{1}{8} = -3
$$

**Add them:**

$$
4 + (-3) = 1
$$

**Answer: 1**
