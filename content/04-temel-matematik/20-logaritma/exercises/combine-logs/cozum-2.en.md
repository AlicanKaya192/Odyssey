The logarithm of the base itself is 1: $\log_6 6 = 1$. The expression becomes:

$$
\log_6 12 + \log_6 18 - 1
$$

Combine the first two terms with the product rule: $\log_6 216$. Since $216 = 6 \cdot 6 \cdot 6 = 6^3$, $\log_6 216 = 3$.

$$
3 - 1 = 2
$$

Both paths end in the same place; the first reduces everything to a single logarithm, the second turns a known value into a number early.

**Answer: 2**
