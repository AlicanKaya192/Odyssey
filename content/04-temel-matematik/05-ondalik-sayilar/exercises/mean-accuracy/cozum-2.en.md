**Idea:** The numbers are around $0.9$. Write how far each one is from $0.9$, and add the mean of these deviations to $0.9$. Working with small numbers lowers the risk of slips.

**Step 1 — The deviations (in thousandths).**

$$
\begin{aligned}
0.870 - 0.9 &= -0.030 \\
0.912 - 0.9 &= +0.012 \\
0.900 - 0.9 &= 0 \\
0.885 - 0.9 &= -0.015 \\
0.843 - 0.9 &= -0.057
\end{aligned}
$$

**Step 2 — The sum of the deviations.** $-30 + 12 + 0 - 15 - 57 = -90$ thousandths, that is, $-0.090$.

**Step 3 — The mean deviation.** $-0.090 \div 5 = -0.018$.

**Step 4 — The mean.**

$$
0.9 + (-0.018) = 0.882
$$

Rounded to two places, $0.88$.

**Why the same result?** Subtracting the same $0.9$ from each value and adding it back does not change the total; we took away five $0.9$s and added one $0.9$ to the mean. This trick is a practical way of averaging in your head when the numbers are close together.

**Answer:** $0.882$ and $0.88$.
