**What is asked?** The mean of five decimals, and its rounded form.

**Idea:** Mean = sum $\div$ number of values. Line up the points when adding. Leave rounding to the end.

**Step 1 — Equalise the places.**

$$
0.870 \quad 0.912 \quad 0.900 \quad 0.885 \quad 0.843
$$

**Step 2 — Add.** Think in thousandths: $870 + 912 + 900 + 885 + 843 = 4\,410$, so

$$
0.870 + 0.912 + 0.900 + 0.885 + 0.843 = 4.410
$$

**Step 3 — Divide by $5$.**

$$
4.41 \div 5 = 0.882
$$

($4.41 \cdot 2 = 8.82$, then $\div 10$.)

**Step 4 — Round to two places.** The third digit is $2 < 5$: round down. $0.88$.

**Reading the result:** The mean must lie between the lowest ($0.843$) and the highest ($0.912$) ✓. As a percentage the model is right $88.2\%$ of the time on average.

**Watch out:** Adding without lining up the points is the most common slip: if you take $0.9$ to be $9$ thousandths, the sum comes out as $3.519$ instead of $4.41$. That is why we first equalised everything to three places.

**Answer:** $0.882$; rounded, $0.88$.
