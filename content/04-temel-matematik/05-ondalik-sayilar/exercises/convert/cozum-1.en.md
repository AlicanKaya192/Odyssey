**What is asked?** Turning a fraction into a decimal and a decimal into a fraction.

**Idea:** A fraction is a division; dividing the numerator by the denominator gives the decimal. A decimal is place value; the number of places after the point tells us the denominator.

**Step 1 — $7 \div 8$, long division.** $7 < 8$, so the whole part is $0$; put the point and turn $7$ into $70$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
70 &= 8 \cdot 8 + 6 \\
60 &= 8 \cdot 7 + 4 \\
40 &= 8 \cdot 5 + 0
\end{aligned}
$$

The remainder is $0$, the division ends. The digits of the quotient are $8, 7, 5$: $\dfrac{7}{8} = 0.875$.

**Step 2 — Turn $0.36$ into a fraction.** Two places, denominator $100$:

$$
0.36 = \frac{36}{100}
$$

**Step 3 — Simplify.** $36 = 2^2 \cdot 3^2$, $100 = 2^2 \cdot 5^2$; the GCD is $4$:

$$
\frac{36}{100} = \frac{9}{25}
$$

**Check:** $0.875 \cdot 8 = 7$ ✓. $9 \div 25 = 0.36$ ✓.

**Answer:** $0.875$ and $\dfrac{9}{25}$.
