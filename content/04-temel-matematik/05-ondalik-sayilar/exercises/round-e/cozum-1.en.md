**What is asked?** The same number rounded to different places.

**Idea:** In rounding we look only at the digit **just to the right** of the required place: $5$ or more, round up; less, round down. Each rounding is done from the **original number**, not from the previous result.

**Step 1 — Write out the places.**

$$
2.\underset{1}{7}\,\underset{2}{1}\,\underset{3}{8}\,\underset{4}{2}\,\underset{5}{8}\,\underset{6}{1}\dots
$$

**Step 2 — Two places.** The second digit is $1$ and the one after it (the third) is $8 \ge 5$: round up.

$$
2.71 \to 2.72
$$

**Step 3 — Three places.** The third digit is $8$ and the one after it (the fourth) is $2 < 5$: round down.

$$
2.718
$$

**Step 4 — Four places.** The fourth digit is $2$ and the one after it (the fifth) is $8 \ge 5$: round up.

$$
2.7182 \to 2.7183
$$

**Watch out:** Rounding the three-place result ($2.718$) again to two places also gives $2.72$ here, but that does not always work (think of $2.449$). Go back to the original number every time.

**Answer:** $2.72$; $2.718$; $2.7183$.
