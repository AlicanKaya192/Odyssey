**What is asked?** An expression with multiplication, division and subtraction of decimals.

**Idea:** The order of operations is the same with decimals: multiplication and division first, then subtraction. In multiplication the numbers of places add up; in division both points are moved.

**Step 1 — Multiplication: $3.75 \cdot 0.4$.** Ignore the points and multiply: $375 \cdot 4 = 1\,500$. The places add up to $2 + 1 = 3$:

$$
3.75 \cdot 0.4 = 1.500 = 1.5
$$

**Step 2 — Division: $1.2 \div 0.05$.** To make the divisor whole, move both points two places right:

$$
1.2 \div 0.05 = 120 \div 5 = 24
$$

**Step 3 — Subtraction.**

$$
1.5 - 24 = -22.5
$$

**Check:** Roughly: $4 \cdot 0.4 = 1.6$ and $1 \div 0.05 = 20$; $1.6 - 20 \approx -18$. The result has the same size and is negative ✓.

**Watch out:** Writing $1.2 \div 0.05 = 0.24$ is a common slip; dividing by a number less than $1$ makes the result **larger**.

**Answer:** $-22.5$.
