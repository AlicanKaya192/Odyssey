**Idea:** Converting the decimals to fractions removes the need for the point rules; fraction multiplication and division are enough.

**Step 1 — Convert.**

$$
3.75 = \frac{15}{4}, \qquad 0.4 = \frac{2}{5}, \qquad 1.2 = \frac{6}{5}, \qquad 0.05 = \frac{1}{20}
$$

**Step 2 — Multiplication.**

$$
\frac{15}{4} \cdot \frac{2}{5} = \frac{30}{20} = \frac{3}{2}
$$

**Step 3 — Division.** Multiply by the reciprocal of the divisor:

$$
\frac{6}{5} \div \frac{1}{20} = \frac{6}{5} \cdot 20 = 24
$$

**Step 4 — Subtraction.**

$$
\frac{3}{2} - 24 = \frac{3}{2} - \frac{48}{2} = -\frac{45}{2} = -22.5
$$

**Why the same result?** The decimal rules come from the fraction rules: adding places is multiplying tenths; moving the point is multiplying top and bottom by $10$. We did the same calculation in two different notations.

**Answer:** $-22.5$.
