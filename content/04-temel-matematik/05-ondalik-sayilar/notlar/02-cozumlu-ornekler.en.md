A worked example for each method in the lesson. Try the question yourself first, then read the solution.

## 1. Decimal to fraction

**Question:** Write $0.048$ as a fraction in simplest form.

Three places, so the denominator is $1\,000$:

$$
0.048 = \frac{48}{1\,000} = \frac{6}{125}
$$

(We divided $48$ and $1\,000$ by their GCD, $8$.)

## 2. Fraction to decimal

**Question:** Write $\dfrac{11}{20}$ as a decimal.

Expand the denominator to $100$: $\dfrac{11}{20} = \dfrac{55}{100} = 0.55$.

## 3. A repeating decimal

**Question:** Write $0.\overline{27}$ as a fraction.

Two digits repeat, so multiply by $100$:

$$
100x - x = 27.\overline{27} - 0.\overline{27} = 27 \quad\Rightarrow\quad x = \frac{27}{99} = \frac{3}{11}
$$

## 4. Ordering

**Question:** Put $0.7$, $0.07$, $0.707$, $0.77$ in order from smallest
to largest.

Equalise to three places: $0.700$, $0.070$, $0.707$, $0.770$.

$$
0.07 < 0.7 < 0.707 < 0.77
$$

## 5. Multiplication

**Question:** What is $2.4 \cdot 0.15$?

$24 \cdot 15 = 360$; places $1 + 2 = 3$: $0.360 = 0.36$.

Check: $2.4 \cdot 0.15 \approx 2 \cdot 0.15 = 0.3$. Reasonable.

## 6. Division

**Question:** What is $4.56 \div 0.12$?

Move both points two places: $456 \div 12 = 38$.

## 7. Rounding

**Question:** Round $\pi = 3.141\,592\dots$ to one, two and four places.

- One place: the next digit is $4$ → $3.1$
- Two places: the next digit is $1$ → $3.14$
- Four places: the next digit is $9$ → $3.141\,6$

## 8. Shopping

**Question:** You buy three items costing $12.45$, $8.90$ and $3.29$ and
pay with $50$. How much change do you get?

Total: $12.45 + 8.90 + 3.29 = 24.64$. Change: $50.00 - 24.64 = 25.36$.
