**What is asked?** The distance between two integers and the point exactly halfway.

**Idea:** The distance between two numbers is the absolute value of their difference: $|a - b|$. The midpoint is half that distance to the right of the left-hand point.

**Step 1 — The distance.**

$$
\lvert -7 - 5 \rvert = \lvert -7 + (-5) \rvert = \lvert -12 \rvert = 12
$$

**Step 2 — Half the way.** Half the distance is $12 \div 2 = 6$.

**Step 3 — The midpoint.** $6$ units to the right of $A$:

$$
-7 + 6 = -1
$$

**Check:** From $-1$ to $A$: $\lvert -1 - (-7) \rvert = |6| = 6$; to $B$: $|5 - (-1)| = 6$. They are equal ✓.

**Watch out:** Computing the distance as $5 - 7 = -2$ or $|5| - \lvert -7 \rvert$ is wrong; $A$ is left of zero and $B$ right of it, so the distance is the sum of the two parts: $7 + 5$.

**Answer:** $12$ units and $-1$.
