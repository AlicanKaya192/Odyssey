A model predicted prices for four houses (in thousands):

| House | True $y$ | Prediction $\hat{y}$ |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | $10$ | $13$ |
| 2 | $7$ | $6$ |
| 3 | $12$ | $9$ |
| 4 | $5$ | $8$ |

The error for each house is $e = y - \hat{y}$.

1. What is the sum of the errors?
2. What is the sum of the absolute values of the errors?
3. What is the sum of the squares of the errors?
