**What is asked?** The value of a sum mixing positive and negative numbers.

**Idea:** Turn the subtractions into additions with the rule $a - b = a + (-b)$. Then only the two addition rules remain: same sign → add; different signs → subtract and take the sign of the larger.

**Step 1 — Turn into addition.**

$$
-7 + 12 + 5 + (-20)
$$

$-(-5)$ means "add the opposite of $-5$", that is, $+5$.

**Step 2 — Left to right.** $-7 + 12$: the signs differ, $12 - 7 = 5$, and the larger, $12$, is positive. Then $5 + 5 = 10$. In the last step the signs differ again: $20 - 10 = 10$, and the larger, $-20$, is negative.

$$
\begin{aligned}
-7 + 12 &= 5 \\
5 + 5 &= 10 \\
10 + (-20) &= -10
\end{aligned}
$$

**Check:** Walk along the number line: from $-7$, $12$ right ($5$), $5$ right ($10$), $20$ left ($-10$).

**Watch out:** Leaving $-(-5)$ as $-5$ is the most common slip; then the result comes out as $-20$.

**Answer:** $-10$.
