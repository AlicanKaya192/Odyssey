**What is asked?** An expression mixing multiplication, division and addition with negative numbers.

**Idea:** The order of operations does not change: multiplication and division first, then addition and subtraction. The sign rule: same signs $+$, different signs $-$.

**Step 1 — The first term: $(-4)(-3)$.** The absolute values give $4 \cdot 3 = 12$; two minuses (same sign), so $+12$.

**Step 2 — The second term: $5 \cdot (-2)$.** $5 \cdot 2 = 10$; different signs, so $-10$. In the expression there is a $-$ in front of this term: $-(-10) = +10$.

**Step 3 — The third term: $(-18) \div (-6)$.** $18 \div 6 = 3$; same signs, $+3$.

**Step 4 — Add.**

$$
12 + 10 + 3 = 25
$$

**Watch out:** The second term has two separate minuses: one inside the multiplication and one in front of the term. Forgetting either gives $5$ ($12 - 10 + 3$).

**Answer:** $25$.
