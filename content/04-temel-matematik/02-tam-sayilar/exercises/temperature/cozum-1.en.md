**What is asked?** The temperature reached after the rises and falls, and the temperature range of the day.

**Idea:** Write a rise as a positive number and a fall as a negative one. Final temperature = initial temperature + all the changes.

**Step 1 — The morning rise.** $06{:}00$–$11{:}00$ is $5$ hours at $+4$ per hour:

$$
5 \cdot 4 = 20, \qquad -8 + 20 = 12
$$

At $11{:}00$ it is $12\,°\mathrm{C}$.

**Step 2 — The afternoon fall.** $11{:}00$–$14{:}00$ is $3$ hours at $-3$ per hour:

$$
3 \cdot (-3) = -9, \qquad 12 + (-9) = 3
$$

At $14{:}00$ it is $3\,°\mathrm{C}$.

**Step 3 — Highest and lowest.** The temperature first rose steadily, then fell steadily. The lowest value is at the start ($-8$), the highest at the turning point ($12$). The $3$ at $14{:}00$ is larger than $-8$, so the lowest is still $-8$.

**Step 4 — The difference.**

$$
12 - (-8) = 12 + 8 = 20
$$

**Watch out:** Writing the difference as $12 - 8 = 4$ is a common slip; the lowest temperature is not $8$ but $-8$. On the number line, from $-8$ to $12$ is $8 + 12 = 20$ units.

**Answer:** $3\,°\mathrm{C}$ and $20$ degrees.
