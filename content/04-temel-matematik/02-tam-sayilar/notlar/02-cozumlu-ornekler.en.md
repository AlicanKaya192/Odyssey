A worked example for each method in the lesson. Try the question yourself first, then read the solution.

## 1. Ordering

**Question:** Put $-3, 5, -8, 0, -1$ in order from smallest to largest.

Read the number line from left to right. The most negative is furthest
left:

$$
-8 < -3 < -1 < 0 < 5
$$

## 2. Adding numbers with different signs

**Question:** What is $-15 + 9$?

The absolute values are $15$ and $9$; subtract the smaller from the
larger: $15 - 9 = 6$. The larger absolute value belongs to $-15$, so the
result is negative: $-6$.

## 3. Turning subtraction into addition

**Question:** What is $-4 - (-9) - 7$?

$$
\begin{aligned}
-4 - (-9) - 7 &= -4 + 9 + (-7) \\
&= 5 + (-7) = -2
\end{aligned}
$$

## 4. The sign with many factors

**Question:** What is $(-2) \cdot 3 \cdot (-5) \cdot (-1)$?

The product of the absolute values is $2 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 \cdot 1 = 30$.
There are three minuses (odd): the result is negative, $-30$.

## 5. Powers and brackets

**Question:** What is $(-3)^2 - 3^2 + (-1)^5$?

$(-3)^2 = 9$, $3^2 = 9$, $(-1)^5 = -1$ (odd power):

$$
9 - 9 + (-1) = -1
$$

## 6. A mixed calculation

**Question:** What is $12 \div (-4) - 2 \cdot (-3)^2$?

Exponent first: $(-3)^2 = 9$. Then division and multiplication:
$12 \div (-4) = -3$, $2 \cdot 9 = 18$. Finally:

$$
-3 - 18 = -21
$$

## 7. Distance

**Question:** A diver is $14$ m below sea level and a bird $9$ m above
it. What is the vertical distance between them in metres?

The diver is at $-14$, the bird at $9$. The distance:

$$
|9 - (-14)| = |9 + 14| = 23
$$

## 8. The sum of errors

**Question:** A model's errors are $4, -6, 1, -2, 3$. Find the sum of the
errors and the sum of their absolute values.

Sum: $4 - 6 + 1 - 2 + 3 = 0$. Sum of absolute values: $4 + 6 + 1 + 2 + 3
= 16$. The first would fool us into thinking the model is good; the
second shows the real size of the errors.
