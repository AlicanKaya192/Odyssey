**Idea:** By definition, $f^{-1}(c)$ is the input that makes $f$ equal $c$. No general formula is needed; solve one equation for each value.

**Step 1 — $f(x) = 11$.** $3x - 4 = 11 \Rightarrow 3x = 15 \Rightarrow x = 5$.

**Step 2 — $f(x) = -1$.** $3x - 4 = -1 \Rightarrow 3x = 3 \Rightarrow x = 1$.

**Why the same result?** The general inverse formula is this equation solved once with the letter $c$: $3x - 4 = c \Rightarrow x = \frac{c + 4}{3}$. When a single value is asked, solving the equation directly is shorter.

**Answer:** $5$ and $1$.
