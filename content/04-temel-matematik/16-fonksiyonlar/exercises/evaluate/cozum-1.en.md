**What is asked?** The value of a function at two inputs.

**Idea:** Finding $f(a)$ means putting $a$ in place of every $x$ in the rule. With negative inputs, brackets prevent sign errors.

**Step 1 — $f(-2)$.**

$$
f(-2) = (-2)^2 - 3(-2) + 2 = 4 + 6 + 2 = 12
$$

**Step 2 — $f(3)$.**

$$
f(3) = 9 - 9 + 2 = 2
$$

**Check:** $f(x)$ factors as $(x - 1)(x - 2)$. $f(-2) = (-3)(-4) = 12$ ✓, $f(3) = 2 \cdot 1 = 2$ ✓.

**Watch out:** Writing $-2^2 = -4$ (no brackets) or counting $-3 \cdot (-2)$ as $-6$ gives a wrong $f(-2)$.

**Answer:** $f(-2) = 12$, $f(3) = 2$.
