**What is asked?** The domain of a function with two parts.

**Idea:** For the sum to be defined, both parts must be defined. Find each part's condition, then apply both together ("and").

**Step 1 — The root.** For $\sqrt{x - 3}$: $x - 3 \ge 0$, so $x \ge 3$.

**Step 2 — The fraction.** For $\dfrac{1}{x - 5}$: $x - 5 \neq 0$, so $x \neq 5$.

**Step 3 — Together.** $x \ge 3$ **and** $x \neq 5$. In interval notation, $[3, 5) \cup (5, \infty)$.

**Step 4 — The answers.** The smallest $x$ is $3$ (included: $\sqrt{0} = 0$ is defined). The single excluded value is $5$.

**Check:** $f(3) = 0 + \frac{1}{-2} = -\frac{1}{2}$ is defined ✓. $f(5)$: $\frac{1}{0}$, undefined ✓.

**Watch out:** Saying $x > 3$ for $\sqrt{x - 3}$ wrongly excludes $x = 3$; the inside of a root can be zero.

**Answer:** $3$ and $5$.
