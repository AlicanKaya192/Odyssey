## Basics

$$
\frac{a}{b} = a \div b, \qquad b \ne 0
$$

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Numerator | the top number, how many parts are taken |
| Denominator | the bottom number, how many parts make the whole |
| Proper fraction | numerator < denominator |
| Improper fraction | numerator ≥ denominator |
| Mixed number | $2\tfrac{1}{3} = \dfrac{7}{3}$ |

## Equivalence

$$
\frac{a}{b} = \frac{a \cdot k}{b \cdot k}, \qquad k \ne 0
$$

Simplest form: divide top and bottom by their GCD.

## The four operations

| Operation | Rule |
|---|---|
| Addition | $\dfrac{a}{b} + \dfrac{c}{d} = \dfrac{ad + cb}{bd}$ (or use the LCM as denominator) |
| Subtraction | $\dfrac{a}{b} - \dfrac{c}{d} = \dfrac{ad - cb}{bd}$ |
| Multiplication | $\dfrac{a}{b} \cdot \dfrac{c}{d} = \dfrac{ac}{bd}$ |
| Division | $\dfrac{a}{b} \div \dfrac{c}{d} = \dfrac{a}{b} \cdot \dfrac{d}{c}$ |

## Comparing

$$
\frac{a}{b} > \frac{c}{d} \iff a \cdot d > c \cdot b \qquad (b, d > 0)
$$

## Mixed numbers

| Direction | Do | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Mixed → improper | whole · denominator + numerator | $3\tfrac{2}{5} = \dfrac{17}{5}$ |
| Improper → mixed | divide with remainder | $\dfrac{17}{5} = 3$ remainder $2$ $= 3\tfrac{2}{5}$ |

## Practical tips

- In addition, the LCM of the denominators is the least common denominator.
- Cancel crosswise before multiplying; keep the numbers small.
- "$\tfrac{2}{3}$ of $x$" = $\tfrac{2}{3} \cdot x$.
- In division, flip only the **second** fraction.
- Bring the result to its simplest form and check roughly: $\tfrac{1}{2} + \tfrac{1}{3}$ must be more than $\tfrac{1}{2}$.
