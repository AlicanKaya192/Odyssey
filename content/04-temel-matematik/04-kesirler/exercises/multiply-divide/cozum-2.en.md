**Idea:** Write the whole expression as a single product: flip the division and gather everything above and below one fraction bar. Then cancel the common factors in one go.

**Step 1 — One product.**

$$
\frac{3}{4} \cdot \frac{8}{9} \cdot \frac{2}{5} = \frac{3 \cdot 8 \cdot 2}{4 \cdot 9 \cdot 5}
$$

**Step 2 — Factorise into primes.**

$$
\frac{3 \cdot 2^3 \cdot 2}{2^2 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5} = \frac{3 \cdot 2^4}{2^2 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5}
$$

**Step 3 — Cancel.** One $3$ and two $2$s go from top and bottom:

$$
\frac{2^2}{3 \cdot 5} = \frac{4}{15}
$$

**Why the same result?** In multiplication, order and grouping do not matter; the brackets only showed which fraction the division applied to. After flipping the division everything is a single product, and we can cancel in any order we like.

**Answer:** $\dfrac{4}{15}$.
