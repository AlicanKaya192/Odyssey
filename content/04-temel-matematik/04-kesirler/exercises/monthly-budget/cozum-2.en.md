**Idea:** Go backwards from the final state: at each step ask "what fraction of the previous amount was this?" and divide by that fraction to get the previous amount.

**Step 1 — Before food.** The $1\,500$ left after food is $\frac{3}{4}$ of the money before food:

$$
1\,500 \div \frac{3}{4} = 1\,500 \cdot \frac{4}{3} = 2\,000
$$

**Step 2 — Before rent.** $2\,000$ is $\frac{2}{3}$ of the income:

$$
2\,000 \div \frac{2}{3} = 2\,000 \cdot \frac{3}{2} = 3\,000
$$

**Reading the result:** Along the way the money went $3\,000 \to 2\,000$, then $2\,000 \to 1\,500$. Going backwards we **divide** by the remaining fraction at each step, because going forwards we **multiplied** by it.

**Why the same result?** Combining the two factors ($\frac{2}{3}$ and $\frac{3}{4}$) into a single fraction ($\frac{1}{2}$) and dividing once is the same as dividing by them in turn: $\frac{4}{3} \cdot \frac{3}{2} = 2$.

**Answer:** $3\,000$.
