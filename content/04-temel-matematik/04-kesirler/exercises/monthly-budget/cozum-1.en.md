**What is asked?** The whole amount of money, given what a fraction of it is.

**Idea:** "$\frac{1}{4}$ of what is left" is a part of what is left, not of the whole. After each expense, write the remaining share as a fraction of the whole; at the end, the remaining fraction equals $1\,500$.

**Step 1 — After rent.** $\frac{1}{3}$ of the income goes, $1 - \frac{1}{3} = \frac{2}{3}$ is left.

**Step 2 — After food.** $\frac{1}{4}$ of what is left goes, $\frac{3}{4}$ of it stays. As a fraction of the whole:

$$
\frac{3}{4} \cdot \frac{2}{3} = \frac{6}{12} = \frac{1}{2}
$$

Half the income is left.

**Step 3 — Find the whole.** If $\frac{1}{2}$ of the income is $1\,500$, the income is:

$$
1\,500 \div \frac{1}{2} = 1\,500 \cdot 2 = 3\,000
$$

**Check:** Rent is $\frac{1}{3}$ of $3\,000$ $= 1\,000$; $2\,000$ left. Food is $\frac{1}{4}$ of $2\,000$ $= 500$; $1\,500$ left ✓.

**Watch out:** Thinking $\frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{4} = \frac{7}{12}$ has been spent is wrong; the $\frac{1}{4}$ is a quarter of what is left, not of the income.

**Answer:** $3\,000$.
