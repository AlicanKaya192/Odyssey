**What is asked?** The sum and difference of three fractions with different denominators.

**Idea:** Fractions with different denominators cannot be added directly because their parts have different sizes. We convert them all to parts of the same size (a common denominator). The least common denominator is the LCM of the denominators.

**Step 1 — The common denominator.** $3$, $4 = 2^2$, $6 = 2 \cdot 3$; the larger exponents give $2^2 \cdot 3 = 12$.

**Step 2 — Expand.** Multiply the top and bottom of each fraction by the number that makes the denominator $12$:

$$
\frac{2}{3} = \frac{8}{12}, \qquad \frac{3}{4} = \frac{9}{12}, \qquad \frac{5}{6} = \frac{10}{12}
$$

**Step 3 — Work on the numerators.** The denominator is the same; left to right:

$$
\frac{8 + 9 - 10}{12} = \frac{7}{12}
$$

**Step 4 — Does it simplify?** $7$ is prime and does not divide $12$; $\frac{7}{12}$ is in simplest form.

**Check:** Roughly: $\frac{2}{3} \approx 0.67$, $\frac{3}{4} = 0.75$, $\frac{5}{6} \approx 0.83$; $0.67 + 0.75 - 0.83 \approx 0.59$. $\frac{7}{12} \approx 0.58$ ✓.

**Answer:** $\dfrac{7}{12}$.
