A worked example for each method in the lesson. Try the question yourself first, then read the solution.

## 1. A set written with a rule

**Question:** What are the elements of $\{x \mid x \text{ prime and } x < 12\}$?

$\{2, 3, 5, 7, 11\}$; $5$ elements.

## 2. Operations

**Question:** For $A = \{a, b, c, d\}$ and $B = \{c, d, e\}$, find $A \cup B$, $A \cap B$, $B \setminus A$.

$A \cup B = \{a, b, c, d, e\}$, $A \cap B = \{c, d\}$, $B \setminus A = \{e\}$.

## 3. Number of subsets

**Question:** How many subsets does $\{1, 2, 3\}$ have?

$2^3 = 8$: $\emptyset$, $\{1\}$, $\{2\}$, $\{3\}$, $\{1, 2\}$, $\{1, 3\}$, $\{2, 3\}$, $\{1, 2, 3\}$.

## 4. The size of a union

**Question:** In a class, $22$ play football, $15$ play basketball and $9$ play both. How many play at least one?

$22 + 15 - 9 = 28$.

## 5. Filling a Venn diagram

**Question:** In a group of $40$, $24$ drink tea and $20$ coffee; $6$ drink neither. How many drink both?

At least one: $40 - 6 = 34$. $24 + 20 - x = 34$, $x = 10$.

## 6. Truth values

**Question:** $p$: "$5$ is even" (false), $q$: "$5 > 3$" (true). What are $p \vee q$, $p \wedge q$, $p \Rightarrow q$?

$p \vee q$ true, $p \wedge q$ false, $p \Rightarrow q$ true (the premise is false).

## 7. De Morgan

**Question:** What is the negation of "every day I both read and walk"?

"Some days I either do not read or do not walk." (The negation of "and" is "or".)

## 8. Converse and contrapositive

**Question:** "If a number is divisible by $10$, it is divisible by $5$." Are its converse and contrapositive true?

The converse ("if divisible by $5$, then by $10$") is false: $15$. The contrapositive ("if not divisible by $5$, then not by $10$") is true.
