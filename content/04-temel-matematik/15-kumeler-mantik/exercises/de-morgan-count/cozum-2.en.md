**Idea:** Find the four regions of the Venn diagram; read which regions make up the sets asked for, and add.

**Step 1 — The regions.** Intersection $10$; $A$ only: $20 - 10 = 10$; $B$ only: $25 - 10 = 15$; outside: $50 - 35 = 15$.

**Step 2 — $A' \cap B'$.** Outside both $A$ and $B$: only the outside region, $15$.

**Step 3 — $A' \cup B'$.** Outside $A$ or outside $B$: the three regions other than the intersection, $10 + 15 + 15 = 40$.

**Why the same result?** De Morgan's laws are a short way of writing this grouping of regions. Knowing the four regions is enough to answer every counting question about two sets.

**Answer:** $15$ and $40$.
