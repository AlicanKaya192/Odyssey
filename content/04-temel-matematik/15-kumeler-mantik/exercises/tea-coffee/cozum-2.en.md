**Idea:** Fill the four regions of the Venn diagram in order: the intersection first, then the "only" regions, and the outside last.

**Step 1 — The intersection.** $8$.

**Step 2 — Tea only.** $25 - 8 = 17$.

**Step 3 — Coffee only.** $18 - 8 = 10$.

**Step 4 — At least one.** $17 + 8 + 10 = 35$.

**Step 5 — The outside.** $40 - 35 = 5$.

**Why the same result?** The "$-8$" in the union formula is the algebraic way of counting the intersection once in the Venn diagram. Filling the diagram shows each region separately, so mistakes are easier to spot.

**Answer:** $35$ and $5$.
