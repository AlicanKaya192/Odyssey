**What is asked?** The size of the union of two groups, and of its complement.

**Idea:** Adding the tea drinkers and the coffee drinkers counts those who drink both twice; we subtract them once. Those who drink neither are what is left of the whole office after removing those who drink at least one.

**Step 1 — The union.**

$$
n(T \cup C) = 25 + 18 - 8 = 35
$$

**Step 2 — The complement.**

$$
40 - 35 = 5
$$

**Check:** Regions: tea only $25 - 8 = 17$, coffee only $18 - 8 = 10$, both $8$, neither $5$. Total $17 + 10 + 8 + 5 = 40$ ✓.

**Watch out:** $25 + 18 = 43$ is more than $40$: forgetting to subtract the common ones makes the total exceed even the number of people in the office.

**Answer:** $35$ drink at least one; $5$ drink neither.
