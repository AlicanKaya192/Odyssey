A model classifies $200$ photos as "cat" or "dog". $80$ of the photos really are cats and $120$ dogs. The model says "cat" for $70$ of the real cats and $20$ of the real dogs, and "dog" for the rest.

1. What is the probability that the model says "cat" for a randomly chosen photo?
2. What is the probability that the model predicts correctly (the accuracy)?
3. If the model said "cat", what is the probability that the photo really is a cat?
