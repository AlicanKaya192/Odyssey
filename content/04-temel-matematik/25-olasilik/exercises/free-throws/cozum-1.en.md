**What is asked?** All, none and at least one in three independent tries.

**Idea:** The probability that independent events happen together is the product; use the complement for "at least one".

**Step 1 — All three.** $0.7^3 = 0.343$.

**Step 2 — None.** $0.3^3 = 0.027$.

**Step 3 — At least one.** $1 - 0.027 = 0.973$.

**Check:** Exactly $1$ make: $3 \cdot 0.7 \cdot 0.3^2 = 0.189$; exactly $2$: $3 \cdot 0.7^2 \cdot 0.3 = 0.441$. The four cases: $0.027 + 0.189 + 0.441 + 0.343 = 1$ ✓.

**Watch out:** Adding "at least one" as $0.7 + 0.7 + 0.7 = 2.1$ pushes the probability above $1$: the events are not disjoint, they can happen together.

**Answer:** $0.343$, $0.027$, $0.973$.
