**What is asked?** The polynomial's value at two points and the sum of its coefficients.

**Idea:** $P(a)$ is the number you get by writing $a$ in place of $x$. The sum of the coefficients is $P(1)$, because every power of $1$ is $1$.

**Step 1 — $P(2)$.** Powers first: $2^3 = 8$, $2^2 = 4$.

$$
P(2) = 2 \cdot 8 - 4 + 4 \cdot 2 - 7 = 16 - 4 + 8 - 7 = 13
$$

**Step 2 — $P(-1)$.** $(-1)^3 = -1$, $(-1)^2 = 1$.

$$
P(-1) = 2 \cdot (-1) - 1 + 4 \cdot (-1) - 7 = -2 - 1 - 4 - 7 = -14
$$

**Step 3 — The sum of the coefficients.** $P(1) = 2 - 1 + 4 - 7 = -2$.

**Check:** The coefficients are $2, -1, 4, -7$; adding them by hand also gives $-2$ ✓.

**Watch out:** The term $-x^2$ is $-1$ at $x = -1$, not $+1$: first the square ($1$), then the minus in front of it.

**Answer:** $P(2) = 13$, $P(-1) = -14$, sum $-2$.
