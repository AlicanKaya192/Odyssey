A polynomial regression model of degree three

$$
\hat{y} = w_0 + w_1 x + w_2 x^2 + w_3 x^3
$$

has been trained with weights $w_0 = 1$, $w_1 = 2$, $w_2 = -1$, $w_3 = 0.5$.

1. What is the prediction $\hat{y}$ for $x = 2$?
2. What is the prediction for $x = -2$?
3. If the example with $x = 2$ has true value $y = 6$, what is the error $y - \hat{y}$?
