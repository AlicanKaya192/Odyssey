**What is asked?** Two coefficients of the product and the sum of its coefficients.

**Idea:** Every term is multiplied by every term, then like terms are added.

**Step 1 — Multiply by $3x$.** $3x \cdot x^2 = 3x^3$, $3x \cdot (-2x) = -6x^2$, $3x \cdot 5 = 15x$.

**Step 2 — Multiply by $1$.** $x^2 - 2x + 5$.

**Step 3 — Add.**

$$
\begin{aligned}
&3x^3 - 6x^2 + 15x + x^2 - 2x + 5 \\
&= 3x^3 - 5x^2 + 13x + 5
\end{aligned}
$$

**Step 4 — The sum of the coefficients.** $3 - 5 + 13 + 5 = 16$.

**Check:** For $x = 1$ the original form is $(3 + 1)(1 - 2 + 5) = 4 \cdot 4 = 16$ ✓.

**Watch out:** In $3x \cdot (-2x)$ the powers add up and the sign stays: $-6x^2$.

**Answer:** coefficient of $x^2$ is $-5$, of $x$ is $13$, sum $16$.
