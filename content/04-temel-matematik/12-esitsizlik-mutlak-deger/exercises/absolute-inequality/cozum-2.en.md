**Idea:** $|A| \le r$ means "$A \le r$ **and** $A \ge -r$". Solve both and intersect.

**Step 1 — The first condition.** $x - 4 \le 2.5 \Rightarrow x \le 6.5$.

**Step 2 — The second condition.** $x - 4 \ge -2.5 \Rightarrow x \ge 1.5$.

**Step 3 — The intersection.** $1.5 \le x \le 6.5$. The whole numbers in it run from $2$, the first above $1.5$, to $6$, the last below $6.5$: $6 - 2 + 1 = 5$.

**Why the same result?** If a number's absolute value does not exceed $2.5$, the number is both below $2.5$ and above $-2.5$; the double inequality writes these two conditions on one line.

**Answer:** $1.5$, $6.5$ and $5$.
