**What is asked?** The numbers whose distance from a point does not exceed a given bound.

**Idea:** $|x - 4| \le 2.5$ means "the distance from $x$ to $4$ is at most $2.5$". On the number line it is a region of $2.5$ units on each side of $4$.

**Step 1 — Turn it into a double inequality.**

$$
-2.5 \le x - 4 \le 2.5
$$

**Step 2 — Add $4$.**

$$
1.5 \le x \le 6.5
$$

$a = 1.5$, $b = 6.5$.

**Step 3 — Whole numbers.** $2, 3, 4, 5, 6$: $5$ of them.

**Check:** $x = 2$: $|2 - 4| = 2 \le 2.5$ ✓. $x = 1$: $|1 - 4| = 3 \le 2.5$ is false ✓. $x = 7$: $3 \le 2.5$ is false ✓.

**Watch out:** Solving $|x - 4| \le 2.5$ one-sidedly as $x \le 6.5$ loses the lower bound; absolute value limits both sides at once.

**Answer:** $a = 1.5$, $b = 6.5$; $5$ whole numbers.
