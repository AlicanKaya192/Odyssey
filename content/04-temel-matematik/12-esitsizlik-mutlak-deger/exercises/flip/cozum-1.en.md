**What is asked?** The solution set of an inequality and the largest whole number satisfying it.

**Idea:** Solve it like an equation; only flip the inequality when dividing by a negative number. Then check whether the boundary is included.

**Step 1 — Move the number across.** Subtract $5$ from both sides:

$$
-2x > 6
$$

**Step 2 — Divide by the negative, flip.**

$$
x < -3
$$

**Step 3 — Boundary and largest whole number.** The boundary is $-3$, but because of $<$ it is not included. The largest whole number below $-3$ is $-4$.

**Check:** $x = -4$: $5 + 8 = 13 > 11$ ✓. $x = -3$: $5 + 6 = 11 > 11$ is false ✓ (boundary excluded). $x = 0$: $5 > 11$ is false ✓.

**Watch out:** Forgetting to flip and writing $x > -3$ is the most common slip; then numbers like $x = 0$ seem to be solutions but do not work.

**Answer:** Boundary $-3$; largest whole number $-4$.
