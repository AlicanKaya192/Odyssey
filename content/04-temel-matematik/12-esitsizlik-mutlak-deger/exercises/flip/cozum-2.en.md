**Idea:** To avoid dividing by a negative, collect the unknown on the side where its coefficient stays positive.

**Step 1 — Move $2x$ right and $11$ left.** Add $2x$ to both sides and subtract $11$ from both:

$$
5 - 11 > 2x \quad\Rightarrow\quad -6 > 2x
$$

**Step 2 — Divide by a positive number.** Dividing by $2$ does not flip it:

$$
-3 > x
$$

That is, $x < -3$.

**Step 3 — The largest whole number.** $-3$ is not included: $-4$.

**Why the same result?** $-3 > x$ and $x < -3$ are the same sentence read from the other end. The rule "dividing by a negative flips it" is a shortcut for doing this swap in one step.

**Answer:** $-3$ and $-4$.
