**What is asked?** For which $x$ the expression inside takes an absolute value of $7$.

**Idea:** Two numbers have absolute value $7$: $7$ and $-7$. So the inside is either $7$ or $-7$; that gives two linear equations.

**Step 1 — The first case.**

$$
\begin{aligned}
&2x - 3 = 7 \\
\Rightarrow\; &2x = 10 \\
\Rightarrow\; &x = 5
\end{aligned}
$$

**Step 2 — The second case.**

$$
\begin{aligned}
&2x - 3 = -7 \\
\Rightarrow\; &2x = -4 \\
\Rightarrow\; &x = -2
\end{aligned}
$$

**Check:** $x = 5$: $|10 - 3| = 7$ ✓. $x = -2$: $\lvert -4 - 3 \rvert = \lvert -7 \rvert = 7$ ✓.

**Watch out:** Solving only $2x - 3 = 7$ and stopping at $x = 5$ forgets that absolute value works in two directions. Most absolute-value equations have two solutions.

**Answer:** $5$ and $-2$.
