**Idea:** $|2x - 3| = 2\left|x - \tfrac{3}{2}\right|$. The equation says "twice the distance from $x$ to $\tfrac{3}{2}$ is $7$", that is, "$x$ is $3.5$ units from $1.5$".

**Step 1 — Take the $2$ out.**

$$
|2x - 3| = \left|2\left(x - \tfrac{3}{2}\right)\right| = 2\left|x - \tfrac{3}{2}\right| = 7
$$

**Step 2 — The distance.** $\left|x - 1.5\right| = 3.5$.

**Step 3 — Two directions.** $1.5 + 3.5 = 5$ and $1.5 - 3.5 = -2$.

**Why the same result?** The two cases of an absolute-value equation are going right and left of the centre on the number line. The centre $1.5$ is the midpoint of the two solutions: $\frac{5 + (-2)}{2} = 1.5$ ✓.

**Answer:** $5$ and $-2$.
