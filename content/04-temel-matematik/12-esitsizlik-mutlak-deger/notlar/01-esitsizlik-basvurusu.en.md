## Notation

| Inequality | Interval | Number line |
|---|---|---|
| $x > a$ | $(a, \infty)$ | open circle at $a$, to the right |
| $x \ge a$ | $[a, \infty)$ | filled circle at $a$, to the right |
| $x < b$ | $(-\infty, b)$ | open circle at $b$, to the left |
| $a \le x < b$ | $[a, b)$ | $a$ filled, $b$ open |

## Rules

| Operation | Direction of the inequality |
|---|---|
| adding or subtracting a number on both sides | stays |
| multiplying or dividing by a positive number | stays |
| multiplying or dividing by a **negative** number | **flips** |

## Absolute value

| Expression | Solution |
|---|---|
| $\lvert A \rvert = r$, $r \ge 0$ | $A = r$ or $A = -r$ |
| $\lvert A \rvert = r$, $r < 0$ | no solution |
| $\lvert x - a \rvert \le r$ | $a - r \le x \le a + r$ |
| $\lvert x - a \rvert \ge r$ | $x \le a - r$ or $x \ge a + r$ |
| $\lvert A \rvert < r$, $r \le 0$ | no solution |

"Centre and radius": in $|x - a| \le r$ the centre is $a$ and the radius
$r$. The other way round: the interval $3 \le x \le 11$ has centre $7$ and
radius $4$, so it is $|x - 7| \le 4$.

## Practical tips

- If you do not want to divide by a negative, collect the unknown on the side where its coefficient stays positive.
- Test the answer: try a number on each side of the boundary.
- In a double inequality, do the same to all three parts.
- If "or" gives two pieces, do not merge them into one line.
