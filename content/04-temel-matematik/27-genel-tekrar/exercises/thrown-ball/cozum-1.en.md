**What is asked?** The maximum of a quadratic function and the solutions of two different equations.

**Idea:** Since $a < 0$ the parabola opens downwards, so its vertex is the highest point. A given height is a quadratic equation.

**Step 1 — The vertex.** $t = -\frac{20}{2 \cdot (-5)} = 2$. $h(2) = -20 + 40 + 25 = 45$.

**Step 2 — Hitting the ground.** $-5t^2 + 20t + 25 = 0$; divide by $-5$: $t^2 - 4t - 5 = 0$, $(t - 5)(t + 1) = 0$. $t = -1$ is before the throw, meaningless; $t = 5$.

**Step 3 — $40$ m.** $-5t^2 + 20t + 25 = 40$, so $t^2 - 4t + 3 = 0$, $(t - 1)(t - 3) = 0$. On the way up $t = 1$, on the way down $t = 3$.

**Check:** The parabola is symmetric about the vertex: $1$ and $3$ are equally far on either side of the vertex time $2$ ✓.

**Watch out:** Taking the negative root ($t = -1$) as an answer; time starts at the throw.

**Answer:** $45$ m, $5$ s, $3$ s.
