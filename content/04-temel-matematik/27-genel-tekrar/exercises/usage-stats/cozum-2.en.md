**Idea:** Variance $= \overline{x^2} - \bar{x}^2$; no deviations needed.

**Step 1 — The mean.** $20$.

**Step 2 — The squares.** $144 + 225 + 324 + 400 + 1225 = 2318$; their mean is $463.6$. Variance $463.6 - 400 = 63.6$; $\sigma \approx 7.975$.

**Step 3 — The z-score.** $\frac{15}{7.975} \approx 1.881$.

**Why the same result?** Expanding $\frac{1}{n} \sum (x_i - \bar{x})^2$ gives $\overline{x^2} - \bar{x}^2$; both ways reach the same number.

**Answer:** $20$, $7.975$ and $1.881$.
