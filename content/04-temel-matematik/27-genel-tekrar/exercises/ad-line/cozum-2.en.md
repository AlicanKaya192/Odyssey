**Idea:** In a linear relationship every equal step brings the same increase; you can walk without an equation.

**Step 1 — The step.** $2000$ extra spending brings $200$ extra users: $0.1$ per unit.

**Step 2 — Walk forward.** From $3000$ to $5000$ is one more step of $2000$: $350 + 200 = 550$.

**Step 3 — Error.** Actual $520$, prediction $550$; the difference is $30$ and its square $900$.

**Why the same result?** Equal increases over equal steps is the definition of slope; the equation $y = 0.1x + 50$ is this walk in short.

**Answer:** $0.1$, $550$ and $900$.
