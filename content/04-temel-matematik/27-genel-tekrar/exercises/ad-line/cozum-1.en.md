**What is asked?** A line through two points, a prediction with it, and the squared error of the prediction.

**Idea:** Two points give the slope; the slope and one point give the line; the error is actual minus prediction.

**Step 1 — Slope.** $m = \frac{200}{2000} = 0.1$.

**Step 2 — Line and prediction.** $150 = 0.1 \cdot 1000 + b$, $b = 50$. $\hat{y} = 0.1 \cdot 5000 + 50 = 550$.

**Step 3 — Squared error.** $(520 - 550)^2 = (-30)^2 = 900$.

**Check:** The other point: $0.1 \cdot 3000 + 50 = 350$ ✓.

**Watch out:** Writing the slope as $\frac{x}{y}$ ($10$) means "$10$ per user"; the right reading is "$0.1$ users per unit of money".

**Answer:** $0.1$, $550$, $900$.
