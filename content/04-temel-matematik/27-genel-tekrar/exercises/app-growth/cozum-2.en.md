**Idea:** Without logarithms, step forward multiplying by $1.1$ each month.

**Step 1 — The first six months.** $2200$, $2420$, $2662$, $2928.2$, $3221.02$, $3543.12$.

**Step 2 — Doubling.** $\approx 3897.43$ at the end of month $7$ and $\approx 4287.18$ at the end of month $8$: doubling ($4000$) happens between the seventh and eighth months. The exact value needs a logarithm: $7.27$.

**Step 3 — Ten thousand.** Keep multiplying: month $16$ $\approx 9189.95$, month $17$ $\approx 10{,}108.94$.

**Why the same result?** Each row of the table is one multiplication by $1.1$; $t$ rows give $1.1^t$. The logarithm answers "how many rows are needed" without writing them out.

**Answer:** $3543.12$, $7.27$ and $17$.
