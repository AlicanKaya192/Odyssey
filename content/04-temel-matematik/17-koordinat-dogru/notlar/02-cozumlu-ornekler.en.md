A worked example for each method in the lesson. Try the question yourself first, then read the solution.

## 1. Finding the quadrant

**Question:** Which quadrants are $(-4, 7)$ and $(5, -1)$ in?

$(-4, 7)$: $x$ negative, $y$ positive, quadrant II. $(5, -1)$: $x$ positive, $y$ negative, quadrant IV.

## 2. Distance

**Question:** What is the distance between $A(-1, 2)$ and $B(5, 10)$?

$\Delta x = 6$, $\Delta y = 8$: $d = \sqrt{36 + 64} = \sqrt{100} = 10$.

## 3. The midpoint

**Question:** What is the midpoint of $(3, -2)$ and $(7, 6)$?

$\left( \frac{3 + 7}{2}, \frac{-2 + 6}{2} \right) = (5, 2)$.

## 4. Slope

**Question:** What is the slope of the line through $(2, 7)$ and $(6, -1)$?

$m = \dfrac{-1 - 7}{6 - 2} = \dfrac{-8}{4} = -2$. The line falls to the right.

## 5. A line through two points

**Question:** What is the equation of the line through $(1, 4)$ and $(3, 10)$?

$m = \frac{10 - 4}{3 - 1} = 3$. $y - 4 = 3(x - 1)$, that is, $y = 3x + 1$. Check: $3 \cdot 3 + 1 = 10$ ✓.

## 6. Slope from the general form

**Question:** What are the slope and the $y$-intercept of the line $4x - 2y + 6 = 0$?

$2y = 4x + 6$, $y = 2x + 3$: slope $2$, $y$-intercept $3$.

## 7. A perpendicular line

**Question:** Which line is perpendicular to $y = \frac{2}{3}x - 1$ and passes through $(2, 1)$?

The perpendicular slope is $-\frac{3}{2}$. $y - 1 = -\frac{3}{2}(x - 2)$, that is, $y = -\frac{3}{2}x + 4$.

## 8. The intersection

**Question:** Where do $y = x + 3$ and $y = -2x + 9$ meet?

$x + 3 = -2x + 9$, $3x = 6$, $x = 2$; $y = 5$. They meet at $(2, 5)$.
