## Points and quadrants

| Quadrant | $x$ | $y$ |
|---|---|---|
| I | $+$ | $+$ |
| II | $-$ | $+$ |
| III | $-$ | $-$ |
| IV | $+$ | $-$ |

Points on an axis belong to no quadrant.

## Formulas

| What | Formula |
|---|---|
| distance | $d = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2}$ |
| midpoint | $\left( \dfrac{x_1 + x_2}{2}, \dfrac{y_1 + y_2}{2} \right)$ |
| slope | $m = \dfrac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}$ |

## The equation of a line

| Form | Equation | When |
|---|---|---|
| slope–intercept | $y = mx + b$ | slope and $y$-intercept known |
| point–slope | $y - y_1 = m(x - x_1)$ | a point and the slope known |
| general | $ax + by + c = 0$ | axis crossings and decision boundaries |
| horizontal | $y = k$ | slope $0$ |
| vertical | $x = k$ | slope undefined |

## Two lines

| Situation | Slopes |
|---|---|
| parallel | $m_1 = m_2$, $b_1 \neq b_2$ |
| the same line | $m_1 = m_2$, $b_1 = b_2$ |
| perpendicular | $m_1 \cdot m_2 = -1$ |
| crossing | $m_1 \neq m_2$: one common point |

## Practical tips

- In the slope the vertical change goes on top; both differences take the points in the same order.
- A line through two points: the slope first, then the point–slope form.
- Perpendicular slope: flip it and change the sign.
- Axis crossings: put $x = 0$ to find $y$, put $y = 0$ to find $x$.
- The triples $3$-$4$-$5$, $5$-$12$-$13$ and $6$-$8$-$10$ speed up distance work.
