**Idea:** Every line perpendicular to $ax + by + c = 0$ has the form $bx - ay + k = 0$: swap the coefficients and flip the sign of one. The only unknown left, $k$, comes from the point.

**Step 1 — The form of the perpendicular line.** $a = 2$, $b = -3$: $-3x - 2y + k = 0$. Multiply everything by $-1$: $3x + 2y - k = 0$.

**Step 2 — Put in the point.** $(4, 1)$: $12 + 2 - k = 0$, $k = 14$. The line is $3x + 2y - 14 = 0$.

**Step 3 — Get $y$ on its own.** $2y = -3x + 14$, $y = -\frac{3}{2}x + 7$.

**Why the same result?** The slope of $ax + by + c = 0$ is $-\frac{a}{b}$, and the slope of $bx - ay + k = 0$ is $\frac{b}{a}$. Their product is $-\frac{a}{b} \cdot \frac{b}{a} = -1$; so this pattern is the "flip it, change the sign" rule in general form.

**Answer:** $-\frac{3}{2}$ and $7$.
