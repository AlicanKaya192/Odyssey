**What is asked?** The distance between two points and the point halfway between them.

**Idea:** The distance is the hypotenuse of the right triangle formed by the horizontal and vertical differences. The midpoint is the average of each coordinate separately.

**Step 1 — The differences.** $\Delta x = 5 - (-3) = 8$, $\Delta y = 7 - 1 = 6$.

**Step 2 — The distance.**

$$
d = \sqrt{8^2 + 6^2} = \sqrt{64 + 36} = \sqrt{100} = 10
$$

**Step 3 — The midpoint.**

$$
M = \left( \frac{-3 + 5}{2}, \ \frac{1 + 7}{2} \right) = (1, 4)
$$

**Check:** $M$ must be the same distance from both points. $AM$: $\sqrt{4^2 + 3^2} = 5$, $MB$: $\sqrt{4^2 + 3^2} = 5$. Together they make $10$ ✓.

**Watch out:** Counting $5 - (-3)$ as $2$ is the most common mistake; minus a minus is a plus, so the difference is $8$.

**Answer:** $AB = 10$, midpoint $(1, 4)$.
