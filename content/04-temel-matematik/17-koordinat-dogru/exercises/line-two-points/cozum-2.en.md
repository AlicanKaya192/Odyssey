**Idea:** The line has the form $y = mx + b$, with two unknowns $m$ and $b$. Each point gives one equation; two points give a system.

**Step 1 — Two equations.**

$$
\begin{aligned}
(-1, 5): &\quad 5 = -m + b \\
(3, -3): &\quad -3 = 3m + b
\end{aligned}
$$

**Step 2 — Elimination.** Subtract the first from the second: $-8 = 4m$, $m = -2$. Put it into the first: $5 = 2 + b$, $b = 3$.

**Step 3 — The $x$-intercept.** $-2x + 3 = 0$, $x = 1.5$.

**Why the same result?** Subtracting the two equations removes $b$ and leaves $-3 - 5 = m \cdot (3 - (-1))$; this is the slope formula with its denominator moved across. The two methods do the same calculation in a different order.

**Answer:** $-2$, $3$ and $1.5$.
