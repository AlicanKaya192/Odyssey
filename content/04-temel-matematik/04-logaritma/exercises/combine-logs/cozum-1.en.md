**1. Combine the added terms with the product rule:**

$$
\log_6 12 + \log_6 18 = \log_6 (12 \cdot 18) = \log_6 216
$$

**2. Combine the subtracted term with the quotient rule:**

$$
\log_6 216 - \log_6 6 = \log_6 \frac{216}{6} = \log_6 36
$$

**3. Find the value:** $6^2 = 36$, so $\log_6 36 = 2$.

**Answer: 2**
