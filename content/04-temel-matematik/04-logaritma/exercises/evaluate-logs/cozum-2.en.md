First write the numbers inside as powers of the base:

$$
81 = 3^4 \qquad \frac{1}{8} = 2^{-3}
$$

Then apply the rule $\log_b (b^k) = k$ (a logarithm and an exponent in the same base cancel):

$$
\log_3 3^4 + \log_2 2^{-3} = 4 + (-3) = 1
$$

Whenever you can write the number as a power, this is the quickest path.
