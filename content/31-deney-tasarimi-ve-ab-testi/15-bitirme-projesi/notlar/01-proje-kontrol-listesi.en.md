Analyse an experiment in the same order every time. Next to each line is
the section where it was covered.

## Before the test

- The question in one sentence: "Does X raise Y?" (The Experiment
  Question and Choosing a Metric)
- One primary metric, a practical threshold, guardrail metrics (the same
  section)
- The unit of assignment and the salt; is there a risk of interference?
  (Random Assignment; Network Effects and Interference)
- Sample size and duration, at least two full weeks (Sample Size)
- The looking rule: only the last day, or sequential thresholds written
  in advance (The Peeking Trap and Sequential Testing)
- The covariate for CUPED, the list of segments (Variance Reduction;
  Segments)

## When the test ends

- SRM and balance (A/A Tests and Sample Ratio Mismatch)
- Primary metric: rates, difference, interval, p (Analysing Your First
  A/B Test)
- The delta method if it is a ratio metric (Ratio Metrics and the Delta
  Method)
- Secondary metrics, corrected if needed (Multiple Comparisons)
- Guardrail metrics: any breakage
- Does the effect change over time? (Novelty Effects and Long-Term
  Impact)
- Segments written in advance (Segments and Simpson's Paradox)

## The decision

- Which of the four outcomes: launch / harmful / inconclusive / not
  worth it (Deciding and Reporting)
- A one-page report, with its limits
- A holdout if launched
