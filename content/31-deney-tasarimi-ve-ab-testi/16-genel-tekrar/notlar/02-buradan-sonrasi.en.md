## If you want to go deeper

- **Sequential testing itself:** computing O'Brien–Fleming boundaries and
  always-valid p-values.
- **Analysis by regression:** the general form of CUPED; several
  covariates, and segment effects with interaction terms.
- **Heterogeneous effects:** methods that estimate who the effect differs
  for without falling into segment hunting (such as causal trees).
- **Causal inference:** estimating effects from observational data when an
  experiment is not possible (matching, difference in differences,
  instrumental variables).
- **Bayesian experiments:** hierarchical models, analysing many tests
  together.

## Related paths in Odyssey

- **MLOps:** canary releases and holdouts are the live counterpart of this
  module's ideas.
- **Machine Learning:** comparing two models is an experiment too; the same
  traps (multiple comparisons, leakage) exist there.
- **Time Series:** in time-dependent metrics the observations are not
  independent; designs such as the switchback build on that.
