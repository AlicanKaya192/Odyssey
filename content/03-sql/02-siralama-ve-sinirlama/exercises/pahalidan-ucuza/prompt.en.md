Sort the eight products in `products` from the **highest price to the
lowest**. The result should have the `name` and `price` columns.

```
name     price  
-------  -------
Laptop   24500.0
Desktop  18900.0
...
```

In this exercise the **order of the rows is checked too** — it did not
matter in the previous section, here it is the whole point.

Remember: without `ORDER BY` the server guarantees no order at all.
