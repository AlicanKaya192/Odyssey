This module taught you to **read the result** of an A/B test. The next
module, Experiment Design and A/B Testing, covers **setting the test up
right** and the traps of real life.

## Next in this track

In the Experiment Design and A/B Testing module:

- the experiment question and choosing a metric, random assignment, sample
  size;
- A/A tests and sample ratio mismatch (SRM);
- the bootstrap and the permutation test: resampling instead of a formula;
- the peeking trap and sequential testing, multiple comparisons;
- variance reduction (CUPED), ratio metrics and the delta method;
- segments and Simpson's paradox, novelty effects, network effects;
- multi-armed bandits, deciding and reporting, a capstone project.
