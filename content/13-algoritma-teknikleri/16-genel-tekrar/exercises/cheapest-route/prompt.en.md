Write the function `cheapest(n, roads, start, goal)` with **Dijkstra**: cities
`0`..`n − 1`, `roads` are **directed** `[a, b, cost]` roads (no negative
costs). It returns the lowest cost from `start` to `goal`, or `-1` if
unreachable.

`(cost, city)` in the heap; skip a stale entry popped from the heap.

**Expected output:**

```
4
-1
```
