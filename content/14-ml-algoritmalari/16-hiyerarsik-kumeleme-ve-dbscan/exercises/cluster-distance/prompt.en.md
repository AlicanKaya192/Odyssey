between two sets of points; `"single"` the smallest, `"complete"` the largest,
`"average"` the mean of all pairs. Return `round(..., 3)`. The matrix `D` is
given: `D[i, j]` = between point `i` of `A` and point `j` of `B`.

**Expected output:**

```
single 3.0
complete 4.123
average 3.571
```
