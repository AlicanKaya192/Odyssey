You will build your first NumPy array and make it introduce itself.

**What you need to do:**

1. Import NumPy as `np`.
2. Build an array from the list `[12, 7, 25, 3, 18]` and call it `values`.
3. Print, in order: the array's **shape**, its **type**, its **total** and
   its **mean**.

**Expected output:**

```
(5,)
int64
65
13.0
```

**Careful:** `shape` and `dtype` are attributes (no parentheses), `sum()` and
`mean()` are methods (with parentheses). This distinction trips people up at
first.
