You have the scores of five students. You are going to compute the average
— no libraries, plain Python.

**What you need to do:**

1. The `scores` list is ready in the starter code: `[72, 85, 90, 64, 78]`.
2. Compute the average and keep it in a variable called `average`.
3. Print the average with one digit after the decimal point.

**Expected output:**

```
77.8
```

This is the very thing you will later write as `data["score"].mean()`. First
you do it by hand.
