The names, cities and scores of five students sit in a table. You will ask
the questions you ask when you first open a dataset.

**What you need to do:**

1. The `data` table is ready in the starter code.
2. Print, in order: the table's **shape**, the **column names** (as a list),
   its **first two rows**, and the **mean** score (rounded to one place).

**Expected output:**

```
(5, 3)
['name', 'city', 'score']
    name    city  score
0    Ada  Ankara     82
1  Kerem   Izmir     74
80.6
```

These four calls are the first things you write when you have a table:
`shape` tells you how many records, `columns` what you are looking at, and
`head()` what the data resembles.
